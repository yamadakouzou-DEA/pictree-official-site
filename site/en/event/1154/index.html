Mission

The “Mission” is a special mission that occurs irregularly.
By capturing designated spots within a set time frame when a Mission appears, players can earn rewards.
In Numazu Season 2, Missions will be available at locations selected as part of “”NUMAZU 100 TREASURES.”

 

【Mission List】
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Ano Zenjyo and Daisenji Temple
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Ukishima Sunflower Land and Nanoha Land
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Koukokuji Castle Ruins
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Shoinji Temple and Zen Master Hakuin
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Akeno Kannondo
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Ebara Soroku
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Ashitaka Park
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Stream Path and Numazu Firefly Festival
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Ayutsubo Falls
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Kochoji Temple
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Kadoike
NUMAZU 100 TREASURES – Camphor of Okanomiya Sengen Shrine

 

【Mission Period】
February 22, 2025 (Sat) 9:00 AM – March 7, 2025 (Fri) 4:00 PM

 

【Rewards】
360 reward coins (equivalent to 30 yen) per mission (1 spot) Limited to the first 200 participants
Power line 200m×1 for all mission completers
