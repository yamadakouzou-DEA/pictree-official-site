Limited postcards created in collaboration with “Maebashi Witches”

【Limited Collaboration Postcards】

You can exchange the reward coins you earn in the game for limited postcards created in collaboration with “Maebashi Witches.”

To exchange for a collaboration postcard, please visit “Gallery Artsoup” in Maebashi City.

 

◼️Postcards Available

● Types

12 designs in total. Distributed at random.

 

● Reward coins required for exchange

2,400 reward coins per postcard

 

◼️How to Exchange Postcards

● Reception period

Saturday, November 1, 2025, 12:30 JST to Sunday, November 30, 2025, 18:30 JST

Closed on Mondays and Tuesdays

 

● Reception hours

12:30 JST to 18:30 JST daily

 

● Exchange location

GalleryArtsoup

 

371-0024

2-20-16 Omotecho, Maebashi-shi, Gunma

About a 5-minute walk from JR Maebashi Station North Exit

 

https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/viewer?mid=1jphuKlXFK0av4fvX6saoXpx8021RE_7f&femb=1&ll=36.38565903563327%2C139.0720521&z=17

 

● Exchange steps

①Tap the menu icon at the top right of the app

 

②Tap “Profile”

 

③Tap “Exchange”

 

④Show the “Goods Exchange” screen to staff

 

⑤Follow staff instructions for the operation

※Please be sure to operate the screen as instructed by staff.

 

⑥Receive your postcard

 

Gallery Artsoup also distributes official PicTrée safety gear such as armbands and neck straps (Investigation Card). Quantities are limited.

Please wear official PicTrée items and play with safety in mind when taking photos.
