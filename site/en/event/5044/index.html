“Eraberu Pay” (Selectable Pay)

■ Overview:

“Eraberu Pay” is a digital gift provided by Gifttee, Inc., which allows users to exchange “Gift Points” for their preferred products from a variety of smartphone payment service points. There is no need for membership registration or app download to use “Eraberu Pay” or “Gift Points,” and it is possible to select multiple payment service points within the held points.

 

■ Key Features:

Select later, Smartphone-based completion, Easy to use from small amounts
