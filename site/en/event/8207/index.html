Event2026-10-26

PicTrée × JR East Station Facility Maintenance Mission in Akita, Oga Line

◼️Event Period

Sunday, November 1, 2026 – Sunday, November 29, 2026 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️Shooting Hours

6:15–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️How to Participate

Open “Photo Missions” from the bottom left of the PicTrée TOP screen, select “Station Facility Maintenance Mission” from the list, and join by tapping the “Challenge” button for each mission.

Alternatively, open “Season Switch” from the top left of the PicTrée TOP screen and select “Regular Season 3” from the list to participate. The photo assets for the “PicTrée × JR East Station Facility Maintenance Mission ~in Akita, Oga Line~” will be displayed together with the photo assets for “Regular Season 3.”

◼️Event Area

8 stations on the Oga Line (Oga, Hadachi, Wakimoto, Funakoshi, Tenno, Futada, Kami-Futada, and Detohama Stations)

◼️Event Map

◼️Photo Targets

・Station building exterior

・Station building interior

・Poles

・Pole groups

・Crossing barriers

For each mission (each station), photograph all target angles to earn 600 reward coins (worth 50 yen).
