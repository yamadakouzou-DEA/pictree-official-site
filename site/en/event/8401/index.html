Group Battle: Okinawa Photo Hunt 2026-2027

As a special feature of “Okinawa-wide Utility Pole Photo Hunt 2026-2027,” we will hold Okinawa’s first group battle event, “Group Battle: Okinawa Photo Hunt 2026-2027,” during the season.

This is a team-based ranking event in which groups of up to 5 players compete on the “Group Points” they earn during the season.

Reward Coins worth a total of ¥500,000 will be given to the top 50 groups.

Group creation and joining will be available starting Thursday, November 12, 2026. Form a group with your family, friends, or coworkers and aim for ¥100,000-equivalent Reward Coins as the winning group (split within the group)!

[Event Period]

Saturday, December 12, 2026, 7:00 – Sunday, March 14, 2027, 18:30 (JST / UTC+9)

[Group Creation & Joining Period]

Thursday, November 12, 2026, 14:00 – Sunday, February 28, 2027, 23:59 (JST / UTC+9)

*You can also leave a group within the period above.

[How to Participate]

Tap “Event” on the PicTrée main screen and select “Group Battle: Okinawa Photo Hunt 2026-2027” to participate.

◼️How to Create or Join a Group

There are two ways to participate: “① Create a new group yourself” or “② Join an existing group.”

●To create a new group

① After launching the app, tap “Event” at the bottom left of the map screen.

② In the “Event List,” tap “Group Battle: Okinawa Photo Hunt 2026-2027.”

③ Tap “Join Event” at the bottom of the screen.

④ Tap “Create a new group here.”

⑤ Enter a group name and tap “Create.”

⑥ Your group has been created! Once an invitation code is issued, share it with the friends you want to invite to your group.

●To join an existing group

Follow steps ① to ③ above.

① After launching the app, tap “Event” at the bottom left of the map screen.

② In the “Event List,” tap “Group Battle: Okinawa Photo Hunt 2026-2027.”

③ Tap “Join Event” at the bottom of the screen.

④ Enter the invitation code of the group you want to join and tap “OK.”

⑤ Tap “Join” on the group join confirmation screen.

⑥ You have joined the group!

◼️Group Information

On the group information screen, you can check information about your group.

・Ranking: Ranking information for your group

・Activity: Displays the activities of group members

・Members: List of current members

To invite friends to your group, share the invitation code at the bottom right of the screen.

◼️Notes

・Each group can have [up to 5 members].

・Each player can belong to only one group.

・During the group creation & joining period, you can create, join, and leave groups as many times as you like.

・Rewards will not be granted if fraud is suspected.

[Group Ranking Rewards]

Rewards will be granted at the end of the event based on the group ranking.

Reward Coins worth a total of ¥500,000 will be distributed to the top 50 groups as Split Rewards. In addition, Individual Rewards (wire items) will be given to all group members according to their rank.

(Unit: Reward Coins)

*Split Rewards are divided equally among the group members (e.g., if a 5-member group places 1st, each member receives 240,000 Reward Coins (¥20,000 equivalent)).
