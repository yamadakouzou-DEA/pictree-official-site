Lottery Ticket (ZAUO Monthly Drawing)

Each time you photograph a photo asset, you receive a treasure chest, which always contains 1 “Lottery Ticket (ZAUO Monthly Drawing).”

In addition, if you photograph all 5 photo assets at one restaurant, you will receive 1 more lottery ticket as a “Mission Clear Reward.”

You can receive up to 6 lottery tickets per restaurant.

A drawing is held every month, and winners will receive the following rewards.

*There are no photo rewards (Reward Coins) for individual photo spots.

●About the Drawing

Once a month, a drawing is held among players who hold lottery tickets, and winners receive Reward Coins (exchangeable for Amazon Gift Cards and more).

Lottery tickets obtained between 0:00 on the 1st and 23:59 on the last day of a month are eligible for the drawing held at 0:00 on the 1st of the following month (JST / UTC+9).

Drawing results can be checked in the “Mail Box” in PicTrée.
