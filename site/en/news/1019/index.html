Regarding the PicTrée Schedule Post-July

Thank you for playing PicTrée.
Although the pilot test in Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture will conclude on June 29th, we will continue our trials in different regions.

 

The schedule is as follows:

 

Until June 29th: Pilot test in Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture
June 30th to July 3rd: Break period
July 4th to 6th: Special Event – Kyoto Stamp Rally – Energize IVS Kyoto!
July 7th to 19th: Break period
From July 20th (tentative): Tokyo Pre-season
From August onward: Tokyo Main Season (The main season in Tokyo will feature special functions and rules)

 

Details for each event will be announced later.
We look forward to your continued support of PicTrée.
