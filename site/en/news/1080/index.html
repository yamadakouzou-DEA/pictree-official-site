Details of PicTrée Tokyo Season

PicTrée is launching its second phase of pilot tests from July 26th to November, targeting the Chiyoda, Chuo, and Minato wards of Tokyo.
The Tokyo trials are divided into two phases: the “Tokyo Pre-Season” from July 26th to early September, and the “Tokyo Main Season” from early September to November, which will include new features.

 

■Event Duration:
Tokyo Pre-Season: July 26, 2024, 7:00 AM to early September
Tokyo Main Season: Early September to November 2024
*Please note that the exact ending time of the Pre-Season and the starting time of the Main Season will be announced later.

 

■Areas Covered: Chiyoda Ward, Minato Ward, Chuo Ward

 

■Photography Target: Utility poles

 

■Format:
Tokyo Pre-Season: Similar to the first pilot test conducted in Maebashi City.
Tokyo Main Season: In addition to the pre-season activities, a “Mission Mode” will be added, featuring mission-based photography events.

 

■Implementation Times:
Photography Time: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Check-in Time: Unlimited
Note: The operational hours for the main season are subject to change, and we will provide an updated announcement accordingly.

 

■Game Rewards:
Tokyo Pre-Season:
Initial Photography Reward: 10 reward coins per utility pole (equivalent to 1 yen).

■Check-in Damage Multiplier:
The damage inflicted during check-ins will be adjusted based on the ranking position of the team you belong to.
1st Place Team: 0.5
2nd Place Team: 0.8
3rd Place Team: 1.0

 

■About the New ‘Mission Mode’:
Periodic “Photography Missions” will be activated for certain power assets within the targeted area.
Users can earn incentives by photographing the targeted power assets within a set time limit.

 

■Precautions for the Event:
As the event is held in Tokyo, high traffic volumes are expected. Avoid photographing in heavily trafficked areas and be mindful of surrounding traffic conditions when taking photos.
When taking photos, ensure that bystanders are not captured in the frame.
Be considerate of people and objects around you while photographing to avoid obstructing or colliding with them.
Do not enter private property for photography. Ensure you do not trespass.
As temperatures may be high during this period, avoid long playing sessions. Take breaks and stay hydrated to ensure safe and comfortable gameplay.
