Details of PicTrée Tokyo Main Season 1

Here are the details for Tokyo Season 1:

 

Duration: September 13, 2024 (Friday), 7:00 AM to September 30, 2024 (Monday), 6:00 PM

 

Photography Times:
Regular photography: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Mission: No time restrictions
Additionally, there are no time restrictions for “check-ins.”

 

Areas Covered: Chiyoda Ward, Minato Ward, Chuo Ward

 

Initial Photography Rewards:
-Utility poles: 60 coins per pole
-Ventilation shafts: 60 coins per shaft
※ What are ventilation shafts? They are openings used for ventilating underground electrical equipment and for worker access, typically found on pathways and roadsides. Targets include any that have one of the following three types of marks stamped on them.

 

　

 

Mission Rewards:
Starting with the Tokyo main season, “Mission” have been introduced.
These missions occur sporadically, and when they do, players can photograph designated electrical assets within a set time limit. Completion rankings determine the rewards earned.
Players can check for active “Mission” by clicking the “Mission” button on the PicTrée home screen and viewing the list of missions. Additionally, mission announcements will be made via the official PicTrée X account (@pictree_dea).
Rewards for completing photography missions are as follows:

1st 7500

5th 1000

※Rewards are available for game items even if you do not finish in the top 5.
※Please note that the reward amounts are subject to change.

 

　

 

Ranking Rewards:
In PicTrée, rankings are determined on a seasonal basis with both “Team Rankings” and “Individual Rankings” within each team. The rewards you can earn at the end of the season are based on these rankings at the season’s close.

 

・Coefficient for Team Points Calculation:
Team rankings are determined by the total team points, which are calculated from the cumulative rank points of all players within each team. However, for the calculation of team points, a specific coefficient is applied to the cumulative rank points of the top-ranking players in each team, as detailed in the table below.

Ranking within the Team Coefficient for Individual Rankings within the Team
