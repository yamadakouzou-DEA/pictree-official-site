About the Distribution of PicTrée Original Merchandise

Regarding PicTrée Original Merchandise

 

Following the merchandise survey conducted on X for PICTERs, we are excited to introduce two new items: the “Investigation Card”, “Neck Strap” and “Cooling Scarf Towel”!

 

During Tokyo Season 1, those who visit our designated pickup locations within the event area on the specified dates will receive either an “Investigation Card & Neck Strap” or a “Cooling Scarf Towel & Investigation Card” as a gift.

 

Please check the following details if you are interested in receiving the merchandise.

 

【PicTrée Merchandise Pickup Schedule and Location】
●Dates for Pickup:
September 13 (Fri), 14 (Sat), 15 (Sun), 17 (Tue), 19 (Thu), 24 (Tue), 26 (Thu)

●Time:
1:00 PM to 6:00 PM

●Location:
Shinbashi Station, Ginza-guchi (Exit 4)

 

【How to Receive the Merchandise】
For users wishing to pick up merchandise, please send the following information via DM on PicTrée’s official X account:

▾Required Information for Merchandise Pickup:
– Desired pickup date and time slot
– Choice of merchandise: ① Investigation Card & Neck Strap or ② Cooling Scarf Towel & Investigation Card

 

●Merchandise Pickup Details on the Day
On the scheduled merchandise pickup days, PicTrée staff wearing armbands will distribute the items at the designated location.

 
 

Special Promotion
Additionally, those who post on X using the hashtags #ピクトレ and #ピクトレグッズ will receive PicTrée-branded salt tablets as an extra gift! This offer is available until supplies run out.

 

■Additional Information:
– An armband will be given as a complimentary gift to all those who request it.
– The PicTrée-branded water, “PicTrée Water,” is currently in production.
