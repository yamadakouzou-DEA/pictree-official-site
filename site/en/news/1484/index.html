PicTrée × GeoQuest Collaboration! “Infrastructure Inspection King”

PicTrée will host a special event in collaboration with Geo Technologies’ “GeoQuest” called “Infrastructure Inspection King” from November 1 to November 24, 2024.

 

■Event Overview
The event will be conducted in Tokyo Minato, Chiyoda, and Chuo wards. Participants will use both “PicTrée” and “GeoQuest” apps to photograph various urban infrastructure, and the winner of the “Infrastructure Inspection King” title will be decided based on the total scores.

 

■Event Details
・Dates:
Photo taking period: November 1, 2024, 7:00 AM to November 24, 2024, 6:00 PM
Results announcement: November 29, 2024
・Areas: Minato, Chiyoda, and Chuo wards in Tokyo
*Please check the maps within the PicTrée and GeoQuest apps for more details.
・Photography targets:
PicTrée: Utility poles, Ventilation shafts, Ground Equipment
GeoQuest: Traffic signs, evacuation signs, fire extinguishers/hydrants, parking meters
・How to participate:
Enter through the following participation form:
https://forms.gle/Gj9qEjTFGiDxE94H6
Install both “PicTrée” and “GeoQuest” apps, and photograph the designated event targets in each app.

・Scoring Method:
Rankings will be created based on scores in each app, and points will be awarded based on rank. The total points awarded across both platforms will be combined to create an overall ranking.

 

PicTrée: Points based on individual rank points accumulated during the event period.
GeoQuest: Points based on the number of OK posts related to the event quests.

・Important Notes:
– Participation awards will be granted to all participants who use both apps. However, to qualify for these awards, participants must register for the event, make at least one OK post on GeoQuest, and send at least one asset (one asset’s worth of photos and submission) on PicTrée.
– For ranking-based score allocation, if there are tie scores in GeoQuest, the earliest posts will be ranked higher. In PicTrée, participants with the same rank points will share the same ranking position.
– Final rankings will be announced on November 29 via the official X accounts of both GeoQuest and PicTrée.
– For GeoQuest: Only posts that are judged to be valid after photo review will qualify. Mile distribution may be delayed. If you disconnect from “TRIMA,” mile credits cannot be given. The miles awarded can be checked under the “mile history” in “TRIMA.”
