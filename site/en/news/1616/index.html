Special Building Photography Event – Mystery! Building Photo Mission in Shibuya

PicTrée will be hosting a special event titled “Mystery! Building Photo Mission in Shibuya” from December 7th (Sat) to December 8th (Sun), 2024.

 

■ Event Purpose
PicTrée supports photographing various infrastructures beyond just utility poles to assist with corporate and municipal challenges. This event specifically focuses on photographing city buildings to evaluate their usefulness in urban 3D model development.

 

■ Event Overview
This event involves using “PicTrée” to photograph various buildings around the Shibuya Station to Harajuku Station area while participating in a mystery-solving challenge. Building photography will be carried out using “PicTrée’s” photography missions. Each targeted building will have one mission set, and completing the photography will grant rewards.
Simultaneously, a mystery-solving activity will take place, and solving the set mysteries will also grant rewards based on your performance in the puzzle-solving tasks.

 

■ Event Details

・Event period (photo-taking hours):
December 7, 2024 (Saturday) 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, December 8, 2024 (Sunday) 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
The event will proceed regardless of weather conditions.
Mystery-solving results announcement: December 16, 2024 (Monday).
・Area: Shibuya Ward, Tokyo (around Shibuya Station to Harajuku Station).
*Please check the PicTrée app map for details.
・Photography targets: Commercial facilities, office buildings.

・How to Participate:
①Install “PicTrée”
▼Download PicTrée here▼
App Store：https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id6478513639
Google Play：https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playmining.i2e&hl=ja&gl=jp
 
②In PicTrée, select the “Mission” named “【Event】Building Photo” and proceed to photograph the designated buildings. There are a total of 20 building photography missions. The timing for these missions is constrained by the “Event Period (photo-taking hours)”—all missions become available at 9:00 AM on December 7, 2024 (Saturday) and end at 5:00 PM on December 8, 2024 (Sunday). For each building photography mission, you are required to photograph one building (from multiple directions).

 

・Mystery Challenge! Special Event:
Wander around the target area and while completing the photography missions, you’ll also have the chance to engage in a mystery-solving challenge. Players are free to choose the starting point and the order in which they visit the mystery spots. Completing 20 photography missions and submitting the keyword derived from the mystery sheet through a specified response form will mark the completion of the event. Ranking will be determined based on the speed of reaching the goal among players who successfully solve and submit the correct keyword.
*Details on the mystery sheet and the keyword response form will be announced later.

 

・Mission Rewards:
For each targeted photography mission completed, 4,000 reward coins will be granted per mission.
*Reward coins will be awarded to the first 30 participants per mission.

 

・Mystery Challenge! Special Event Rewards:
In addition to the regular mission rewards, special event rewards are also available.
Based on the rankings determined by the above Mystery Challenge! event rules, the following Amazon gift cards will be awarded as special event prizes:
1st Place: Amazon gift card worth 5,000 yen
2nd Place: Amazon gift card worth 4,000 yen
3rd Place: Amazon gift card worth 3,000 yen
4th Place: Amazon gift card worth 2,000 yen
5th Place: Amazon gift card worth 1,000 yen
Participation Prize: Power line 200m×10

 

・Survey Rewards:
Please enter using the response form that will be announced later.
All participants who complete the photography mission and respond to the survey will receive an Amazon gift card worth 300 yen.

 

・Notes:
-The results of the puzzle-solving will be announced on PicTrée’s official X by December 16th (Monday).
-Please send the keyword derived from the puzzle-solving sheet after completing 20 missions. If sent before completing 20 missions, it will not qualify for a reward, so be sure to send it after completion.
-To qualify for survey rewards, participants must have photographed and completed the mission for the designated object during the event period (at least one asset photographed and transmitted is required). Note that to receive the gift, the “Player ID” and “email address (account required for logging into PicTrée)” registered on PicTrée are necessary.
-Amazon gift cards will be granted sequentially to the above email addresses after December 16th (Monday).
