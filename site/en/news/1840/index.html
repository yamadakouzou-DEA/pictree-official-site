News2024-12-19

Using “Exclusive Power Line Items” (500m/1,000m/2,000m) in the Tokyo Season

We previously announced that the “Exclusive Power Line Items,” which allow long-distance pole connections, could be used outside the Akita Season (Northern Akita Season, Central & Southern Akita Season) but with distance limitations.

 

However, it has been confirmed that due to certain actions, these Exclusive Power Line Items have been used in the Tokyo Season exceeding the intended distance limitations.

 

This incident was not anticipated by the PicTrée management team and could potentially create an unfair advantage between players who participated in the Akita Season and those who did not. Therefore, we will implement the following measures:

 

Response Policy:
・Exclusive Power Line Items can still be used in the Tokyo Season, but the maximum connection distance is capped at 850 meters, which is the distance achievable through normal operations.
・If connections exceed 850 meters, they will be considered as connected at 850 meters, and a rank point adjustment will be made after the season ends.

 

Please be aware that the rankings displayed in the game at the end of Tokyo Main Season 4 will reflect the pre-adjustment rankings. The final rankings will be officially announced at a later date.

 

Additionally, the Exclusive Power Line Items will be removed after the Akita Season ends and will no longer be available for use.

 

We apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused.
