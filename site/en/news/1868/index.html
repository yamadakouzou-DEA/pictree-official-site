“Love Live! Sunshine!!” Collaboration Confirmed for the Numazu Season!

The Numazu Season, a collaboration with “Love Live! Sunshine!!”, is set to begin on February 1, 2025 (Saturday). This event will be carried out with the support of TEPCO Power Grid, GGG, DEA, the backing of Numazu City, and special cooperation from Bandai Namco Filmworks INC.

 

The Numazu Season will take place across Numazu City in Shizuoka Prefecture from February 1 (Saturday) to April 27 (Sunday).
The entire area of Numazu City will be divided into five parts, with each part hosting one of the five seasons sequentially.

 

Additionally, during the Numazu Season, team selection will feature special collaboration specifications. There will also be appearances of special utility poles and special effects for connecting poles, incorporating numerous elements from the “Love Live! Sunshine!!” collaboration into the game.

 

 

Furthermore, for the Numazu Season, the reward points can be exchanged not only for Amazon gift cards and DEAPcoin but also for special collaboration merchandise.

 

Please look forward to this new development in PicTrée during the Numazu Season.
