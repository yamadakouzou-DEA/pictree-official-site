Numazu Season: About the “Disaster Prevention Mission”

In the Numazu Season, starting from Season 3, special missions called “DDisaster Prevention Mission” will be introduced, focusing on disaster prevention-related facilities.

 

【How to Participate in the Disaster Prevention Mission】
Follow the steps below to display the target facilities for the Disaster Prevention Mission on the map:
1. Select Numazu Season, then tap “Mission” from the map screen.
2. Tap “Disaster Prevention Mission” from the “Photography Mission List.”
3. Tap “Challenge.”

 

 

【Photography Target and Reward】

Photography Target Number of objects to be photographed Photography fee per spot
View-O (Large Observation Water Gate) 1 720 Reward Coins
TSUNAMI Evacuation Tower 3 960 Reward Coins
Fire Brigade Station 40 240 Reward Coins
Fire Hydrant 49 240 Reward Coins
Evacuation Site Sign 103 240 Reward Coins

※ For each photography target, only the first 100 participants per spot will be eligible to receive reward coins.

 

 

【Disaster Prevention Mission Period】
March 8, 2025 (Sat) 9:00 – April 27, 2025 (Sun) 16:00

 

【Point Ranking Rewards】
In addition to the regular photography rewards, point ranking rewards are also available in the DDisaster Prevention Mission.
Point ranking rewards are distributed based on the ranking determined by the number of disaster prevention-related facilities photographed and the performance in the Disaster Prevention Quiz (starting from Season 4) at the end of the Disaster Prevention Mission.
The Disaster Prevention Mission ranking can be checked by tapping “Ranking” in the upper right corner of the Disaster Prevention Mission screen.
