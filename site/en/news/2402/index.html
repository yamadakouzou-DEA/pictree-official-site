Let’s Capture Remote Utility Poles Together! PicTrée Caravan Support Campaign

The North Kanto Season has now entered its latter half, with over 80% of the 150,000 utility poles already captured. The remaining poles are gradually decreasing, but many of the unphotographed poles are located in areas with limited access via public transportation. Therefore, the cooperation of PicTrée users (hereafter referred to as PICTERs) who can use cars has become increasingly important.
To support PICTERs who help transport other players by car, we are launching the “PicTrée Caravan Support Campaign.”

 

Campaign Overview:
If you use a private car or rental car and take other PICTERs as passengers to conduct photography, both the driver and passengers will receive additional reward coins based on the total number of utility poles photographed.

 

Target Season: North Kanto Season

 

March 8 (Sat) – March 16 (Sun)

 

Eligible Utility Poles:Unphotographed utility poles (utility poles that have never been photographed)
※ Utility poles that have been photographed (captured) once and later returned to a neutral state are not eligible.

 

How to Participate: Both the driver and passengers must complete and submit the “Pre-Application Form” and “Report Form” by following the steps below.
①Submit the Pre-Registration Form:
Enter necessary details such as the planned date for the caravan, usernames of the driver and passengers, and submit the form.
“Pre-Application Form” https://forms.gle/uPTjvhvGfHvnLryd8
②Conduct the Utility Pole Photography Caravan:
Follow the details entered in the pre-registration form and carry out the photography caravan.
※When submitting the report form, a group photo including all participants is required. Don’t forget to take a picture.
※Each participant must photograph at least 10 utility poles to be eligible.
③Submit the Report Form:
Enter the required details, including the photos taken during the event, and submit the form.
“Report Form” https://forms.gle/hxFedFmW8m6zR2tcA

 

Rewards:
Reward coins will be granted to both the driver and passengers who participate in the caravan.
The amount of reward coins given will be determined based on the total number of utility poles photographed by the driver and passengers on the caravan day (review-approved photos only).
Rewards will be distributed approximately one week after submitting the report form.

Total Number of Photographed Poles Reward Coins Passenger Reward
1,000 poles or more 150,000 coins 30,000 coins
750 – 999 poles 100,000 coins 20,000 coins
500 – 749 poles 75,000 coins 15,000 coins
250 – 499 poles 50,000 coins 10,000 coins
100 – 249 poles 25,000 coins 5,000 coins

※ Caravans with fewer than 100 poles in total will not be eligible for rewards.

 

Important Notes:
• To participate in this campaign, both the “Pre-Registration Form” and the “Report Form” must be submitted. Submitting only one of them will result in disqualification.
If the “Scheduled Caravan Date” spans multiple days, multiple applications are required. Please submit an application for each date separately.
To participate in the campaign, at least one additional PICTER must accompany the driver and participate in the shooting. (Solo participation by the driver is not allowed.)
Each PICTER can participate in this campaign up to two times. (Applications for three or more times will not be eligible.)
• Each participant must photograph at least 10 utility poles. If a participant fails to meet this requirement, their participation will be considered invalid.
• Any suspicious activity, such as using multiple accounts, may result in disqualification from the campaign. If fraudulent behavior violating the terms of service is detected, the account may be suspended.
• This campaign is subject to suspension or termination without prior notice. Thank you for your understanding.
