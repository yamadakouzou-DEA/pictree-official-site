About PicTrée’s New Features (1.9.0)

In PicTrée, a new update will be implemented on Friday, April 4, 2025, introducing new features and improvements.
This article provides an overview of the update.

 

【List of New Features】
① Batch Use of Attack/Recovery Items
② Display of Availability of Rewards for Second and Subsequent Photographs
③ Display of Number of Mission Achievers
④ Quiz-Type Ads Added to Banner Ads at the Bottom of the Screen
⑤ Bug Fix Related to the Reporting Feature

 

① Batch Use of Attack/Recovery Items on Utility Poles
When using attack or recovery items on electric assets, you can now select the number of items to use at once.

 

 

② Display of Availability of Rewards for Second and Subsequent Photographs
Until now, you could only confirm whether there was an “Initial Shooting Reward.” With this update, you can check whether rewards are available for the second and subsequent shootings as well.
Example: In the screen shown below, by neutralizing this utility pole and retaking the photograph, you can earn “120 reward coins.”

 

 

③ Display of Number of Mission Achievers in Missions
For missions, the number of Photographers who have completed the mission will now be displayed in the upper right corner of the shooting target screen.

 

 

④ Quiz-Type Ads Added to Banner Ads at the Bottom of the Screen
Quiz-type ads will now appear in the banner ads displayed at the bottom of the map screen.
By tapping the banner ad and correctly answering the quiz, you can earn rewards such as reward coins (you can answer regardless of your location).
Note: Regular banner ads (non-quiz ads) will also continue to appear.

 

 

⑤ Bug Fix Related to the Reporting Feature
A bug where the captured image would be reset if a report was submitted without sending the photograph from the shooting screen has been fixed.
