Thank you for always playing PicTrée!
Thanks to all the PICTERs, PicTrée is celebrating its 1st anniversary on April 13!
In this article, we are excited to announce the Top 100 in the 1-Year Shooting Count Ranking for PicTrée.
Original PicTrée goods will be awarded to the top 100 PICTERs in the ranking! (For details about the giveaway, please refer to the article below.)
https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/en/news/2670/

 

● Aggregation Period:
April 13, 2024 (Sat) 9:00 – April 4, 2025 (Fri) 16:00

 

● Target Seasons:
Maebashi Season 1–5, Tokyo Pre-Season, Tokyo Main Season 1–5, Akita Season (“Northern Akita” and “Central & Southern Akita”), North Kanto Season, Numazu Season 1–4

 

● Aggregation Method:
The number of photos taken in PicTrée during the target period (limited to those marked as “Review OK”) was totaled to calculate the cumulative “infrastructure photo count.”

Poles, Pole(with Protective Tube), Pole(with Support Pole), Ventilation shafts, Ground Equipment
