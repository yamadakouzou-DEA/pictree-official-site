Celebrating PicTrée’s 1st Anniversary!! Announcing 4 Major Projects

Thank you for always playing PicTrée!
Thanks to all the PICTERs, PicTrée will celebrate its 1st anniversary on April 13!
To commemorate the 1st anniversary, we will be holding the “1st Anniversary! 4 Major Projects” from April 11 to April 18!

 

———————————————
[1st Anniversary! 4 Major Projects]
<Part 1> Thank You, PICTERs! Giveaway Campaign
<Part 2> 1st Anniversary! “Memory Post Contest”
<Part 3> Big PicT Giveaway!! 1st Anniversary Offline Meetup
<Part 4> 1st Anniversary Special X Space: “Looking Back Together with Kito-P & 旅嬢saori!”
———————————————

 

[Details]
<Part 1> Thank You, PICTERs! Giveaway Campaign

 

Throughout the year, PICTERs have captured over 394,000 infrastructure assets, including utility poles, resulting in more than 1 million photos that contributed to infrastructure inspection and maintenance efforts.
As a token of our gratitude for this incredible contribution, we will be giving PicTrée original goods to the top-ranking PICTERs based on the total number of infrastructure photos taken during the past year!

 

〇 Gift Recipients:
The top 100 players in the infrastructure shooting ranking, based on the number of reviewed and approved photos taken across all seasons held between April 13, 2024, and April 4, 2025 (from Maebashi Season 1 to Numazu Season 4).

 

Check the ranking results here: https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/en/news/2651/

 

〇 Original Gifts:
・Top 1st–5th Place: Gold Original Business/Survey Card
 ※Your PICTER player name and the total number of infrastructures photographed during the period will be engraved on the card.
・Top 6th–20th Place: Silver Original Business/Survey Card
 ※Player names and shooting numbers will not be included; it will be a common design.
・Top 21st–100th Place: 1st Anniversary Keychain

 

〇 How to Receive Your Gift:
If you are one of the recipients, please fill out the delivery form linked below with your shipping information. We will send your gift to the address provided!
 Gift Shipping Information Form: https://forms.gle/nn1ioW4oAKCzNZRs9
※ Submission Deadline: April 30, 2025 (Wed) 23:59
※ Please note that we will not be able to ship gifts if the deadline is missed.
※ Shipping is scheduled for mid to late May.

<Part 2> 1st Anniversary! “Memory Post Contest”
Over the past year, PicTrée seasons were held across Japan, leading to many encounters, discoveries, and memorable moments through infrastructure photography.
We invite all PICTERs to share your favorite memories from the past year!
Please post a photo and a short story on X (formerly Twitter) with the hashtag “#ピクトレ1周年” !

 

〇 Prizes:
Among the PICTERs who participate, three individuals with the most memorable posts will each receive a 1st Anniversary Keychain!

 

〇 Campaign Period:
– Memory posting period: April 11 (Fri) – April 17 (Thu)
– Winner announcement: April 18 (Fri) during the X Space session starting at 21:00
– Prize shipping: Scheduled for mid to late May
※ Please note that the schedule is subject to change.

 

〇 Prize Shipping Method:
After the winners are announced, we will contact you via DM on X to collect your shipping information and send the prize to the provided address.

 

Both PICTERs who played onsite and those who participated remotely have a chance to win, so please join in!
