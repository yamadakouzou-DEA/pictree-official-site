Photography Targets and Lucky Telephone Pole in the PicTrée Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season

Starting from Saturday, May 17, 2025, a new season, “Prefecture Battle! Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata” (Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season), will be held, targeting three prefectures: Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata.
This season’s photography targets are two types of assets: “Steel Telephone Pole” and “Concrete Telephone Pole.”
Make sure to capture key points during your photography to maximize the use of your photos for inspection purposes.

 

【Photography Targets】
■ Steel Telephone Pole
There are two types: with and without support poles.

 

<Icon>
-Steel Telephone Pole

-Steel Telephone Pole (with Support Pole)

 

 

<Photography Angles> The following six angles are the target (nine angles if there is a supporting pole).
Full View 1, Full View 2, Pole Number, Lower View 1, Lower View 2, Lower View 3

 

-Full View：
Ensure that the entire pole is captured in the frame, including both the top and bottom.
For the second shot, take the photo from an angle that is 90-180 degrees different from the first position.

 

 

-Pole Number:
Take a photo so that the characters on the number plate are clearly visible.
If there are multiple number plates on a single pole, make sure they are all visible in one photo.

 

 

-Lower View:
Capture the photo so that the boundary between the ground and the pole is clearly visible, getting as close as possible.
If there is grass around, gently press it down with your foot to ensure the boundary is visible.
Take the second and third photos from different angles, covering three directions: front, back, and sides.

 


 

■ Concrete Telephone Pole
There are two types: with and without support poles.

 

<Icon>
-Concrete Telephone Pole

-Concrete Telephone Pole (with Support Pole)

 

 

<Photography Angles> The following five angles are the target (seven angles if there is a supporting pole).
Full View 1, Full View 2, Pole Number, Step Bolt Socket, Nameplate

 

-Full View 1 & 2, Pole Number:
Please take photos from the same angles as the Steel Telephone Pole.

 

-Step Bolt Socket:
The scaffolding bolt receptacle is a hole about 2 cm in diameter.
Make sure to photograph it from the front so that the color is clearly visible.

 

 

-Nameplate:
Zoom in as much as possible and photograph the nameplate straight on, ensuring that the text is clearly readable and the image is not blurry.

 

 

[Photography Rewards]
Photography rewards vary depending on the type of pole (Steel Telephone Pole/Concrete Telephone Pole) and the location (urban area/suburban area).

Type of Pole Location Area Initial Photography Reward Amount
Steel Telephone Pole urban area 600 Coins(Equivalent to 50 yen)
suburban area 720 Coins(Equivalent to 60 yen)
Concrete Telephone Pole urban area 480 Coins(Equivalent to 40 yen)
suburban area 600 Coins(Equivalent to 50 yen)

※ No Photography rewards will be given for the second and subsequent shots.

 

You can distinguish between “urban area” and “suburban area” by checking the reward amount number on the asset details screen (the “Earn 〇〇 Coins!” section above the “Take a Photo” button).

 

 

[Lucky Telephone Pole]
During the Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season, special poles called “Lucky Telephone Poles” will appear.
Lucky Telephone Poles differ from regular poles in both asset icons and images. In addition to regular shooting rewards, you have a chance to earn an extra reward called “Lucky Reward.”

 

<Lucky Telephone Pole Icon>
-Lucky Steel Telephone Pole

-Lucky Steel Telephone Pole (with Support Pole)

-Lucky Concrete Telephone Pole

-Lucky Concrete Telephone Pole (with Support Pole)

 

 

<Rewards from Lucky Telephone Poles>
After shooting and submitting a Lucky Telephone Pole, if the image passes the review, you will receive the “Initial Photography Reward (First Reward).”
In addition, if the captured image is recognized as high quality, you can receive an “Lucky Reward (Second Reward)” at a later date.
To take high-quality images, make sure to follow the shooting points mentioned above.
