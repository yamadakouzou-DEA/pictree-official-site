Operation Vines in Chiba & Ibaraki Season: Vines Discovery Ranking <First Ranking>

We are pleased to announce the first ranking for the number of vines discovered in the “Operation Vines in Chiba & Ibaraki” season.
Players who captured images of power poles with actual vine growth during the “Vines Boost Period” will receive “Vines Discovery Rewards” based on the number of such images they have taken.
※ “Vines Discovery Rewards” are only awarded for the first capture of each pole (subsequent captures of the same pole are not eligible).
※ “Vines Discovery Rewards” will be distributed three times during the season, after being verified by the operation team.
