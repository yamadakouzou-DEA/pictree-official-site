PicTrée Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season: Friend Referral Campaign

Thank you for using PicTrée on a regular basis. From May 27 to June 22, 2025, we will be holding a Friend Referral Campaign during the Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season.

 

[Campaign Overview]

 

Campaign Period:
Tuesday, May 27, 2025, 18:00 – Sunday, June 22, 2025, 18:00

 

Details:
During the campaign period, if a user currently playing the Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season refers a friend or family member who has not yet played PicTrée, and the referred person installs the app and photographs 50 or more poles in the Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season, both the referrer and the referred person will receive reward coins equivalent to 500 yen.

 

How to Participate:
After meeting the conditions, please submit both the referrer’s and the referred person’s player name and player IDs using the form below.
Either party may submit the form—there is no need for both to do so.

 

<< Submission Form >> https://forms.gle/Q45THBKDGEGeiFdW7

 

Reward Distribution:
After verifying the eligibility, reward coins equivalent to 500 yen will be granted to both the referrer and the referred person sequentially.

 

Important Notes:
– Referrers must already be playing in the Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season (must have photographed at least one utility pole during this season).
– The referrer does not need to install the app during the campaign period. As long as the referred person installs the app during the campaign period and photographs 50 or more utility poles, the conditions will be met.
– A referrer may refer multiple users, up to a maximum of 20.
– Referred users must install the PicTrée app after the referrer. Users who installed the app before the referrer are not eligible.
– Each referred user can only receive the referral reward once. If multiple referrers submit a claim for the same user, only the first valid submission will be accepted.
– If fraudulent activity is suspected, rewards will not be granted.
– This campaign may be altered or canceled without prior notice.

 

We look forward to your participation!
