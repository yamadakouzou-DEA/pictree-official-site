Announce of Lucky Reward Distribution for the “Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season” <First Ranking>

We are pleased to announce of Lucky Reward Distribution for the “Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season.”

 

In the Aomori, Akita, and Yamagata Season, special poles known as “Lucky Telephone Poles” have appeared.
These “Lucky Telephone Poles” feature distinct asset icons and images compared to regular poles, and in addition to standard photo rewards, they offer a chance to earn additional Lucky Rewards.
For more details, visit: https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/en/news/3173/

 

※ “Lucky Rewards” (Secondary Rewards) are additional rewards granted at a later date when submitted photos are judged to be of high quality.
※ Multiple reward distribution dates will be scheduled during the season, and rewards will be granted after verification by the administration.
※ If the images taken during the aggregation period are not deemed to be of high quality, no rewards will be granted to the photographer.

● Reward Distribution Date:

※If the images taken during the first aggregation period were not deemed high quality, no rewards were granted to the photographer. The corresponding utility poles became neutral poles and were eligible for re-photographing starting from Saturday, June 14, 2025.
