About PicTrée’s New Features (2.1.0)

In PicTrée, a new update will be implemented on Friday, July 4, 2025, introducing new features and improvements.
This article provides an overview of the update.

 

【List of New Features】
①Heat map display
②Display by area and Ranking by area

 

①Heat Map Display
As you zoom out of the map on the home screen, the display of the location of the shooting asset switches to a heat map display.
This heat-map display has been upgraded to allow more accurate location of shooting assets.

 

②Display by area and Ranking by area
It is now possible to check a single season in units of more detailed “areas”.
In addition, rankings are now compiled by area.
Information on areas can be found on the following screen.
*The display by area will be applied from “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025” starting July 12.

 

1. Area indication on the asset information screen
It shows which area each shooting asset belongs to.

 

2. Area indication on the map
Zoom out on the map to see the center of each area and the shooting status.
In addition, the color of the edge of the area icon is displayed in the color of the team with the highest number of assets conquered in that area..

 

3. Area display when tapping the center button
Tapping the center button on the home screen displays a list of areas and allows you to move to the center of the selected area.
In the area list, you can also check the conquered status of each area.

 

4. Display of Area Ranking on the ranking screen
You can check the “Area Ranking” from the ranking screen.

 

5. Display of area-by-area ranking results on the Season Results screen
You can check the ranking results for each area on the results screen at the end of the season.
