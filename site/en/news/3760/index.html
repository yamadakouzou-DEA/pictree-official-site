PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido [Sapporo]: Mini Opening Ceremony & User Meetup Announcement

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
Starting Saturday, July 12, 2025, PicTrée’s Hokkaido season will launch for the first time!
In addition to the kickoff event in Hakodate City, a mini opening ceremony & user meetup will also be held in Sapporo City!
PICTERs planning to join the event in Sapporo, we hope you will participate enthusiastically!

 

◎ Mini Opening Ceremony
【Date & Time】
Saturday, July 12, from 8:45 AM

 

【Venue】
Odori Park, in front of the TV Tower (Odori Nishi 1-chome, Chuo-ku, Sapporo)

 

【Contents】
・Explanation of the rules for PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido
・Distribution of armbands and investigator cards!
・You might find group members!?
 Check the announcement below on how to create a group:
  PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025 – About the Group Competition

 

※No advance registration required!!

 

◎ User Meetup
【Date & Time】
Saturday, July 12, 2025, 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM

 

【Venue】
An izakaya in Sapporo (to be announced)

 

【Participation Fee】
Around 3,000 yen

 

【Schedule】
July 9 (Wed): Registration deadline
July 10 (Thu): Venue (restaurant) decided, will be announced via X DM group
July 12 (Sat) 6:00 PM: Meetup held

 

Operation staff will also join!
Let’s celebrate the first day of City Battle in Hokkaido together!

 

If you wish to participate, please fill out the required information and submit the application form below.
