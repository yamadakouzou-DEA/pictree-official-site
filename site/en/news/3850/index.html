PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025 Friend Referral Campaign

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
From July 12 to October 12, 2025, we will be holding a “Friend Referral Campaign” during the PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025.

 

【Campaign Overview】

 

Implementation Period:
Saturday, July 12, 2025, 9:00 ～ Sunday, October 12, 2025, 17:00

 

Details:
During the campaign period, users playing PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025 can refer friends or family members who have not yet played PicTrée. If the referred person newly installs the app and photographs 50 or more poles during PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025, both the referrer and the referred person will receive reward coins equivalent to 500 yen.

 

How to Participate:
After meeting the conditions, please report both the referrer and referred player names and player IDs via the form below.
*Applications can be submitted by either the referrer or the referred person (submission by both is not required).

 

<<Application Form>> https://forms.gle/n6b4WHfzP6CRLpGY8

 

Rewards:
After confirming that the conditions have been met, reward coins equivalent to 500 yen will be granted to both the referrer and the referred person.
Rewards are scheduled to be distributed in the following three installments.

 

<Scheduled Reward Distribution Dates>
・1st: Scheduled for mid-August
・2nd: Scheduled for mid-September
・3rd: Scheduled for mid-October

 

Notes:
・The referrer must already be a user playing PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025 (must have photographed at least one pole in this season).
・The referrer does not need to install the app during the implementation period. The condition is met if the referred person installs the app and photographs 50 or more poles during the implementation period.
・The referrer can refer multiple people, up to a maximum of 20.
・The referred person must be a user who installed the app after the referrer (users who installed before the referrer are not eligible).
・The referred person can only receive the campaign reward once (if multiple referrers submit an application for the same referred person, only the earliest application will be considered valid).
・If fraudulent activity is suspected, rewards will not be granted.
・This campaign may be changed or canceled without notice.

 

We look forward to your participation.
