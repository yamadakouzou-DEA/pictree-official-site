PicTrée Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima, and Niigata Season – Friend Referral Campaign

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
From July 23 to September 15, 2025, we will be holding a “Friend Referral Campaign” during the Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima & Niigata Season.

 

[Campaign Overview]

 

Implementation Period:
Wednesday, July 23, 2025, 18:30 – Monday, September 15, 2025, 19:00

 

Details:
During the campaign period, if a current PicTrée player introduces a friend or family member who has not yet played PicTrée, and the referred person installs the app and photographs 50 or more poles in the Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima & Niigata season, both the referrer and the referred person will receive reward coins worth 500 yen.

 

Conditions:
Referrer: Must have photographed at least 1 Telephone Pole in the Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima & Niigata Season
Referred Person: Must photograph 50 or more Telephone Poles in the same season

 

How to Participate:
After meeting the conditions, please submit the player names and player IDs of both the referrer and referred person using the form below.
※ Either party may submit the form (no need for both to apply).

 

<<Application Form>> https://forms.gle/n6b4WHfzP6CRLpGY8

 

Rewards:
After confirming that the conditions have been met, reward coins equivalent to 500 yen will be granted to both the referrer and the referred person.
Rewards are scheduled to be distributed in the following two installments.

 

<Scheduled Reward Distribution Dates>
・1st: Scheduled for Mid-August
・2nd: Scheduled for Mid-September

 

Notes:
・The referrer does not need to install the app during the campaign period. As long as the referred person installs the app and completes the required number of pole photographs during the period, the condition is met.
・A referrer may refer multiple people, up to a maximum of 20.
・Only users who install the app after the referrer will be considered eligible as referred persons.
・A referred person can only receive the referral reward once. If multiple applications are submitted, only the earliest valid submission will be accepted.
・If any fraudulent activity is suspected, rewards will not be granted.
・This campaign may be changed or canceled without prior notice.

 

We look forward to your participation.
