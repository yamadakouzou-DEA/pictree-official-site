High-Value Treasure Pole in “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025”

In “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025,” which began across all of Hokkaido on Saturday, July 12, 2025, a high-value treasure pole, the first of its kind in PicTrée history, will appear starting Friday, July 25.

 

◼️ What is a High-Value Treasure Pole?
A high-value treasure pole is a special pole funded by corporations or individuals who wish to support their local area. The funds are returned to players as special rewards.

 

Hidden among the poles designated for photography in the “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025,” these poles grant rewards to those who find and photograph them. The first of these treasure poles will appear somewhere in Hakodate City, and photographing it will earn the player 100,000 yen worth of DEAPcoin (DEP) cryptocurrency.

 

This initiative was made possible through sponsorship by Naoto Yoshida, CEO of DEA, who wished to give back to his hometown of Hakodate. As the “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025” unfolds across all of Hokkaido, this special campaign aims to first energize Yoshida’s home region.

 

◼️ Details
・Deployment Date:
Friday, July 25, 2025, at 9:00 AM
Note: Once the designated pole is photographed, the first high-value treasure pole event will end. An official announcement will be made upon its completion.

 

・Location:
Within Hakodate City, Hokkaido

 

・Overview:
The high-value treasure pole is a special pole that can be discovered and photographed within PicTrée to earn significantly higher rewards than standard poles. When photographed, a “Rainbow Treasure Chest” will appear, and from that chest, players can obtain a reward voucher worth 100,000 yen in DEAPcoin (DEP) (for this first round).
