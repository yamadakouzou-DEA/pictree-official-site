“PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025” – Gotoken Curry Poles Arrive!

On Saturday, July 12, 2025, “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025” kicked off across all of Hokkaido.
Starting Saturday, August 16, the second installment of “Treasure Poles” sponsored by local businesses will appear: the Gotoken Curry Poles.

 

◼️About the “Gotoken Curry Poles”
As the second wave of treasure poles offering prizes through local business sponsorship, five special poles will appear, each awarding a set of 12 boxes of “Gotoken Curry” from Gotoken, a long-established Western-style restaurant that has grown alongside the history of Hakodate.

 

When you photograph a pole and discover it is a “Gotoken Curry Pole”, you will receive a Gotoken Curry Exchange Voucher.
※ You won’t know which poles are “Gotoken Curry Poles” until you photograph them.
By using the voucher and submitting your delivery address, the Gotoken Curry will be shipped directly to your home.
Keep snapping photos to find the “Gotoken Curry Poles”!

 

・Installation date:
Saturday, August 16, 2025 – 9:00 AM
※ Once all the target poles have been photographed, the second wave of treasure poles will end. An official announcement will be made when this occurs.

 

・Number of poles: 5 poles (each awards a voucher for 12 boxes of Gotoken Curry)

 

・Location:
Within Hakodate City, Hokkaido

 


(Image for illustration purposes only)

 

■What is Gotoken Curry?

 

Founded in 1879 in Hakodate, Gotoken is a Western-style restaurant that blends tradition and innovation.
Its curry, rooted in Russian cuisine and adapted to suit the Japanese palate, has become its signature dish. At the historic Gotoken Main Building, guests can enjoy authentic Western cuisine and the traditional curry in an atmosphere that preserves the elegance of the past.
A wide range of ready-to-eat products is also available nationwide, allowing you to enjoy the taste of Gotoken in your own home.
