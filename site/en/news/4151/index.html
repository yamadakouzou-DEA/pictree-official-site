Important Notice Regarding the Photo Shooting of “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025”

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

In the ongoing event “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025,” we have confirmed several cases where the designated photo shooting rules were not properly followed.

To ensure fair play for all participants and to maintain the quality of the information collected, we kindly ask once again for your strict adherence to the official photo shooting methods.

Prohibited Photo Shooting Methods

  • Shooting from non-designated angles: Taking photos in ways that differ from the camera angles specified in the game.

  • Reusing the same angle: Submitting all photos from the exact same angle repeatedly.

  • Shooting from long distances: Taking photos without approaching the utility pole, from a far-off position.

  • Shooting in inappropriate environments: For example, taking photos from inside a vehicle.

    (Reflections from glass or excessive distance from the pole may reduce photo quality.)

Examples of prohibited photos are shown below.

 

If the operations team determines that these rules have not been observed, we may take measures in accordance with the Terms of Use, including invalidating earned points or temporarily suspending accounts.

For details on the correct shooting methods, please refer to the in-game photo screen and the help page.

We sincerely appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

 

PicTrée Operations Team
