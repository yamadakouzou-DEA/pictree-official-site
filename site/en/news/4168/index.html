Notes on Photographing Scaffold Bolt Sockets in the Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima, and Niigata Season

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
In the ongoing Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima, and Niigata Season, we have observed frequent mistakes in distinguishing the designated photo target, the scaffold bolt socket.

 

Please review this notice carefully and cooperate in ensuring accurate photo submissions.

 

Correct Photo Target

Scaffold Bolt Socket
•A hole approximately 2 cm in diameter, sometimes covered with a white or black cap.

 

Incorrect Photo Target

Pole Center Mark
•Also a hole of about 2 cm in diameter, but the hole itself is red.
•Note: The “scaffold bolt socket” and the “red pole center mark” are located about 90 degrees apart on the pole.

 

How to Distinguish
Color:
•Scaffold bolt sockets appear in gray, white, or black (if a cap is present).
•They are never red.

 

Because the two look very similar, please pay close attention to the color of the hole and avoid photographing red ones.
