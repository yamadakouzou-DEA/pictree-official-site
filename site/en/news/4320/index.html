“PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – 2025 Summer” × Shoes Iwai, Sapporo’s 140-Year-Old Legacy Shoe Store — Special Collaboration!

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
In the ongoing “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025,” we are launching a special collaboration with Iwai Shinroku Shoten (Shoes Iwai), a long-established shoe store in Sapporo.
Starting Friday, September 12, 2025, the joint project “Pole to Sole Campaign” will begin — a chance to walk, take photos, enjoy the city, and discover your “next pair” of shoes.

The campaign consists of two parts: Point-back for Shoe Purchases, and PicTrée Special Event “Iwai Shinroku Cup.”

 

【Pole to Sole Campaign: “Walk, Snap, and Find Great Shoes!” Overview】

 

◼ Point-Back for Shoe Purchases

Customers who purchase shoes at participating stores will receive 5% of the pre-tax purchase amount back in PicTrée Reward Coins (maximum ¥500 per transaction).

 

● Period:

September 12, 2025 (Fri) – October 13, 2025 (Mon, Holiday)

 

● Participating Stores:

・Shoes Shop IWAI Chikagai Store
(https://www.sapporo-chikagai.jp/shop/single/101)
・Hush Puppies Chikagai Store
(https://www.sapporo-chikagai.jp/shop/single/173)
・Shoes Shop IWAI / Hush Puppies Catepri Shin-Sapporo Store
(https://www.sunpi-duo.com/shop/1679/)

 

● Eligible items:

Shoes (footwear) only (※ Socks, care items, and repairs are excluded).

 

● Details:

5% of the pre-tax purchase amount will be returned in PicTrée Reward Coins (maximum ¥500 per transaction).

 

● Application Method:

① Purchase footwear at a participating store. At checkout, tell the staff “This is for PicTrée,” and you will receive a receipt stamped with the store seal.
② Enter the required information in the application form and attach an image of the receipt.
※ You will need your PicTrée “Player ID” to apply. Please make sure you have downloaded the PicTrée app before submitting.

https://forms.gle/7GfaXDFdsZ1zYsScA

③ After confirming the application, the operations team will grant the Reward Coins in PicTrée.
※ Rewards will be distributed after the campaign ends, by the end of October.

 

● Notes:

・Applications with receipts that do not have the official seal will be invalid.
・If a purchase is returned or canceled, the Reward Coins will be revoked.

 

◼ PicTrée Special Event “Iwai Shinroku Cup”

During the campaign, in the “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido Summer 2025,” players who capture as many unphotographed utility poles as possible and rank in the Top 3 will win a pair of shoes.

 

● Period:

September 12, 2025 (Fri) 7:00 – September 28, 2025 (Sun) 18:00

 

● Prizes:

Product: [Men’s] Arnold Palmer Slide Fit / [Women’s] Pure Walker Slide Fit
※ Size can be selected.

 

● Details:

During the campaign period, players compete by photographing unphotographed utility poles (those eligible for the first-time shooting reward) in the “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido Summer 2025.”
The Top 3 players in the ranking will each receive a pair of shoes.

 

● How to Participate:

① Fill in the required information on the entry form.
https://forms.gle/y9zuX5oKoSC3vV8r9

② During the campaign, photograph as many unphotographed utility poles as possible in the “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido Summer 2025” to compete in the rankings.
※ Ranking updates will be announced via in-app notifications and the official X account.

③ If you are in the Top 3 at the end of the campaign, you will win a pair of shoes.
※ Prizes can be collected in-store (delivery is also available for distant participants).
