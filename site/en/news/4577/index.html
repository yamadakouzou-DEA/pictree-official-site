“Laid-Back Camp SEASON 3” Collaboration! Announcement & Season Details for “My Electric Pole Battle in Nagano, Yamanashi, and Saitama”

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
Starting Saturday, October 11, 2025, the new season “My Electric Pole Battle in Nagano, Yamanashi, and Saitama” will be held in Nagano, Yamanashi, and Saitama Prefectures. In this season, we are pleased to announce a special collaboration with Furyu Pictures Co., Ltd. and the popular anime “Laid-Back Camp SEASON 3.”
This article provides details of the “My Electric Pole Battle in Nagano, Yamanashi, and Saitama” season and the collaboration with “Laid-Back Camp SEASON 3.”

 

1. Season Details

【Season Duration and Playable Time】

◼️ Season Duration

October 11, 2025 (Sat) 9:00 – November 30, 2025 (Sun) 16:30

 

◼️ Playable Time

● Photography:
October: 6:00–17:00
November: 6:45–16:30

● Check-in:
No restrictions
※ On the first day of the season, gameplay begins at 9:00

 

【Participation Format】

At the start of the season, you choose one of three teams (“Nadeshiko & Ayano,” “Rin & Ena,” “Chiaki & Aoi”). You will belong to that team and compete against the other teams in score rankings.

2. Collaboration with “Laid-Back Camp SEASON 3”

① Original Teams

Characters appearing in “Laid-Back Camp SEASON 3” are divided into three teams: “Nadeshiko & Ayano,” “Rin & Ena,” and “Chiaki & Aoi.” Players select a team and participate in the game as a member of that team.

 

② Season-Limited Skin

PicTrée’s in-game UI will feature a special design themed after “Laid-Back Camp SEASON 3.”

 

③ Limited Items

Multiple collaboration-limited items featuring “Laid-Back Camp SEASON 3” will be available.

 

④ Original Treasure Chests

When checking in a photo asset, special treasure chests themed on “Laid-Back Camp Δ SEASON 3” may appear with a certain probability.
Season-limited collaboration items can be obtained from these chests.

 

⑤ Special Telephone Poles

“Special Telephone Poles” in character colors will be placed at real-world locations around each area and will appear together with the characters.
Special Telephone Poles differ from regular ones in the following ways:
・Asset HP: 5,000
・Connection Capacity in Pole Connect: Up to 5 connections
・Treasure Chest Drop Rate and Item Drop Rate

 

⑥ Special Cut-Ins

“Special cut-in animations” featuring characters will play when you perform a Utility Pole Connect action.

 

⑦ Collaboration-Limited Postcards

“Collaboration-Limited Postcards” will be sold at select Animate stores in each prefecture (Ōmiya / Aeon Mall Kōfu Shōwa / Nagano).
Price: ¥660 (tax included)
