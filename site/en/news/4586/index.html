Announcement & Season Details for “My Electric Pole Battle in Tochigi”

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
Starting Saturday, October 11, 2025, a new season “My Electric Pole Battle in Tochigi” will be held in Tochigi Prefecture.
In this season, the photography targets are mainly selected from areas that were not photographed during the previously held North Kanto Season.
To encourage photographing these unphotographed poles, Tochigi Prefecture will be divided into smaller “areas,” and top players in each area ranking can earn Reward Coins.
Focus on capturing all the unphotographed poles in each area and aim for 1st place in the area rankings!!
