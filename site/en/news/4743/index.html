“Maebashi Witches” Collaboration! Announcement of “My Pole Battle in Maebashi City 2025”

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
Starting Saturday, November 1, 2025, a new season, “My Pole Battle in Maebashi City 2025”, will be held in Maebashi City, Gunma Prefecture.
In this season, we are pleased to announce a special collaboration with Bandai Namco Filmworks, featuring the beloved anime “Maebashi Witches”, cherished by many Gunma residents and beyond.

 

【Season Overview】

◼️ Period

November 1 (Sat), 2025, 9:00 – November 30 (Sun), 16:00

 

◼️ Available Play Time

9:00–16:00

 

◼️ Target

Poles (Please check shooting angles in the game)

 

◼️ Target Area

Maebashi City, Gunma Prefecture (Approx. 55,000 Poles)

 

◼️ Participation

At the beginning of the season, players choose one of three teams: “Yuina & Keroppe,” “Azu & Mai,” or “Choco & Kyouka.” Players then compete in scores against other teams. At the end of the season, the winning team and top-ranked players will receive special ranking rewards in addition to the regular photo rewards per Pole. (Ranking rewards will be announced later.)

 

◼️ Rewards

・For the first-time photo of each Pole: reward coins equivalent to 20 JPY
・For photographing a Pole (Vine-Prone): reward coins equivalent to 100 JPY (Vine Discovery Reward)

 

* Reward coins can be exchanged in-game for Amazon Gift Cards, the cryptocurrency DEAPcoin (DEP), or exclusive collaboration postcards with “Maebashi Witches.”
* The “Vine Discovery Reward” is granted when a photo of a Pole (Vine-Prone) is submitted and approved by operations.
* Details may be subject to change. Thank you for your understanding.

 

Further details will be announced later.

 

【Collaboration with “Maebashi Witches”】

In this season, the team selection will feature special collaboration designs, along with the appearance of special Poles and unique Pole Connect effects. Many collaboration elements with “Maebashi Witches” will appear in the game.
