Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season × Freshly Boiled Pasta “Popolamama” Collaboration!

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
Starting Saturday, October 11, 2025, in ”Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season,” we will collaborate with Popolamama Co., Ltd., which offers authentic freshly boiled pasta at a reasonable price, to launch three campaigns.

 

[Campaign ①] Get “100 yen OFF” + “200 yen Coupon” by downloading the PicTrée app

Show the PicTrée app screen to the staff at the participating stores below to receive 100 yen off your bill that day, plus a 200 yen coupon for use on your next visit.

 

◼️Start Date:

From Saturday, October 11

 

◼️Participating Stores:

① First 50 visitors: Popolamama Kawagoe Crea Mall (Nagamine Bldg. 1F, 11-4 Wakita-cho, Kawagoe)
② First 50 visitors: Popolamama Kawagoe West Exit (Unicus Kawagoe 1F, 1-17-1 Shinjuku-cho, Kawagoe)
③ First 100 visitors: Popolamama Peony Walk Higashimatsuyama (4-3 Azuma-cho, Higashimatsuyama)
④ First 100 visitors: Popolamama Shin-Tokorozawa (1-1-11 Midori-cho, Tokorozawa)
⑤ First 100 visitors: Popolamama Ario Washinomiya (Ario Washinomiya 1F, 7-1 Hisamotoji Yada, Kuki)
⑥ First 100 visitors: Wa Pasta Popolamama Frespo Yashio (Frespo Yashio 2F, 1-1-3 Ose, Yashio)

 

[Campaign ②] Capture the “Popolamama Pole” to receive a Pasta Set

As a sponsored treasure Pole, the “Popolamama Pole” will appear. By finding and photographing the “Popolamama Pole,” you can win a pasta set.

 

◼️Installation Date:

From 7:00 AM, Saturday, October 11, 2025

 

◼️Installation Locations:

① Within 3 km of Popolamama Kawagoe Crea Mall
② Within 3 km of Popolamama Frespo Yashio
③ Within 3 km of Popolamama Peony Walk Higashimatsuyama
④ Within 3 km of Popolamama Shin-Tokorozawa
⑤ Within 3 km of Popolamama Ario Washinomiya

 

◼️Details:

Some Poles within the event area will be designated as “Popolamama Poles.” When you capture them, a rainbow treasure chest will appear, from which you will receive a prize voucher. After obtaining the voucher, submit your claim to have the prize shipped to you.

 

◼️Prize:

“Popolamama” Pasta Set × 5 packs

 

 

[Campaign ③] Official X Follow & Repost Campaign

To commemorate the launch of the “Popolamama Pole,” we will run an Official X Follow & Repost Campaign. Follow the official Popolamama X account (@popolamama) and the official PicTrée X account (@pictree_dea), and repost the campaign post (quote reposts also eligible) to enter a lottery for prizes.

 

◼️Prizes:

● Popolamama Pasta Set × 2 winners
● PicTrée Original Mobile Battery × 1 winner
● Amazon Gift Card 1,000 yen × 2 winners

 

◼️How to Apply:

1. Follow the official Popolamama X account (@popolamama) and the official PicTrée X account (@pictree_dea)
2. Repost the designated campaign post (quote reposts also accepted)

 

◼️Campaign Schedule:

● Start: Monday, October 6
● Deadline: Thursday, October 16, 23:59
● Winner Announcement: From Friday, October 24 via DM from the official PicTrée X account

 

*Campaign details may change or be canceled without prior notice.

 

[Sponsorship for Treasure Poles Wanted]

PicTrée is currently seeking companies (and individuals) to sponsor events through product offerings and other contributions.

 

◼️What is a Treasure Pole?

A Treasure Pole is a special Pole sponsored by companies or individuals to support their local community. The sponsorship funds or products are returned as rewards to participants. Treasure Poles are hidden among the many Poles available in PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido – Summer 2025, and participants can obtain rewards by discovering and capturing them.
