“Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season” “Tochigi Season” – Friend Referral Campaign

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

From October 11 to November 30, 2025, we will be holding a “Friend Referral Campaign” for “Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season” and “Tochigi Season”.

 

[Campaign Overview]

In “Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season” and “Tochigi Season”, when you refer a friend or family member who hasn’t played PicTrée yet, and the referred person installs the app, and both the referrer and referee photograph a certain number of utility poles, both parties will receive reward coins based on their photography achievements at the end of the season.

 

■ Campaign Period:

“Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season”

October 11, 2025 (Sat) 9:00 AM(JST) – November 30, 2025 (Sun) 4:30 PM(JST)

“Tochigi Season”

October 11, 2025 (Sat) 6:00 AM(JST) – November 30, 2025 (Sun) 4:15 PM(JST)

 

■ How to Participate:

Please submit both the referrer’s and referee’s player names and player IDs using the form below.

Note: Either the referrer or referee may submit the application (applications from both parties are not required).

 

<<Form>>

 

■ Requirements:

・ The referee must have installed the PicTrée app after the referrer

・ Both the referrer and referee must photograph at least 10 utility poles during the target season

■ Rewards:

Based on photography achievements at the end of the season, the following reward coins will be granted to both the referrer and referee.

 

When both the referrer and referee have photographed:

① 10-29 poles: 1,200 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥100)

② 30-49 poles: 3,600 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥300)

③ 50+ poles: 6,000 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥500)

 

*The number of photographed poles will be counted based on the number of photos that have been approved for review.

*If the number of photographed poles differs between referrer and referee, the lower tier reward will be applied.

(Example) Referrer photographs 50+ poles (③), Referee photographs 10-20 poles (①) ⇒ Both referrer and referee will receive 1,200 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥100).

 

<Reward Distribution Date>

Scheduled for early December

 

■ Important Notes:

・ The referrer does not need to install the app during the campaign period. The requirements are met as long as the referee installs the app during the campaign period and both parties photograph at least 10 poles.

・ The referee must be a user who installed the PicTrée app after the referrer (users who have already installed the app before the referrer are not eligible).

・ Referees can only receive rewards from this campaign once (if multiple referrers submit applications for the same referee, only the first application will be considered valid).

・ Rewards will not be granted if fraud is suspected.

・ This campaign may be changed or cancelled without notice.
