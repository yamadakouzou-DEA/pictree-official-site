“Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season” “Tochigi Season” – Review Award Campaign

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

From October 11 to November 30, 2025, we will run a “Review Award Campaign” in “Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season” and “Tochigi Season”.

 

Campaign overview

Photos you take for “My Power Pole Battle in Nagano/Yamanashi/Saitama” and “My Power Pole Battle in Tochigi” will be processed by NTT-ME to read information from the utility poles.

 

At review time, the most important points we check are whether the photo is taken from an appropriate angle and whether the target subject is in focus. These factors impact reward eligibility and overall season outcomes.

 

Based on these points, we will announce exemplary photos from participating users every week. After the season ends, the operations team will select three Review Award winners from among those exemplary photos. Each winner will receive 60,000 reward coins (equivalent to 5,000 JPY).

 

Period

Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season

October 11, 2025 (Sat) 9:00 JST – November 30, 2025 (Sun) 16:30 JST

 

Tochigi Season

October 11, 2025 (Sat) 6:00 JST – November 30, 2025 (Sun) 16:15 JST

 

How to participate

In the target seasons, take photos of the designated utility poles.

Please refer to the photo tips below and try to capture the subject as clearly and legibly as possible.
