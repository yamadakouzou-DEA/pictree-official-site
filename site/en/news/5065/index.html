New Reward Coin Redemption Options: “Eraberu Pay” and “giftee Box®”

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, November 1, 2025, we will add two new redemption options for reward coins you earn in PicTrée: “Eraberu Pay” and “giftee Box®,” both provided by giftee Inc.

 

【New redemption options】

① Eraberu Pay

● Overview

Eraberu Pay is a digital gift by giftee Inc. You can redeem gifted “gift points” for your preferred items from a variety of supported smartphone payment services. No account registration or app download is required to use Eraberu Pay or gift points. You can also choose multiple payment services within your available points.

● Key features

Choose later, Fully completed on your smartphone, Easy to use from small amounts.

● Gift catalog examples

QUO Card Pay, Ponta Points, V Money, nanaco Gift, FamiPay, giftee PreMo Plus, Visa e-Gift, au PAY Gift Card, G-POINT, WAON Point ID, Kyash (Visa prepaid card) gift code, V-POINT,” Edy Gift ID, WAON POINT e-Gift, JAL Pay Points, and many more.

 

For details about Eraberu Pay, see the following page:

https://giftee-user.zendesk.com/hc/ja/articles/15780350956313

 

② giftee Box®

● Overview

giftee Box® is a digital gift by giftee Inc. You can redeem gifted “gift points” for your preferred items from a rich lineup. No account registration or app download is required to use giftee Box® or gift points. You can also choose multiple gifts within your available points.

 

● Key features

Wide selection of brands and items, Strong gift experience and perceived value.

 

● Gift catalog examples

Baskin-Robbins, TULLY’S COFFEE, Uber Eats, Demae-can, Skylark Group, Online Book Gift, Coca-Cola, Godiva, PRONTO, Komeda Coffee, Sushiro, QUO Card Pay, Ponta Points, V Money, nanaco Gift, FamiPay, giftee PreMo Plus, Visa e-Gift, d POINT, au PAY Gift Card, WAON Point ID, V-POINT, Edy Gift ID, WAON POINT e-Gift, JAL Pay Points, and many more.

 

For details about giftee Box®, see the following page:

https://giftee-user.zendesk.com/hc/ja/articles/15425566172057

 

【How to redeem reward coins】

①Home → Menu → Open Profile

 

②Tap “Redeem”

 

③From the redemption list, select “Eraberu Pay” or “giftee Box®”

 

④Enter the recipient email address, choose the amount, then tap “Redeem”

 

<Recipient email address>

・ If you have registered an email address in the app, it will be automatically filled in.

・ We cannot resend due to input errors. Please make sure the address is correct.

 

<Redemption rate>

You can convert reward coins earned in PicTrée into “points” usable with Eraberu Pay or giftee Box®. The rate is as follows.

Required reward coins Amount redeemed
1,350 100 points

 

・ You can select in units of 100 points.

・ To redeem the maximum amount based on your current coin balance, tap “Max” to auto-fill the maximum redeemable points.

・ Up to 10,000 points can be redeemed per transaction.

 

⑤Submission complete

A link or code for receipt will be displayed and also sent to your registered email address.
