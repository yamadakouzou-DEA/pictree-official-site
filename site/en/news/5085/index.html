Special Mission: Ageo City Saitama Prefecture “Hirakata Kawamachi Festival” Announcement

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

On Sunday, October 26, 2025 from 10:30 to 13:30 JST only, we will hold a Special Mission: Ageo City, Saitama Prefecture “Hirakata Kawamachi Festival.”

 

There are two missions in total.

Complete a mission by photographing all four checkpoints set for each mission. As a mission reward, you can earn up to 300 yen worth of reward coins.

Also, Ageo City’s mascot character “Appy” is hiding at one of the checkpoints. Please observe which occupation outfit “Appy” is wearing.

Show the mission completion screen at the PicTrée booth at the Hirakata Kawamachi Festival venue and answer the “Appy” outfit quiz to receive a 300 yen discount coupon that can be used only on the festival day.

 

【Special Mission Period】

October 26, 2025 (Sun) 10:30 – 13:30 JST

※ The Hirakata Kawamachi Festival runs from 11:00 – 14:00 JST on the same day

 

【Event Area】

Hirakata area, Ageo City, Saitama Prefecture

 

【Mission Details and Rewards】

◼️Hirakata Kawamachi Festival North Side of Arakawa Course

 

Approach the four designated checkpoints and take photos that match the sample images.

 

● Estimated time:

20–30 minutes on foot

 

● Mission completion reward:

3,600 reward coins (worth 300 yen)

 

● Festival-limited discount coupon:

300 yen discount coupon (valid only on the day)

 

◼️Hirakata Kawamachi Festival South Side of Arakawa Course

 

Approach the four designated checkpoints and take photos that match the sample images.

 

● Estimated time:

10–20 minutes on foot

 

● Mission completion reward:

1,800 reward coins (worth 150 yen)

 

● Festival-limited discount coupon:

300 yen discount coupon (valid only on the day)

 

【How to Join the Special Mission In-Game】

①Tap [Shooting Mission] at the bottom left of the app screen

②Tap [Ageo City “Hirakata Kawamachi Festival” Special Mission]

③Choose either [North Side of Arakawa Course] or [South Side of Arakawa Course]

④Approach a checkpoint and tap to start the mission

 

【Notes】

・A PicTrée booth will be set up at the Hirakata Kawamachi Festival venue. (Near Yae Shrine)

・The festival-limited discount coupon can be obtained by showing the mission completion screen at the PicTrée booth and answering the Appy quiz correctly by naming the outfit. The coupon will be handed out directly at the booth.

 

◼️Overview of “Hirakata Kawamachi Festival”

https://www.city.ageo.lg.jp/page/371369.html

 

【Finally】

To keep PicTrée a better experience, please follow good manners while playing.

Because PicTrée involves taking photos around town, it can be easy to drift into unsafe behavior like looking at your phone while walking. Unsafe behavior or actions that disturb others will make it difficult to continue this positive initiative.

Please pay attention to the following points:

________________________________________

・Do not use your phone while walking. Safety first.

・Do not obstruct vehicles or pedestrians around you.

・Do not park illegally. Follow traffic rules and etiquette.

・Do not enter private property without permission.

・Be careful not to capture private houses, nameplates, car license plates, or faces of passers-by in your photos.

・Avoid loud voices, long stays, and occupying sidewalks in groups.

・Respect the everyday lives of local residents.

________________________________________

Your mindfulness helps create a safe and enjoyable environment for everyone.

With everyone’s correct actions and consideration, we can keep PicTrée fun for a long time to come. Thank you for your continued understanding and cooperation.

 

 

PicTrée Operations Team
