“My Electric Pole Battle in Maebashi 2025” – Friend Referral Campaign

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

We are running a “Referral Campaign” during “My Electric Pole Battle in Maebashi 2025” from November 1 to November 30, 2025.

 

【Campaign Overview】

During “My Electric Pole Battle in Maebashi 2025,” if you refer a friend or family member who has not yet played PicTrée, and the referred person newly installs the app, then both the referrer and the referred person will receive reward coins at the end of the season based on how many utility poles they have photographed, provided both of them photograph at least a certain number of poles.

 

◼️Period：

November 1, 2025 (Sat) 9:00 JST to November 30, 2025 (Sun) 16:00 JST

 

◼️How to participate：

Please submit both the referrer’s and the referred person’s player names and player IDs via the form below.

Either the referrer or the referred person can submit. A single submission is sufficient.

 

<<Application Form>>

 

◼️Conditions：

・The referred person must have installed the PicTrée app after the referrer.

・In the target season, both the referrer and the referred person must photograph 10 or more utility poles.

 

◼️Rewards：

At the end of the season, reward coins will be granted to both the referrer and the referred person according to the numbers below.

 

When both the referrer and referred person have:

①Photographed 10–29 poles: 1,200 reward coins (equivalent to 100 JPY)

② Photographed 30–49 poles: 3,600 reward coins (equivalent to 300 JPY)

③Photographed 50+ poles: 6,000 reward coins (equivalent to 500 JPY)

 

※The count is based on photos that have passed review.

※If the numbers photographed differ between the referrer and referred person, the lower tier applies.

Example: Referrer 50+ (3), Referred 10–20 (1)

→ Both receive 1,200 reward coins (equivalent to 100 JPY).

 

<Distribution date of rewards>

Early December (planned)

 

◼️Notes：

・The referrer does not need to install the app during the campaign period. As long as the referred person installs during the period and both photograph 10 or more poles, the conditions are met.

・Eligible referred users are those who installed the PicTrée app after the referrer. Users who installed it before the referrer are not eligible.

・A user can receive the campaign rewards as a referred person only once. If multiple submissions claim the same referred person, only the earliest submission will be considered valid.

・Rewards will not be granted if fraudulent activity is suspected.

・The campaign may be changed or canceled without prior notice.
