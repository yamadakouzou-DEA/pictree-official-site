“Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Maebashi City 2025” Vines Discovery Ranking

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

We are pleased to announce the Vines Discovery Ranking for “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Maebashi City 2025”.

During the season, players who photographed poles where vines were twining and whose photos were approved by the operations team will receive a Vines Discovery Reward based on the number of such photos.

※The Vines Discovery Reward is granted only for the first photo of each pole. Photos of the same pole taken a second time or later are not eligible.

※Even if you photograph a pole with vines, you will not receive a Vines Discovery Reward if you selected “None” in the vines report before submitting the image, so please take care when reporting.

■ Rewards

■ Counting period

Saturday, November 1, 2025, 9:00 JST ~ Sunday, November 30, 2025, 16:00 JST

■ Reward distribution date

Friday, December 12, 2025

■ Vines Discovery Ranking
