Impact on PicTrée and Future Steps Regarding PlayMining’s Service Transition

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

As previously announced by the official PlayMining team, the PlayMining platform is transitioning to a public chain (a complete shift to Web3). Consequently, PlayMining Wallet functions and NFT handling on the PlayMining platform (PlayMining NFT) are scheduled to be discontinued in stages. This means users will need to transfer their held assets (DEP / NFT) to an external wallet (e.g., MetaMask).

Since this change affects certain features within PicTrée, we are writing to inform you of the details below.

(1) Regarding the DEAPcoin (DEP) Exchange Function

The DEAPcoin (DEP) exchange function within PicTrée will remain available until the deadline below.

Exchange Function Deadline: Tuesday, March 24, 2026, at 18:00

From Tuesday, March 24, 2026, at 18:00: Temporarily Suspended

*Please note that PlayMining plans to stop the DEP withdrawal function at the start of maintenance on Tuesday, March 31, 2026. Once stopped, you will no longer be able to withdraw to external sources or transfer between wallets from the PlayMining Wallet, so please complete your procedures well in advance.

(Reference) Withdrawal Guide: Please refer to the page below for detailed steps

https://fluorescent-link-994.notion.site/How-to-Withdraw-DEAPcoin-2c4bcd33a86a81ea92c1ca29100fd981

(2) Regarding PicTrée “PoleNFT”: Bridging (Migration) Required. NFTs currently managed on PlayMining will need to be migrated (bridged) to Polygon for future management in external wallets.

Accordingly, PicTrée’s PoleNFT will also require bridging.

NFT Migration (Bridge) Deadline: Tuesday, March 31, 2026

After this deadline, migrating (bridging) NFTs from the PlayMining Wallet is scheduled to become unavailable.

(Reference) Detailed NFT Migration Steps: Please refer to the following page.

https://fluorescent-link-994.notion.site/How-to-Bridge-NFTs-2c4bcd33a86a812fa880e411b71af6e2
