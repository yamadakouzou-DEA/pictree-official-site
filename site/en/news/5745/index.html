Announcing the “Convex Mirror Photo Mission in Niigata 2km”!

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

Starting January 9, 2026, we will be holding a special mission called “Convex Mirror Photo Mission in Niigata 2km” in the “Niigata 2km” area of Niigata City, Niigata Prefecture.

Here are the mission details.

【Mission Overview】

◼️Event Period

January 9, 2026 (Fri) 7:15 – January 18, 2026 (Sun) 18:30 JST

◼️Event Area

Niigata 2km (The urban area connecting Niigata Station, Bandai, and Furumachi)

*Please check the link below for more details on “Niigata 2km”.

https://www.city.niigata.lg.jp/shisei/seisaku/jigyoproject/niigata2km/niigata2km.html

◼️Photography Hours

7:15 – 18:30 JST

◼️Target Infrastructure

164 Convex Mirrors

*Please take a total of 5 photos per mirror: “Full View”, “Mirror (Front/Back)”, “Sticker”, and “Base”.

◼️Rewards

360 Reward Coins per mirror (equivalent to 30 yen)

◼️How to Participate

You can display the target infrastructure (convex mirrors) on the map by following these steps:

1. Install the PicTrée app.

2. After registering your email address, tap “Road Infrastructure Mission” from the map screen.

3. Tap “Convex Mirror Photo Mission in Niigata 2km” from the “Photo Mission List”.

4. Tap “Challenge”.

◼️Important Notes

・Please refrain from taking photos in areas with heavy traffic as it is dangerous.

・Be careful not to include other people in your photos.

・Entering private property without permission is prohibited. Please be careful.

・Please be considerate of the people and property around you when taking photos.

・If you feel it is unsafe or difficult to take a photo, please do not force yourself and refrain from shooting.

・In case of severe weather such as strong winds, heavy rain, or heavy snow, the app may undergo maintenance and the mission may be suspended. Thank you for your understanding.

【Survey】

We are conducting a survey for those who participated in the mission.

By answering, you can earn Reward Coins (equivalent to 300 yen), so please give us your feedback.

▼ Click here to answer the survey ▼

https://customform.jp/form/input/234833

◼️Survey Period

January 9, 2026 (Fri) 7:15 – January 25, 2026 (Sun) 23:59 JST

*The survey may close early depending on circumstances.

◼️Eligibility

Those who participated in the “Convex Mirror Photo Mission in Niigata 2km” and took at least one photo.

◼️Survey Rewards

3,600 Reward Coins (equivalent to 300 yen) for all respondents.

◼️Reward Distribution Date

Early February 2026

◼️Important Notes

・Those who have not taken any photos are not eligible for survey rewards.

・Please note that even if the same respondent (same Player ID) answers multiple times, only one entry will be valid.

・Please note that responses determined to be clearly fraudulent will not be eligible for rewards.
