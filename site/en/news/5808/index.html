“Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa” Season Details

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
Starting February 1, 2026 (Sunday), we will be holding a new season, “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa,” in Okinawa Prefecture, in collaboration with Okinawa Electric Power Company, Inc., NTT WEST Okinawa Branch, and NTT Field Techno Corporation.
This article provides details about the season.

【Season Period and Gameplay Hours】

◼️Season Period

7:00 JST, February 1, 2026 (Sunday) – 18:00 JST, March 22, 2026 (Sunday)

◼️Gameplay Hours

・Photo Capture: 7:00 JST – 18:00 JST
・Check-in: 7:00 JST – 18:00 JST

【About Team Selection】

In PicTrée, you select one team to join when participating in a season.
Choose from “Manta Riders,” “Tiida Force,” or “Gusuku Warriors” and compete with other teams for scores as a member of that team.
・The color of the pole when you capture it by photographing will change based on the color of your chosen team.
・Once you select a team, you cannot change it during the season, so choose carefull

【Request for Reporting When Photo Capture is Difficult】

If there is any problem with the photo target, or if photography is difficult due to the surrounding environment or the condition of the photo target, please report it using the reporting function instead of forcing yourself to take photos.

Please refer to the following for how to report.

◼️How to Report

① Tap the target asset icon

② Tap the megaphone icon in the upper right

③ Check the problems occurring with the photo target and tap “Next”

④ If you have any helpful photos, photograph them using the “Capture” button and tap “Submit

【Event Areas and Area Ranking Rewards】

This season’s event region covers Okinawa Main Island and the remote islands.

◼️Target Regions

Okinawa Main Island: Itoman City, Ogimi Village, Kunigami Village, Tomigusuku City, Nago City, Naha City, Higashi Village
Remote Islands: Aguni Island, Ie Island, Ishigaki Island, Izena Island, Iheya Island, Kitadaito Island, Kume Island, Zamami Island, Taketomi Island, Tokashiki Island, Tonaki Island, Minamidaito Island

Additionally, the target region is divided into 13 areas, and Area Ranking Rewards will be awarded based on your ranking.

◼️Event Areas (13 Areas)

Southern, Northern, Ishigaki, Aguni Village, Ie Village, Izena Village, Iheya Village, Kumejima Town, Zamami Village, Tokashiki Village, Tonaki Village, Minamidaito Village, Kitadaito Village
