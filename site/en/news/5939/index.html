Announcement: “Naha City Tourism Mission,” “Kokusai Street Shopping District Mission,” and “Island Tour Mission”

Starting February 1, 2026, in conjunction with “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa,” we will hold three special missions: the “Naha City Tourism Mission,” the “Shopping District Mission,” and the “Island Tour Mission.”

In these missions, you can earn rewards by completing missions that involve photographing designated targets for each mission.

Please explore various areas of Okinawa through these missions and discover the charm of the region.

①【Naha City Tourism Mission】

A mission to tour tourist sites in Naha City.

◼️Event Period

Sunday, February 1, 2026, 7:00 AM – Sunday, March 22, 6:00 PM

◼️Photography Hours

7:00 AM – 6:00 PM

◼️Photography Targets

・Shikinaen Garden

・Fukushuen Garden

・Saion Ufu Shisa (Makishi)

・Shureimon Gate

・Makishi Public Market

・Naha Maguro Market

・Gajanbira Park

・Shuri Kinjo-cho Stone-Paved Road

・Naminoue Beach

・Cellular Stadium Naha

◼️Photography Rewards

Complete photography of all 10 spots to earn 6,000 reward coins (approximately ¥500 equivalent)

②【Kokusai Street Shopping District Mission A・B・C】

A mission to photograph utility poles in shopping districts around Kokusai Street.

There are three types of missions, one for each shopping district.

◼️Event Period

Sunday, February 1, 2026, 7:00 AM – Sunday, March 22, 6:00 PM

◼️Photography Hours

7:00 AM – 6:00 PM

◼️Photography Targets

Utility poles (detailed locations can be confirmed in the app)

◼️Photography Rewards

For each mission, photograph all target poles to earn 2,400 reward coins (approximately ¥200 equivalent)

③【Island Tour Mission】

A mission to tour the remote islands of Okinawa and photograph designated spots on each island.

Missions are divided by island, with a total of 10 missions.

◼️Event Period

Sunday, February 1, 2026, 7:00 AM – Sunday, March 22, 6:00 PM

◼️Photography Hours

7:00 AM – 6:00 PM

◼️Photography Targets

・Island Tour Mission (Aguni Island): Mahana Observatory, Hora-ji Temple

・Island Tour Mission (Ie Island): Mt. Gusuku (Tacchu), Nyatiya Cave

・Island Tour Mission (Izena Island): Izena Castle Ruins, Shoen-o Oniwa Park

・Island Tour Mission (Iheya Island):念頭平松 of Iheya Island, Kumaya Cave

・Island Tour Mission (Kita-Daito Island): Phosphate Mine Ruins, Okinawa’s Easternmost Monument

・Island Tour Mission (Kumejima): Hiyajo Banta, Tatami-ishi

・Island Tour Mission (Zamami Island): Furuzamami Beach, Takatsukiyama First Observatory

・Island Tour Mission (Tokashiki Island): Aharen Beach, Teruyama Observatory

・Island Tour Mission (Tonaki Island): Fukugi Tree-Lined Street, Historical Folk Museum

・Island Tour Mission (Minami-Daito Island): Hoshino Cave, Minami-Daito Fishing Port

◼️Photography Rewards

For each mission, photograph all target spots to earn 6,000 reward coins (approximately ¥500 equivalent)

【How to Participate in Each Mission】

You can display the photography targets for each mission on the map by following these steps:

1. Install the PicTrée app

2. After registering your email address, tap “Photography Mission” at the bottom left of the map screen

3. From the photography mission list screen, tap “Tourism Mission” or “Kokusai Street Shopping District Mission”

※ The “Naha City Tourism Mission” and “Island Tour Mission” are included in “Tourism Mission.”

4. Tap “Challenge” to start the mission
