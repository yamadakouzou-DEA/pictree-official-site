Mozuku Chips Poles and Shinpoi-san Poles Debut in “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa!”

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Starting Sunday, February 1, 2026, in the new season “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa” in Okinawa Prefecture, Mozuku Chips Poles through a collaboration with Hantakawa Foods and Shinpoi-san Poles through a collaboration with influencer しんぽい@お得オタク will appear.

At the same time, recovery items in collaboration with Hantakawa Foods will also appear.

【Overview】

Hantakawa Foods is a food manufacturer based in Naha City, Okinawa Prefecture, that produces processed foods using Okinawa-grown mozuku seaweed.

They create products that bring out the natural flavor of ingredients, such as “Mozuku Chips.”

This time, “Mozuku Chips Poles” where you can obtain Hantakawa Foods’ “Mozuku Chips” will appear, and the original recovery item “Mozuku Chips” will be added.

しんぽい@お得オタク (@Shinpoi_OTOKU) is an influencer who shares information about “profitable living” including point activities, cashless payments, saving tips, and investments. With a track record of accumulating 16 million points annually, they are also known as the “Emperor of Point Activities.” This time, “Shinpoi-san Poles” where you can obtain up to 10,000 yen worth of reward coins will appear.

◼️Installation Date:

From Sunday, February 1, 2026, 7:00 AM ~

◼️Installation Location:

Will appear randomly in each area within the target region.

◼️Details:

①”Mozuku Chips Poles” and “Shinpoi-san Poles” will be installed within the target area. When you photograph the corresponding poles, a rainbow treasure box will appear, and you can obtain the respective prizes from that box.

(Prizes)

Mozuku Chips Poles: Mozuku Chips 1 carton ×4 poles

Shinpoi-san Poles:

120,000 reward coins (worth 10,000 yen) ×1 pole

96,000 reward coins (worth 8,000 yen) ×1 pole

60,000 reward coins (worth 5,000 yen) ×2 poles

24,000 reward coins (worth 2,000 yen) ×10 poles

12,000 reward coins (worth 1,000 yen) ×18 poles

6,000 reward coins (worth 500 yen) ×68 poles

②Original recovery item “Mozuku Chips” in collaboration with Hantakawa Foods will appear from treasure boxes.

◼️What are Treasure Poles

Treasure Poles are special poles where companies or individuals contribute funds or goods to support their local area, and rewards are returned to participants using those funds. Treasure Poles are hidden among the poles that are photography targets in “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa,” and participants can obtain rewards by finding and photographing Treasure Poles among the many poles.
