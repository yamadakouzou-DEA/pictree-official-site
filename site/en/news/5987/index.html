“Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Tokyo, Kanagawa & Chiba” Review Awards Campaign

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

From Saturday, January 31, 2026, to Sunday, March 8, 2026, we will be holding the “Review Awards Campaign” for “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Tokyo, Kanagawa & Chiba.”

【Campaign Overview】

The photos you take in “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Tokyo, Kanagawa & Chiba” will be reviewed by NTT-ME to read the information on the telegraph poles.

During the review, we check whether the photos are taken at an appropriate angle and whether the target subject is in focus as the most important points when confirming the photos, which are important points related to the payment of shooting rewards and the success or failure of the season.

In “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Tokyo, Kanagawa & Chiba,” we will announce users who have taken excellent photos based on the above points every week. In addition, after the season ends, the operation team will select 3 Review Awards winners from among them, and the 3 winners will receive 60,000 reward coins (equivalent to 5,000 yen).

◼️Implementation Period

“Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Tokyo, Kanagawa & Chiba”

Saturday, January 31, 2026, 7:00 ~ Sunday, March 8, 2026, 17:00 (UTC+9)

◼️How to Participate

Take photos of telegraph poles in the target season.

◼️Key Points for Photographing “Nameplates”

We will share key points for photographing “nameplates,” which were often rejected in previous seasons.

Please refer to this and take photos of the target as clearly and legibly as possible.

<Nameplate> Ideal Photography Example

・Please focus firmly on the nameplate

・Please shoot in the highest possible quality

・Please shoot from the front at an appropriate angle

Nameplate photography is in a format where you select whether or not you can shoot.

If there is a problem with the nameplate, or if you cannot shoot due to the surrounding environment or the condition of the nameplate, select the closest reason from the options and take a photo that shows you cannot shoot.

●Examples where nameplates cannot be photographed

If the nameplate is at a high location and it is difficult to take an ideal photo as in the following example, please select “Cannot shoot because the nameplate is at a high location.”

◼️Rewards

60,000 reward coins (equivalent to 5,000 yen) × 3 Review Awards winners

<Reward Distribution Date>

Scheduled for mid-March

◼️Notes

・ Review Awards winners will be announced after the season ends.

・ If fraud is suspected, rewards will not be granted.

・ This campaign may be changed or canceled without notice.

PicTrée Operation Team
