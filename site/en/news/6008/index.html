Notice: Distribution of Armbands and Survey Cards for “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa”

This is a notice for everyone planning to play “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa”, which will be held in Okinawa Prefecture starting February 1, 2026.

To help you enjoy playing PicTrée more safely, we will be giving away armbands and survey cards. (*Both items are available in limited quantities.)

If you would like to receive them, please use one of the following methods:

(1) Direct pickup from PicTrée staff in Naha City:

◼️ Pickup Period

February 4 (Wed), 2026, around 10:00 (UTC+8) – February 6 (Fri), 2026, around 19:00 (UTC+8)

◼️ Pickup Location

Scheduled near Asahibashi Station – Kenchomae Station.

*Detailed pickup locations will be provided individually.

◼️ Pickup Method

Please send a DM to the official PicTrée X account (https://x.com/pictree_dea) or email the PicTrée inquiry address

(pictree_cs@dea.sg) with the following information:

Subject: [PicTrée Okinawa Season] Regarding Armband and Survey Card Pickup

・Nickname (PicTrée username)

・Preferred pickup date and time

・Number of armbands and survey cards requested

（2）Pickup at Permanent Locations

Armbands and survey cards are available at the following 10 remote island village offices.

Please feel free to use them if you are nearby.

・Aguni Village Office (483 Higashi, Aguni Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 901-3792)

・Ie Village Office (38 Ejimae, Ie Village, Kunigami District, Okinawa Prefecture 905-0592)

・Izena Village Office (1687-22 Nakada, Izena Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 905-0695)

・Iheya Village Office (251 Gakiya, Iheya Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 905-0793)

・Kita-Daito Village Office (218 Nakano, Kita-Daito Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 901-3902)

・Kumejima Town Office (2870 Higa, Kumejima Town, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 901-3193)

・Zamami Village Office (109 Zamami, Zamami Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 901-3496)

・Tokashiki Village Office (183 Tokashiki, Tokashiki Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 901-3592)

・Tonaki Village Office (1917-3 Tonaki Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 901-3692)

・Minami-Daito Village Office (144-1 Minami, Minami-Daito Village, Shimajiri District, Okinawa Prefecture 901-3895)

*The quantities available at each village office are limited.

*Pickup hours follow each village office’s business hours.

We plan to add more locations in the future.

New locations will be announced on PicTrée official SNS.

（3）Mail Delivery:

If it is difficult to receive items through the methods above, mail delivery is also available.

If you would like to receive items by mail, please send the following information via DM to the official PicTrée X account (https://x.com/pictree_dea) or by email to (pictree_cs@dea.sg):

・Postal code

・Address

・Name

・Phone number

・Quantity requested

Wear your armband or survey card, be mindful of your surroundings, and enjoy photographing utility poles safely.

Thank you for your continued support of PicTrée.
