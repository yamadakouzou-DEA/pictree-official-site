Notice: “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa” Meetup

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Starting February 7, 2026 (Saturday), we will be holding a meetup for “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa.”

To everyone in Okinawa, even if you’re just “stopping by to check it out” or feeling “anxious about not knowing anyone”—all are welcome! It’s a casual gathering, so let’s chat and have fun together!

【Details】

◼️ Date and Time

February 7, 2026 (Saturday) 18:00～ (UTC+8)

◼️ Location

Okinawan cuisine restaurant near Asahibashi Station

※ Detailed location will be provided individually to those who wish to participate.

◼️ Participation Fee

4,000 yen

◼️ Participation Benefits

Participants of this meetup will receive a PicTrée armband, investigation card, and PicTrée original small pouch (mini pouch) as gifts.

◼️ Recommended for

・ Those who want to casually interact with other pictors

・ Those who are thinking of playing PicTrée from now on

・ Those who want to hear about the Okinawa season and events

◼️ How to Participate

Please send the following information via DM to the official PicTrée X (https://x.com/pictree_dea）or to the PicTrée inquiry email (pictree_cs@dea.sg).

▾ Subject

【PicTrée Okinawa Season】Regarding Meetup Participation

▾ Message Content

・ Nickname (your PicTrée username)

・ A note saying “I wish to participate in the PicTrée Okinawa Meetup”

◼️ First Application Deadline

February 5 (Thursday) around 23:59 (UTC+8)

※ The deadline may change depending on the situation.

Even if you’re thinking “I might go if I can,” you are very welcome!

If you’re even slightly interested, please feel free to contact us.

We look forward to your continued support of PicTrée.
