“Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa” – Tenshi no Hane Pole, Allegresse Pole, Bukubuku Tea Pole, and First Coffee Okinawa Pole Now Live!

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, February 14, 2026 (UTC+9), various collaboration poles have been confirmed for the new season “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa” in Okinawa Prefecture. The “Tenshi no Hane Pole” by Marukichi Shio Senbei Co., Ltd., the “Allegresse Pole” by Allegresse Co., Ltd., the “Bukubuku Tea Pole” by Uchina Chaya Bukubuku, and the “First Coffee Okinawa Pole” by First Coffee Okinawa will appear.

【Overview】

①”Marukichi Shio Senbei Co., Ltd.”

Based in Naha City, Okinawa Prefecture, this food manufacturer has been handcrafting salt rice crackers one by one for 46 years. “Tenshi no Hane” (Angel’s Wings), born from the salt cracker manufacturing process, is beloved as a popular product featuring a wonderfully light and airy texture.

This time, the “Tenshi no Hane Pole” will appear, where you can obtain “Tenshi no Hane” from Marukichi Shio Senbei Co., Ltd.

②”Allegresse Co., Ltd.”

As a home fragrance brand born in Okinawa, they propose a lifestyle where mind and body rejoice through scent. They offer fragrance items that blend into daily life, such as fabric & room sprays and aroma wax bars that create comfortable spaces.

This time, the “Allegresse Pole” will appear, where you can obtain either a “Trial Spray Set (1 set)” or an “Aroma Wax Bar (1 piece)” from Allegresse Co., Ltd.

③”Uchina Chaya Bukubuku”

Located on Tsuboya Yachimun Street in Naha, this is a pottery café and gallery that serves the traditional Okinawan “Bukubuku Tea.” You can enjoy sweets and drinks made with Okinawan ingredients in yachimun pottery.

This time, the “Bukubuku Tea Pole” will appear, where you can obtain a “Bukubuku Tea Free Drink Voucher (1 cup)” from Uchina Chaya Bukubuku.

④”First Coffee Okinawa”

A self-roasted coffee shop located in Ishimine-cho, Naha City, established in 1989 (Heisei 1). They provide coffee with a carefully balanced taste, richness, and aroma by self-roasting carefully selected green beans.

This time, the “First Coffee Okinawa Pole” will appear, where you can obtain “Coffee Drip Bags (10-pack)” from First Coffee Okinawa.

◼️Installation Date:

Saturday, February 14, 2026, 7:00 (UTC+9) ~

◼️Installation Location:

They will appear randomly throughout each area within the target region.

◼️Details:

“Tenshi no Hane Pole,” “Allegresse Pole,” “Bukubuku Tea Pole,” and “First Coffee Okinawa Pole” will be installed within the target area. When you photograph the corresponding pole, a rainbow treasure box will appear, and the respective exchange vouchers will be dispensed from that box.

(Prizes)

①Tenshi no Hane Pole: Tenshi no Hane, 1 case of 10 packs × 20 poles

※The prize will be shipped after submitting an application following voucher acquisition.

②Allegresse Pole: Trial Spray Set (1 set) × 5 poles / Aroma Wax Bar (1 piece) × 5 poles

※The prize will be shipped after submitting an application following voucher acquisition.

③Bukubuku Tea Pole: Bukubuku Tea Free Drink Voucher (1 cup) × 10 poles

※The voucher can be exchanged for the prize by presenting it at “Uchina Chaya Bukubuku” in Naha City.

④First Coffee Okinawa Pole: Coffee Drip Bags (10-pack) × 10 poles

※The voucher can be exchanged for the prize by presenting it at “First Coffee Okinawa” in Naha City.

◼️What are Treasure Poles?

Treasure Poles are special poles where companies or individuals contribute funds or goods to support their local area, and rewards are distributed to participants using those funds as the source. Treasure Poles are hidden among the poles that are photo targets in “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa,” and participants can obtain rewards by finding and photographing Treasure Poles among the many poles.
