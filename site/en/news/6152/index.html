“Discover! Crow’s Nest Season” Friend Referral Campaign

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, March 7, 2026, we will be holding a Friend Referral Campaign for “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba” and “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Sano City, Tochigi Prefecture / Tatebayashi City, Gunma Prefecture / Kazo City, Saitama Prefecture.”

【Campaign Overview】

In “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba” and “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Sano City, Tochigi Prefecture / Tatebayashi City, Gunma Prefecture / Kazo City, Saitama Prefecture,” if you refer a friend or family member who has not played PicTrée yet and the referred person installs the app as a new user, then as long as both the referrer and the referred person photograph at least a certain number of telephone poles or crow’s nest during the season, we will present reward coins to both parties based on their photography progress at the end of the season.

◼️Period:

●“Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba”

2026/03/05 (Thu) – 2026/04/10 (Fri) 17:30 (UTC+9)

●“Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Sano City, Tochigi Prefecture / Tatebayashi City, Gunma Prefecture / Kazo City, Saitama Prefecture”

2026/03/05 (Thu) – 2026/05/31 (Sun) 17:30 (UTC+9)

◼️How to participate:

Please submit both the referrer’s and referred person’s player name and player ID using the form below.

Note: Either the referrer or the referred person may submit the application. Submissions from both parties are not required.

<<Application Form>>

◼️Requirements:

The referred person must have installed the PicTrée app after the referrer.

During the designated season, both the referrer and referee must meet the following requirements:

① Have photographed at least 10 utility poles

② Have photographed at least one crow nest

◼️Rewards:

At the end of the season, reward coins will be granted to both the referrer and the referred person according to the number of telephone poles photographed, as follows.

If both the referrer and the referred person have photographed:

① Take photos of 10 utility poles: 3,600 reward coins (equivalent to ¥300)

② Take photos of 1 crow’s nest: 4,800 reward coins (equivalent to ¥400)

※The number of photos is counted based on the number of photos that have been marked Review OK.

<Reward distribution date>

●“Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba”: Scheduled for late April to early May

●“Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Sano City, Tochigi Prefecture / Tatebayashi City, Gunma Prefecture / Kazo City, Saitama Prefecture”: Scheduled for mid-June to early July

◼️Important notes:

・The referrer does not need to install the app during the implementation period. If the referred user installs the app during the implementation period and both parties have taken photos, the reward eligibility requirements will be met.

・Only users who installed the PicTrée app after the referrer are eligible as the referred person. Users who installed the app before the referrer are not eligible.

・A user may receive the campaign reward as the referred person only once. If applications are submitted stating the user was referred by multiple referrers, only the earliest application will be considered valid.

・If fraud is suspected, rewards will not be granted.

・This campaign may be changed or canceled without prior notice.
