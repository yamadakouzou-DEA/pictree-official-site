Discover the Crow Nest Season — Season Details

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

Starting on Saturday, March 7, 2026, PicTrée will hold its first-ever initiative, “Discover the Crow Nest Season,” with the goal of detecting crow nests on utility poles at an early stage.

In this article, we share the season details for “Discover the Crow Nest Season in Chiba” and “Discover the Crow Nest Season in Sano City, Tochigi / Tatebayashi City, Gunma / Kazo City, Saitama.”

【Season Period and Playable Hours】

◼️ Season Period

● Discover the Crow Nest Season in Chiba

March 7, 2026 (Sat) 6:30 – April 10, 2026 (Fri) 17:30 (UTC+9)

● Discover the Crow Nest Season in Sano City, Tochigi / Tatebayashi City, Gunma / Kazo City, Saitama

March 7, 2026 (Sat) 6:30 – May 31, 2026 (Sun) 17:30 (UTC+9)

◼️ Game Playable Hours:

・ Photography: 6:30–17:30 (UTC+9)

・ Check-in: No restrictions

【How to Participate】

At the start of the season, choose one of three teams (Ampere, Volt, or Watt). You will join that team and compete against the other teams for points.

【Photo Targets and Rewards】

◼️ Photo Targets

・ Utility poles

＜Required photo angles＞
Two photos are required: “Wide shot” and “Pole number.”

・Wide shot: Take a photo so the entire pole fills the frame.
※Please keep a distance of about 5–10 meters from the pole.

・Pole number: Take a photo so the pole number fills the frame.
※If there are two plates, make sure both plates are visible in the photo.

◼️ Photography Reward

There is no reward coin set per utility pole.

You can earn reward coins through the “Discovery Reward” and “Treasure Box Reward” described below.

◼️ Discovery Reward

When you discover and report a utility pole with a crow nest, you will receive a Crow Nest Discovery Reward (reports will be reviewed by the operations team).

To report a crow nest discovery, tap the megaphone icon in the top-right of the utility pole detail screen.

Details on how to report crow nests will be announced at a later date.

*For details on how to report crow nests, please refer to the following notice.

https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/en/news/6173/

● Crow Nest Discovery Reward

For each photo of a crow nest, you will receive 3,600 reward coins (equivalent to ¥300).

＜Example of a crow nest＞

● Treasure Box Reward (Crow Nest Treasure Box)

When you photograph a utility pole, a special treasure box (Crow Nest Treasure Box) may appear with a certain probability, allowing you to obtain reward coins. There are four types of Crow Nest Treasure Boxes, and the amount of reward coins you can receive depends on the type.

◼️ Request: Please report when photography is difficult

If there is any issue with the photo target, or if it is difficult to take a photo due to the surrounding environment or the condition of the target, please do not force photography. Use the reporting function instead.

Please refer to the instructions below.

＜How to report＞

① Tap the target asset icon

② Tap the megaphone icon in the top-right

③ Select the issue(s) occurring with the photo target, then tap Next

④ If you have a helpful photo, take a photo using the Take Photo button, then tap Send

For this event, Discover the Crow Nest Season in Chiba will cover all of Chiba Prefecture.

Discover the Crow Nest Season in Sano City, Tochigi / Tatebayashi City, Gunma / Kazo City, Saitama will cover Sano City (Tochigi), Tatebayashi City (Gunma), and Kazo City (Saitama).

In addition, each season is divided into areas, and area ranking rewards will be granted after the season ends.

◼️ Area Ranking Rewards

These rewards are granted based on the results of the ranking competition for each area set within each season.

Regardless of the team’s placement, rewards are granted according to your individual rank within your team.

【Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)】

These rewards are granted based on the results of the team-versus-team ranking competition held for the season.

This ranking includes both a Team Ranking and an Individual Ranking within Team. The rewards you can obtain at the end of the season are determined by the final team and individual ranking results.

◼️ Coefficients used when calculating team points

The team ranking is determined by each team’s team points.

Team points are calculated as the sum of the cumulative rank points earned by all players on the team.
