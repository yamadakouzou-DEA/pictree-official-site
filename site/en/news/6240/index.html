Announcement: Treasure Hunt Puzzle Game “PicTreasure in Okinawa” (Supervised by TAKARASH!)

We are hosting the interactive puzzle-based treasure hunt game “PicTreasure in Okinawa” as part of “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa”, which has been running since February 1.

In this game, when users take photos of utility poles, “puzzle items” will appear at random. Use the displayed riddles as clues to explore and deduce the answer, then find the “Treasure Pole.” Rewards will be given based on the order of correct submissions.

Please give the puzzles a try.

◼️Eligible Area

Okinawa

◼️Event Period

March 7, 2026 – March 22, 2026

◼️How to Play

　1.When you take photos of utility poles during the Okinawa season, you will randomly obtain puzzle items ①–④.

　2.Solve all the puzzles to pinpoint the location of the “Treasure Pole” in Okinawa.

　3.Identify the “Treasure Pole” and, using the report feature, enter the report keyword that appears along with the puzzle.

　4.From among the correct submitters, the first 10 people will receive the virtual currency “DEP” coins as prizes.
