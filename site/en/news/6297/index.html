“Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa” Attractive Treasure Poles Appear – DEP Worth ¥10,000 ×60 and ¥100,000 ×1, and More!

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

Starting March 7, 2026 (Sat), in Okinawa Prefecture’s new season “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa,” various collaboration poles have been confirmed. There will be 60 poles with prizes worth ¥10,000, one pole worth ¥100,000, and many other attractive Treasure Poles.

【Overview】

1. “Okinawa Electric Power Company” Okinawa Electric Power Poles

The “Okinawa Electric Power Poles” are special poles provided by Okinawa Electric Power Company, which hosts the Okinawa season. By finding and photographing the eligible Treasure Poles, you can obtain high-value Reward Coins.

2. “NTT WEST Corporation” NTT WEST Poles

The “NTT WEST Poles” are special poles provided by NTT WEST Corporation, which hosts the Okinawa season. By finding and photographing the eligible Treasure Poles, you can obtain high-value Reward Coins.

3. “Growth Ring Grid Pte. Ltd.” GRG Poles

The “GRG Poles” are special poles provided by Growth Ring Grid Pte. Ltd., which co-developed the participatory social contribution game “PicTrée – Grid Grab: Capture the Current” with DEA. By finding and photographing the eligible Treasure Poles, you can obtain high-value Reward Coins.

4. “Digital Entertainment Asset Co., Ltd.” 50% Off Airfare Poles

The “50% Off Airfare Poles” are special poles provided by Digital Entertainment Asset Co., Ltd., which operates the participatory social contribution game “PicTrée – Grid Grab: Capture the Current.” By finding and photographing the eligible Treasure Poles, you can obtain “50% Off Airfare Tickets ×2 (RAC domestic routes only).”

5. “Ganju” Pork Village Poles

This company operates “Okinawa Pork Village” at Toguchi Beach in Yomitan Village, Okinawa, sharing the appeal of Okinawa’s “sea” and “pork.” It runs an experiential facility where you can enjoy food and nature at once, including BBQ using Beni Agu and Beni Pork, house-made charcuterie, and marine activities.

This time, “Pork Village Poles” will appear, where you can obtain “Pork Village Gift Certificates worth ¥2,000” provided by Ganju.

6. “Whitening Center Okinawa” Whitening Poles

“Whitening Center Okinawa” is a self-whitening specialty salon for those who want whiter teeth. With easy-to-feel results and safe treatment designed to reduce pain, it operates 14 locations nationwide.

This time, “Whitening Poles” will appear, where you can obtain a “Free Whitening Experience Ticket” provided by Whitening Center Okinawa.

7. “Ikutouen (Tsuboya-yaki Kiln)” Mini Mini Shisa Poles

This is a Tsuboya-yaki kiln that has been passed down for over 340 years in Tsuboya, the town of yachimun pottery. In addition to producing and selling tableware and Shisa made with a focus on Okinawan clay and handcrafting, it also offers pottery experiences so you can experience Tsuboya craftsmanship.

This time, “Mini Mini Shisa Poles” will appear, where you can obtain “Mini Mini Shisa ×1” provided by Ikutouen.

8. “Tokeshi Meat” Okinawa BBQ Set Poles

“Tokeshi Meat” is a long-established butcher shop in Ginowan City, Okinawa, with over 60 years of wholesale and retail history. It handles a wide range of meat products that support Okinawa’s food culture, including locally produced meat for yakiniku and BBQ, as well as goat soup and nakami-jiru.

This time, “Okinawa BBQ Set Poles” will appear, where you can obtain an “Okinawa BBQ Set (for 1 person)” provided by Tokeshi Meat.

9. “Kuba Kasaya (Heiwa-dori Store)” Kuba Kasaya Accessories Poles

A select shop on Heiwa-dori near Kokusai-dori that offers Ryukyu glass and Okinawan crafts and folk goods. It has a wide selection of Okinawa-style souvenirs and original goods, including glowing glasses with fire opals, dragonfly-bead accessories, and Shisa.

This time, “Kuba Kasaya Accessories Poles” will appear, where you can obtain “Select Accessories (1 item only)” provided by Kuba Kasaya (Heiwa-dori Store).

10. “Gallery MITSUO Shisa Museum & Shisa Shrine” Gallery MITSUO Shisa Poles

A private museum and studio in Tsuboya, Naha, run by Shisa artist Mitsuo. In addition to exhibiting and selling traditional plaster Shisa, it also hosts hands-on classes where you can learn directly from artisans, sharing the unique “Mitsuo World.”

This time, “Gallery MITSUO Shisa Poles” will appear, where you can obtain a “Shisa Painting & Chopstick Rest Set” provided by Gallery MITSUO Shisa Museum & Shisa Shrine.

11. “Okinawa Aloe” Shikuwasa Poles

A manufacturer based in Nago City, Okinawa, producing and selling products using Okinawan ingredients such as aloe vera juice and shikuwasa juice. It uses materials from its own farm and contracted farmers, delivering safe products nationwide with a process that does not use preservatives, sweeteners, or fragrances.

This time, “Shikuwasa Poles” will appear, where you can obtain “Okinawa-grown Shikuwasa (Okinawa Prefecture), 100% Juice, 150ml ×6” provided by Okinawa Aloe.

12. “OKCoin Japan” OKJ Poles

OKCoin Japan is the Japanese subsidiary of OK Group, which operates crypto asset exchanges and blockchain businesses globally. In 2020, it registered as a crypto asset exchange service provider and operates the crypto asset exchange service “OKJ.” With a diverse lineup of supported assets and an intuitive user experience, OKJ supports a wide range of users from beginners to professional traders.

This time, “OKJ Poles” will appear, where you can obtain “DEAPcoin (DEP) worth ¥5,000” provided by OKCoin Japan.

13. “Yamasho” Okinawa Gourmet Specialty Store Yamasho Poles

Founded in 1986 as an Okinawan cuisine specialty izakaya, it serves awamori and dishes made with local ingredients. In addition to running restaurants, it also operates an online shop, sharing the appeal of authentic Okinawan flavors and food culture nationwide.

This time, “Yamasho Poles” will appear, where you can obtain “¥1,000 OFF Coupon (usable for totals of ¥5,000 or more)” and “¥2,000 OFF Coupon (usable for totals of ¥10,000 or more)” provided by Okinawa Gourmet Specialty Store Yamasho.

*only one coupon can be used per bill.

◼️ What Are Treasure Poles?

Treasure Poles are special poles funded by companies or individuals who contribute money or goods to support local communities, and those funds are used to return rewards to participants. Treasure Poles are hidden among the utility poles that are eligible for photography in “Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa.” By finding and photographing Treasure Poles among many utility poles, participants can earn rewards.
