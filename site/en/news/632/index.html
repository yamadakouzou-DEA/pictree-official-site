Event Area Details:
For Season 4 of PicTrée (June 1 to June 14), the utility poles in the following areas around Mount Akagi are designated as photography targets:

① Mid-mountain and summit area of Mount Akagi (along Prefectural Road 4 and the area around Ono Lake).
② Foothill area of Mount Akagi (areas with facilities like Mine Park and Roadside Station Green Flower, and Tonton Plaza).
Players are encouraged to choose an area that is most convenient for them to play.
※Please note that along Prefectural Road 4, which leads to the summit, the sidewalks are narrow and some utility poles may be difficult to photograph. Pay close attention to traffic safety, and if you feel it is unsafe, please stop playing.

●Introduction to Tourist Attractions in the Mid-mountain and Summit Area of Mount Akagi:
▼赤城神社(Akagi Shrine)

Situated on the shores of Ono Lake, where many of Mount Akagi’s hiking trails converge, this beautifully vermilion-painted shrine is known as one of Gunma Prefecture’s leading spiritual sites. It is associated with the legend of 赤城姫, who was summoned by the deities of Mount Akagi and the lake, known as “赤城大明神”. According to the “神道集,” prayers made to this goddess are said to be particularly effective, making it a popular spot especially among women.
Source: https://www.maebashi-cvb.com/spot/1053

 

▼Ono Lake

Surrounded by primeval forests, this approximately 800,000 square meter caldera lake features a walking path around its perimeter that takes about an hour to complete. In the summer, the lake is a popular spot for boating and camping; in autumn, visitors can enjoy boat fishing for smelt, and in winter, when the lake surface freezes over, ice fishing for smelt is available. Situated at an altitude of about 1,350 meters, it is one of the few places in the Kanto region where ice bubbles can be observed.

Source: https://www.maebashi-cvb.com/spot/1085

●Introduction to Tourist Spots:
▼道の駅まえばし赤城(Roadside Station Maebashi Akagi)

 

This station offers a spacious parking lot, clean restrooms, and a fully equipped bath facility. It also features a selection of unique local souvenirs. In the food court, you can enjoy local delicacies such as roast beef bowls made with Akagi beef and grilled manju soft-serve.

 

・Address: 36 Taguchi-machi, Maebashi, Gunma 371-0048
・Website： https://maebashi-akagi.jp/
・Phone Number: 027-233-0070

 

▼道の駅 ぐりーんふらわー牧場・大胡(Roadside Station Green Flower Ranch & Ogo)

 

Known for its large Dutch-style windmill, this station’s farm produce market includes the “Milk House,” where you can enjoy superb farm-fresh soft ice cream.

 

・Address: 1369-1 Takikubo-machi, Maebashi, Gunma 371-0235
・More Information: https://www.maebashi-cvb.com/spot/3001
・Phone Number: 027-283-5792

 

▾とんとん広場(Tonton Plaza)

 

This restaurant offers homemade dishes that are kind to your body, featuring “福豚” raised in the rich natural environment of Mount Akagi and abundant fresh local vegetables. Ham and sausages, ideal for souvenirs, are also available for purchase.

 

・Address: 534 Miyazawa-machi, Maebashi, Gunma 371-0247
・Website： https://tonton.akagi-venture.jp/
・Phone Number: 027-283-2983

 

Important Points to Consider:
・The sidewalk along Prefectural Road 4 leading to the summit of Mount Akagi is narrow, and some utility poles may be difficult to photograph. Please pay close attention to traffic safety, and stop playing if you feel it is dangerous. If you find it too difficult to play due to photography challenges, please contact us via DM at PicTrée Official（https://x.com/pictree_dea）.
・(Especially for those playing in the summit area) In Maebashi city, particularly from late April to October, there have been many sightings and reports of bears in residential areas at the southern base of Mount Akagi. If you encounter a bear or other large wildlife, seek refuge temporarily in a vehicle or building, or leave the area quietly and calmly.
