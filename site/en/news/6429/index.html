How to Report a Crow’s Nest Discovery in the “Discover the Crow’s Nest Season”

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

In the new season starting on Saturday, March 7, 2026, “Discover the Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba” and “Discover the Crow’s Nest Season in Sano City (Tochigi), Tatebayashi City (Gunma), and Kazo City (Saitama),” you can receive a Crow’s Nest Discovery Reward by finding a utility pole with a crow’s nest and submitting a discovery report (the operations team will review the report).

This article explains how to report when you find a crow’s nest.

◼️Crow’s Nest Discovery Reward

For each photo of a crow’s nest: 3,600 reward coins (equivalent to ¥300)

<Example of a crow’s nest>

*Note

・The discovery reward is granted only when the operations team reviews the report and it is marked Review OK.

・It may take around 10 days for the discovery report review.

◼️How to Report a Crow’s Nest

If you find a crow’s nest, follow the steps below and submit a discovery report using the report button in the top-right of the screen.

①Tap the icon of the target Photo Asset.

②Tap the megaphone icon in the top-right.

③Tap the Crow’s Nest button.

④Take all photos for the specified angles, then tap Submit.

⑤Report completed. (The discovery reward will be granted after you pass the review.)

*Note

・If you take photos using the normal Take Photo button instead of submitting from the Report button, it will not be eligible for the discovery reward even if a crow’s nest is captured. Please be careful.

・Utility poles that have already been reported for a crow’s nest are not eligible for rewards. You can check the report status in either of the following ways.

①Tap the report button at the top-right of the utility pole information screen. If it shows Reported, it has already been reported.

②Tap User Reports at the bottom of the utility pole information screen. If it shows Bird’s Nest Present ●, it has already been reported.

◼️Photo angles

You must take 4 photos: “Wide Shot,” “Utility Pole Number,” “Crow’s Nest 1,” and “Crow’s Nest 2.”

・Wide Shot: Take a photo so the entire utility pole fills the camera frame.

*Please keep a distance of about 5–10 meters from the utility pole.

・Utility Pole Number: Take a photo so the utility pole number fills the camera frame.

*If there are two tags, please capture both tags in the photo.

・Crow’s Nest 1 & 2: Take photos so that the crow’s nest and the equipment at the top of the utility pole are visible.

*Take the second photo from a different direction than the first.

◼️How to check the review status of discovery reports

You can check the review status of your discovery report by following these steps.

①Tap the menu bar at the top-right of the map screen.

②Tap Review Status.

③Tap the Discovery Reports tab.

④Check the review status for each utility pole.

┣If Review OK: The number of reward coins you earned will be displayed.

┗If Review NG: Tap Reason to view the reason for rejection.
