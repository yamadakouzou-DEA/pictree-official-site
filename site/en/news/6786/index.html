Notice of “Find! Crow Nest Season” Chiba Prefecture Limited Last Sprint Event

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

We are pleased to announce that a Chiba Prefecture limited event will be held as part of “Find! Crow Nest Season in Chiba Prefecture.”

This is the season when crow nesting activity increases. Early detection of nests is a vital initiative to protect local power infrastructure and ensure a stable electricity supply. Let’s liven things up together with our pictors in an event that only PicTrée can offer, while contributing to the local community!

【Chiba Prefecture Limited Event】

◼️Overview

From Wednesday, April 1 to Friday, April 10, we will hold a ranking event competing on the number of crow nests photographed across all of Chiba Prefecture. Rewards are prepared according to ranking placement, so please participate eagerly. No form submission is required to join.
