Season Details for “Vines Operation 2026 in Chiba ①, Saitama ①, Ibaraki ①”

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

We are pleased to announce the details of three new seasons starting Saturday, April 11, 2026: “Vines Operation 2026 in Chiba,” “Vines Operation 2026 in Saitama,” and “Vines Operation 2026 in Ibaraki.”

“Vines Operation 2026” is held with the purpose of early detection of vines wrapping around utility poles. Additionally, in “Vines Operation 2026 in Chiba,” following the “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba,” discovering crow’s nests is also one of the objectives.

【Season Duration and Playable Hours】

◼️Season Duration

● “Vines Operation 2026 in Chiba ①”

April 11, 2026 (Sat) 6:00 – June 28, 2026 (Sun) 18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

● “Vines Operation 2026 in Saitama ①”

April 11, 2026 (Sat) 6:00 – June 28, 2026 (Sun) 18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

● “Vines Operation 2026 in Ibaraki ①”

April 11, 2026 (Sat) 6:00 – June 28, 2026 (Sun) 18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️Playable Hours

・Photography: 6:00–18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

・Check-in: No restrictions

【Participation Format】

At the start of the season, you will select one of three teams (“Ampere,” “Volt,” or “Watt”) and compete against other teams for scores as a member of your chosen team.

【Photography Targets and Camera Angles】

◼️Photography Targets

・Utility Poles

・Utility Poles (Vines)

In this season, some utility poles are designated as “Utility Poles (Vines),” which are more susceptible to vine growth. “Utility Poles (Vines)” have a different asset icon from regular utility poles and possess special effects in the game.

*Photography rewards are the same as regular utility poles.

<Utility Pole (Vines) Icon>

<Characteristics of Utility Poles (Vines)>

・Utility Pole Connect connections (up to 5)

・Utility Pole HP 5,000

◼️Camera Angles

Three photos are required: “Full View 1,” “Full View 2,” and “Pole Number.”

・Full View 1, Full View 2: Photograph so that the entire utility pole fills the camera frame.

Full View 2 should be taken from a different direction than Full View 1.

※Please keep a distance of approximately 5–10 meters from the utility pole.

・Pole Number: Photograph the pole number so that it fills the camera frame.

*If there are two plates, photograph so that both plates are visible.

【How to Report Vines】

In this season, you will be asked to select whether vines are present before submitting your photos.

If you find vines while photographing, check the height of the vine tips on the utility pole and select the appropriate option.

When selecting “Yes,” you can take one additional photo. If you feel the vine growth is not clearly visible in the photos already taken, please take an additional photo before submitting (the angle is flexible, so please photograph from an angle where the vine growth is clearly visible).

If there are no vines, select “No” and press the “Send” button.

【How to Report Crow’s Nest Discoveries】

In “Vines Operation 2026 in Chiba,” if you discover and report a utility pole with a crow’s nest, you will receive a “Crow’s Nest Discovery Reward” (verification by the PicTrée Operation Team will be conducted).

Please make crow’s nest discovery reports from the megaphone icon in the upper right of the utility pole detail screen.

For details on how to report crow’s nests, please refer to the following announcement.

https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/en/news/6786/

◼️Request Regarding Difficulty in Photography

If any problem has occurred with the photography target, or if photography is difficult due to the surrounding environment or the condition of the target, please do not force yourself to photograph it and instead report it using the report function.

Please refer to the following for the reporting method.

＜Reporting Method＞

①Tap the target asset icon

②Tap the megaphone icon in the upper right

③Select the category of the problem occurring with the photography target, then tap “Next”

④If you have a photo that may be useful, take one using the “Photograph” button, then tap “Send”

【Photography Rewards and Discovery Rewards】

Photography rewards and discovery rewards are the same for both regular utility poles and Utility Poles (Vines).

◼️Photography Rewards

◼️Discovery Rewards

●Vines Discovery Reward

If you photograph a utility pole that actually has vine growth during the season period, and it is approved through verification by the PicTrée Operation Team, you will receive a “Vines Discovery Reward” in addition to the regular reward.

*The “Vine Discovery Reward” is only granted for the first-time capture of a utility pole. Subsequent captures of the same pole are not eligible.

*Not all “Utility Poles (Vines)” necessarily have vine growth.

*Please note that if you find vine growth but select “No” in the vine report before submitting the image, the Vines Discovery Reward will not be granted.

*Please do not select “Yes” in the vine report when there is no vine growth (repeated false reports may result in account suspension).

Please note that the “Vines Discovery Reward” is granted after verification by the PicTrée Operation Team, so it is not granted immediately after photography like the photography reward. It may take approximately 1–2 weeks for the reward to be granted.

●Crow’s Nest Discovery Reward

If you photograph a utility pole with a crow’s nest during the following target period, and it is approved through verification by the PicTrée Operation Team, you will receive a “Crow’s Nest Discovery Reward.”

・Period: April 11, 2026 06:00:00 – May 31, 2026 18:00:00 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️Area-Based Ranking Rewards

Rewards granted based on the results of the area ranking battles set for each season.

Rewards are granted based on “Individual Ranking within Team” regardless of team standing.

【Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)】

Rewards granted based on the results of the inter-team ranking battles conducted on a season basis.

This ranking consists of “Team Ranking” and “Individual Ranking within Team.” The rewards earned at the end of the season are determined based on the team ranking and individual ranking results at the time the season ends.
