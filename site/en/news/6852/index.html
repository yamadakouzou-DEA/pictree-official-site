Notice of “Vines Campaign 2026” Campaigns

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

We are pleased to announce two campaigns during the “Vines Campaign 2026 in Chiba①·Saitama①·Ibaraki①” season. We hope you will join us!

【Photo Promotion Campaign】

During the season, in addition to your regular photo rewards, we will give you bonus Reward Coins based on the number of utility pole photos you take!

◼️Campaign Period:

“Vines Campaign 2026 in Chiba①·Saitama①·Ibaraki①”

Saturday, April 11, 2026 – Sunday, June 28, 2026

◼️How to Participate:

No form submission required. After the season ends, we will check the number of photos taken and distribute rewards accordingly.

◼️Rewards:

① 50 or more photos: 12,000 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥1,000)

② 100 or more photos: 24,000 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥2,000)

③ 200 or more photos: 36,000 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥3,000)

※ Only the highest qualifying reward will be distributed.

◼️Reward Distribution Date

Scheduled for late July

【Friend Referral Campaign】

If you refer a friend or family member who has not yet played PicTrée, and that person newly installs the app and both the referrer and the referred person each take a certain number of utility pole photos, Reward Coins will be given to both parties based on their photo count at the end of the season.

◼️Campaign Period:

“Vines Campaign 2026 in Chiba①·Saitama①·Ibaraki①”

Friday, April 3, 2026 – Sunday, June 28, 2026

◼️How to Participate:

Please submit the player names and player IDs of both the referrer and the referred person using the form below.

※ Either the referrer or the referred person may submit the application (submissions from both parties are not required).

<<Application Form>>

◼️Conditions:

・ The referred person must have installed the PicTrée app after the referrer.

・ Both the referrer and the referred person must have taken 10 or more utility pole photos during the target season.

◼️Rewards:

Reward Coins will be distributed to both the referrer and the referred person based on their photo count at the end of the season.

When both the referrer and the referred person have taken:

① 10–29 photos: 1,200 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥100)

② 30–49 photos: 3,600 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥300)

③ 50 or more photos: 6,000 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥500)

※ Photo counts are based on photos that have passed review.

※ If the referrer and the referred person have different photo counts, the lower count will determine the reward tier for both.

(Example) Referrer: 50 photos (③), Referred person: 10 photos (①)

　⇒ Both the referrer and the referred person will receive 1,200 Reward Coins (equivalent to ¥100).

◼️Reward Distribution Date

Scheduled for late July

◼️Notes:

・ The referrer does not need to install the app during the campaign period. The conditions are met as long as the referred person installs the app during the campaign period and both parties take 10 or more photos.

・ Only users who installed the PicTrée app after the referrer are eligible as referred persons.

・ Users who installed the app before the referrer are not eligible.

・ Each person may only receive the reward as a referred person once.

・ If applications are submitted from multiple referrers, only the first application received will be considered valid.

・ If fraudulent activity is suspected, rewards will not be distributed.

・ This campaign may be changed or cancelled without prior notice.
