Celebrating PicTrée’s 2nd Anniversary! Announcing 3 Major Campaigns

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Thanks to all of you PICTER, PicTrée has reached its 2nd anniversary since launch on April 13.

To commemorate PicTrée’s 2nd anniversary, we will be holding the “2nd Anniversary Special! 3 Major Campaigns” starting April 13.

◼️Overview of the 2nd Anniversary 3 Major Campaigns

(1) Thank You, PICTER! Gift Campaign

(2) 2nd Anniversary Memory Post Contest

(3) 2nd Anniversary Special X Space “Look Back with Producer Kito & Evangelist Tabijou saori!”

◼️Campaign Details

(1) Thank You, PICTER! Gift Campaign

We will present limited-edition original goods to those who placed in the “Top 20 First-Capture Infrastructure Ranking” across the target seasons over the past year!

● Period:

May 17, 2025 – March 22, 2026

● Target Seasons:

All 9 seasons

① Prefecture Battle! Utility Pole Contest in Aomori, Akita, Yamagata

② Vines Operation (combined ranking across all 8 sessions: Chiba ①②③, Ibaraki ①②③, Saitama ①②)

③ PicTrée Town Battle in Hokkaido Summer 2025

④ Utility Pole Contest in Iwate, Miyagi, Fukushima, Niigata

⑤ Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Nagano, Yamanashi, Saitama

⑥ Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Tochigi

⑦ Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Maebashi City 2025

⑧ Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba

⑨ Grid Grab: Capture the Current in Okinawa

* Because “Vines Operation” includes overlapping rankings, we will publish up to the Top 25.

● Ranking Method:

Rankings are based on the number of first-capture infrastructure submissions in PicTrée for each season.

● Target Infrastructure Equipment:

Utility poles, telephone poles, manholes, handholes

* Target infrastructure equipment varies by season.

● How to Check the Rankings:

Please refer to the announcement below.

PicTrée 2nd Anniversary Campaign! Announcement of First-Capture Ranking by Season

● Premium Prize Lineup:

・1st place: PicTrée T-shirt (M–XL)

・2nd place: PicTrée hoodie (X or XL)

・3rd–5th place: PicTrée original towel

・6th–10th place: PicTrée original pouch (5 colors, random)

・11th–17th place: PicTrée original neck strap

・18th–20th place: PicTrée original tenugui (pink) or cooling muffler towel (lightweight)

* If you placed in multiple seasons, you will receive the prize corresponding to your highest ranking.

● How to Receive Your Prize:

Those who ranked in, please enter your shipping information using the form below.

Prize Shipping Information Form

Deadline: April 30, 2026 (Thu) 23:59 (JST / UTC+9)

* Please note that prizes cannot be delivered after the deadline (shipping scheduled for May–June).

(2) 2nd Anniversary Memory Post Contest

Tell us about the “encounters,” “drama,” and “favorite photos” born from your shoots across Japan over the past year!

● How to Participate:

Add the hashtag #ピクトレ2周年 and post your episode and photo on X!

● Prize:

From the PICTER who participate in the contest, 2 people who shared particularly wonderful posts will receive a “2nd Anniversary Commemorative Can Badge & Small Gift.”

● Campaign Period:

– Posting period: April 13 (Mon) – April 24 (Fri) 23:59 (JST / UTC+9)

– Winner announcement: Announced during the 2nd Anniversary X Space

– Prize shipping: Mid to late May

* Schedule is subject to change.

● Prize Shipping Method:

After the winners are announced, we will reach out via X DM to ask for your shipping information and send the prize to that address.

We welcome posts from those who visited the locations in person, as well as from those who cheered us on remotely!

(3) 2nd Anniversary Special X Space “Look Back with Producer Kito & Evangelist Tabijou saori!”

We will be welcoming Producer Kito and our evangelist Tabijou saori for a PicTrée 2nd Anniversary commemorative space!

Let’s look back on the past year together with all our PICTER! You might even hear about the next season! We will also announce the winners of the “2nd Anniversary Memory Post Contest”!

● Date and Time:

We will announce on X once details are confirmed.

● Featured Speakers:

PicTrée Producer Kito

PicTrée Evangelist Tabijou saori (@shana_butterfly)

PicTrée Operation Team Picpen

● X Space URL:

We will announce on X once details are confirmed.

The entire operation team is looking forward to enjoying this anniversary period together with all of you PICTER!
