“Discover! Crow Nest Season” Chiba Prefecture Exclusive Ranking Results

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

We are pleased to announce the ranking results and reward distribution for “Discover! Crow Nest Season in Chiba.”

The ranking results and the reward coins distributed based on each rank are as follows.

◼️Crow Nest Discovery Ranking

Crow nests discovered across Chiba Prefecture on Saturday, March 21

*Reports from Saturday, March 21 are counted based on the review status as of Wednesday, April 15.

*As previously announced, only those who responded via the Google Form are eligible.

*When the number of discoveries is tied, participants share the same rank.

*Reward coins are scheduled to be distributed in late April.

◼️Last Spurt Ranking

Crow nests discovered across Chiba Prefecture from Wednesday, April 1 to Friday, April 10

*Reports from Wednesday, April 1 to Friday, April 10 are counted based on the review status as of Wednesday, April 15.

*When the number of discoveries is tied, all tied participants share the same rank, and the subsequent ranks are skipped accordingly.

*Reward coins are scheduled to be distributed from late April to mid-May.
