Request Regarding Vine/Ivy Reporting in “Operation Vines 2026”

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

During the currently ongoing “Operation Vines 2026,” some reports that do not qualify as valid vine/ivy reports have been identified.

Please refer to the NG (incorrect) and OK (correct) examples below, and we kindly ask for your cooperation in submitting appropriate reports.

【NG Examples】

・Cases where grass is only growing at the base and there is no vine/ivy wrapping around the utility pole

・Cases where it is unclear whether vines/ivy are wrapping around the pole

【OK Examples】

・Cases where vines/ivy are wrapping around the very top of the utility pole

・Cases where vines/ivy are wrapping around the upper half of the utility pole

・Cases where vines/ivy are wrapping around the lower half of the utility pole

At PicTrée, we conduct smooth equipment inspections based on your reports.

We sincerely appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

PicTrée Operation Team
