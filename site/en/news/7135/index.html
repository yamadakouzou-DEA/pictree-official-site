[Apology] “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season” — Notice Regarding Missing Crow’s Nest Discovery Rewards and Chiba-Limited Event Ranking Corrections

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

We have identified errors in data aggregation during the “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season,” resulting in missing “Crow’s Nest Discovery Rewards” for some users and incorrect rankings in the Chiba-limited events.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused.

The details of this matter are as follows.

1. Additional Distribution of “Crow’s Nest Discovery Rewards”

The missing “Crow’s Nest Discovery Rewards” have been distributed to affected users for the following seasons:

■ “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba”

■ “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Sano City (Tochigi), Tatebayashi City (Gunma), Kazo City (Saitama)”

2. Ranking Corrections for “Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba” Chiba-Limited Events

The correct rankings for the following events are as shown below.

■ 3/21 (Sat) Chiba-Limited Crow’s Nest Photo Event

■ 4/1 (Wed) – 4/10 (Sat) Chiba-Limited Last Spurt Event

*The corrected reward amounts based on the updated rankings have been distributed to affected users as of today.

The entire operation team is committed to strengthening our data aggregation processes to prevent recurrence.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused.

We appreciate your continued support of PicTrée.

PicTrée Operation Team
