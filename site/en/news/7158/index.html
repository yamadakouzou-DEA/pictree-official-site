[“Vines Blitz 2026 in Chiba” & “Grid Grab: Capture the Current (Regular Season)”] Collab with HEALTHREE! Follow & Repost Campaign, Treasure Utility Poles, and Collab Meetup!

Thank you for playing PicTrée!

Starting Friday, May 15th, we’re launching a collaboration with HEALTHREE — the movement RPG where you can walk 10 million steps — across “Vines Blitz 2026 in Chiba” and “Grid Grab: Capture the Current (Regular Season).” We’re running three campaigns, so don’t miss your chance to join!

1. In-App Events

① HEALTHREE In-App Event:

・Reward: 3,000 yen worth of DEP

・Winners: 66 participants

・How to earn:

　① Complete the limited map (PicTrée Collab Map) in the HEALTHREE app to earn rewards

　② Open an OKJ account and photograph 30 or more utility poles in PicTrée (combined total across Vines Blitz and Regular Season)

※ Winners will be contacted individually.

[PicTrée Collab Map Overview]

The “PicTrée Collab Map” is a limited collaboration map accessible by scanning a QR code on X — playable once per person. Clearing the map earns you the limited reward: a “3,000 yen DEP Acquisition Ticket.”

② PicTrée In-App Event: Photograph “HEALTHREE Utility Poles” (Treasure Poles) — Win 10 HEAL-3 Coins!

“HEALTHREE Utility Poles” will appear as special sponsor-backed Treasure Poles. Spot and photograph a HEALTHREE Utility Pole to receive 10 HEAL-3 Coins (worth 100 yen).

[HEALTHREE Utility Pole Overview]

“HEALTHREE Utility Poles” are exclusive Treasure Poles in PicTrée, created in collaboration with HEALTHREE — the movement RPG where you can walk 10 million steps. When you find and photograph a HEALTHREE Utility Pole, a Rainbow Treasure Chest appears, and you’ll earn a ticket for 10 HEAL-3 Coins (worth 100 yen), usable to purchase items within HEALTHREE.

・Details: HEALTHREE Utility Poles are placed at select poles within the event area. Photographing one triggers a Rainbow Treasure Chest, which dispenses a prize redemption ticket.

・Placement date: Saturday, June 6, 2026 (JST / UTC+9) onwards

・Reward: Photograph a HEALTHREE Utility Pole among the target poles to receive 10 HEAL-3 Coins (worth 100 yen)

・Quantity: 1,000 poles total (500 each in “Vines Blitz 2026 in Chiba” and “Grid Grab: Capture the Current (Regular Season)”)

※ Prizes are shipped after submitting a redemption request once the ticket is obtained.
