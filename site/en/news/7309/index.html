“Vines Mission 2026 in Chiba①・Saitama①・Ibaraki①” Season Extension & “Double Reward Campaign — Vines Mission 2026 in Chiba①” Launch

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

The season for the currently ongoing “Vines Mission 2026 in Chiba①・Saitama①・Ibaraki①” has been extended to Sunday, July 5, 2026.

Additionally, starting Saturday, June 20, 2026, we will be launching the “Double Reward Campaign — Vines Mission 2026 in Chiba①,” which increases the first-time shooting reward for unshot utility poles in select areas by the equivalent of 20 yen.

◼️Season Extension

The season dates will be extended as follows.

Please note that shooting hours remain unchanged.

＜Before＞

Saturday, April 11, 2026 6:00 – Sunday, June 28, 2026 18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

＜After＞

Saturday, April 11, 2026 6:00 – Sunday, July 5, 2026 18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️Double Reward Campaign — Vines Mission 2026 in Chiba①

During the following period, the first-time shooting reward for unshot utility poles in eligible areas within “Vines Mission 2026 in Chiba①・Saitama①・Ibaraki①” will increase by the equivalent of 20 yen compared to normal.

●Target Period:

Saturday, June 20, 2026 – Sunday, July 5, 2026 (JST / UTC+9)

●Target Areas:

Isumi City, Tako Town, Katsuura City, Minamiboso City, Otaki Town, Kamogawa City, Futtsu City, Asahi City, Yokoshiba-Hikari Town, Sammu City, Katori City, Shibayama Town, Kozaki Town, Onjuku Town, Kujukuri Town, Sosa City, Tateyama City, Shirako Town, Kyonan Town, Mutsuzawa Town, Chonan Town, Kimitsu City, Tohno Town, Yachiyo City

●Reward:

＜Before＞

240 Reward Coins (equivalent to 20 yen)

＜After＞

480 Reward Coins (equivalent to 40 yen)
