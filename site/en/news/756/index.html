About the STEPN and SNPIT/Pictia Campaign

During Season 4 of PicTrée (June 1 to June 14), we are hosting three campaigns for those playing locally in the Mount Akagi area.

 

▼STEPN Community × PicTrée Collaboration (June 1)
①「 STEPN コミュニティ×ピクトレ」キャンペーン〜電柱撮影ウォーキングin赤城山〜　

 

SNPIT/Pictia × PicTrée Collaboration (June 1-14)
②「SNPIT×ピクトレ」キャンペーン〜ツツジ＆電柱撮影バトル in 赤城山〜
③「ピクティア×ピクトレ」キャンペーン〜置き銭&電柱撮影キャンペーンin赤城山〜

 

Take advantage of playing on-site to participate in these campaigns and win fabulous prizes!

 

【1】STEPN コミュニティ×ピクトレプレゼントキャンペーン〜電柱撮影ウォーキングin赤城山〜　

 

■Campaign Overview:
On June 1st (Saturday), visitors to the Mount Akagi area who play STEPN and PicTrée will be entered into a lottery to win special prizes!

 

■Prizes:
Participants will have the chance to win one of the following prizes through a lottery:

・3 STEPN Shoe NFTs
・5 PicTrée Utility Pole NFTs
・5 prizes of 100 GMT each
Each NFT will be given as a prize to one individual (a total of 13 winners will be selected in this campaign).

 

 

■How to Enter:
・Declare your participation by adding the hashtag #STEPN_ピクトレ on X by June 1st (Saturday).
*Participation declarations on the day itself are also accepted (Updated on May 29).
・On June 1st (Saturday), post a screenshot of your STEPN activity that day and images of the utility poles you photographed, together with the hashtag #STEPN_ピクトレ on X.

 

▾ Image of a sample screenshot

 

■Prize Distribution Timing:
Prizes are planned to be distributed around the end of June.

 

*Reference Site: https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/en/news/368/

 

【2】SNPIT/ピクティア×ピクトレプレゼントキャンペーン　　　　　 　　　　　　　　
②「SNPIT×ピクトレ」ツツジ＆電柱撮影バトル in 赤城山　　

 

■Campaign Overview:
From June 1st (Saturday) to June 14th (Friday), visitors to Mount Akagi who play both SNPIT and PicTrée will be entered into a lottery to receive special prizes!

 

■Prizes:
Participants will have the chance to win one of the following prizes through a lottery:

・2 Camera NFTs
・2 PicTrée Utility Pole NFTs
Each NFT will be given as a prize to one individual (a total of 4 winners will be selected in this campaign).

 

 

*The images represent the NFTs.
Note: The Camera NFTs are not in a box but are already opened.

 

■How to Enter:
・From June 1st (Saturday) to June 14th (Friday), post screenshots of the SNPIT photography screen and images of the utility poles you photographed, along with the hashtag #SNPIT_ピクトレ.
*There is no need to declare participation.

 

▾ Image of a sample screenshot

 

■Prize Distribution Timing:
Prizes are planned to be distributed around the end of June.
