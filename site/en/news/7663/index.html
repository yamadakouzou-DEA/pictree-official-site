“PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” Season Details

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

We are pleased to announce the overview and special events for the new season, “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido 2026 Summer,” starting Saturday, August 8, 2026.

1. “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” Season Overview

[Season Period and Available Play Hours]

◼️Season Period

August 8, 2026 (Sat.) – November 3, 2026 (Tue./Public Holiday)

◼️Gameplay Hours

●Photo Capture Hours:

August: 5:00–19:00 (JST / UTC+9)

September: 5:00–18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

October: 6:00–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

November: 6:00–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

●Check-in Hours:

No restrictions

[Participation Format]

At the start of the season, choose one of three teams (“ヒグマ”, “キタキツネ”, or “シマエナガ”) and compete against the other teams for score.

[Shooting Targets and Angles]

◼️Shooting Targets

[Request to Report Difficult Shooting Situations]

If a shooting target has any issues, or if the surrounding environment or condition of the target makes shooting difficult, please do not force a photo and instead use the report function to submit a report.

Please refer to the steps below for how to report.

◼️How to Report

① Tap the target asset icon

② Tap the megaphone icon in the upper right

③ Check the issue affecting the shooting target and tap “Next”

④ If you have a helpful photo, take one using the “Take Photo” button, then tap “Submit”

[Capture Rewards] ※1

※1) Once a reward has been earned from a shooting target, no further capture rewards can be obtained from that target.

※2) Utility poles in urban areas have relatively lower rewards, while rural poles are set higher. The capture reward for each pole can be identified by the number displayed on the screen (the “〇〇 Coins GET” shown above “Capture”).

[Event Areas]

◼️Event Areas

Wakkanai, Asahikawa, Kitami, Sapporo, Iwamizawa, Otaru, Kushiro, Obihiro, Iburi-Hidaka, Hakodate

◼️Heatmap

The heat map is color-coded as follows. Please use this as a reference.

Red: Utility Pole
Yellow-green: Concrete Utility Pole (urban areas)
Green: Concrete Utility Pole (suburban areas)
Light blue: Manholes and handholes (urban areas)
Blue: Manholes and handholes (suburban areas)

[Ranking Rewards]

“PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” ranking rewards consist of two types: “Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)” and “Area Ranking Rewards.”

◼️Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)

Rewards granted based on the results of the team ranking battle.

This ranking includes both a “Team Ranking” and an “Individual Rank within Team.” Rewards earned at the end of the season are determined by both your team’s ranking and your individual ranking at the end of the season.

(Unit: Reward Coins)

◼️Area Ranking Rewards

Rewards granted based on the ranking battle results in each area. There are 10 areas in total.

Rewards are granted based on your “Individual Rank within Team.”

2. “PicTrée City Battle in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” 3 Special Events

Special Event ① “Hokuden NW & NTT ME Cup! Group Battle! City Battle in Hokkaido 2026”

A group event where up to 5 players compete for points, “Hokuden NW & NTT ME Cup! Group Battle! City Battle in Hokkaido 2026,” will be held simultaneously.

The 1st place group wins rewards worth 1,000,000 yen!

A campaign to earn bonus rewards for participating in a group of 5 is also running!

Please check the following article for how to participate in the group battle.

https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/news/7367/

◼️Group Ranking Rewards

During “Hokuden NW & NTT ME Cup! Group Battle! City Battle in Hokkaido 2026” held throughout the season, rewards will be distributed at the end of the season based on group ranking.

There are two types of “Group Ranking Rewards”:

・Shared Reward: A reward split equally among all group members based on the number of participants.

・Individual Reward: A reward given to every group member.

Special Event ② “Treasure Poles”

Treasure Poles that earn high-value Reward Coins when photographed are appearing!

“High-value Poles” worth 5,000 yen each and “Daily Poles” worth 500 yen, with 5 new ones appearing every day, will be featured.

Please check the following article for details.

(URL)

Special Event ③ “Emergency Mission”

Emergency Missions will occur irregularly this season.

“Emergency Mission” is a mission that occurs irregularly during the season, requiring you to photograph specified shooting targets within a time limit.

If you complete the mission within the set time limit, you can earn up to 3,000 yen in rewards based on your completion rank.

◼️Event Period

August 8, 2026 (Sat.) – November 3, 2026 (Tue./Public Holiday)

◼️Rewards

1,200 Reward Coins (equiv. 100 yen) – 360,000 Reward Coins (equiv. 3,000 yen)

※Rewards are set individually for each mission.

The reward for each mission can be confirmed by tapping the information icon in the upper right of the reward mark for that mission on the Shooting Mission List screen.

◼️How to Participate

Follow these steps to display Emergency Mission target facilities on the map:

1.　Tap “Shooting Mission” on the map screen

2. Tap “Emergency Mission” from the “Shooting Mission List”

3. Tap “Challenge”
