PicTrée Town Battle in Hokkaido 2026 Summer: Hokuden NW × SO&HIGH PROJECT Utility Poles Appear!

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Starting with the new season “PicTrée Town Battle in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” on August 8, 2026 (Saturday), a collaboration with “SO&HIGH PROJECT,” supported by Hokkaido Electric Power Network Co., Inc. (Hokuden NW), brings two new utility pole types: the “Hokuden NW × SO&HIGH PROJECT High-Value Utility Pole,” which awards Reward Coins worth 5,000 yen per pole, and the “Hokuden NW × SO&HIGH PROJECT Daily Utility Pole,” with 5 appearing every day.

[“Hokuden NW × SO&HIGH PROJECT High-Value Utility Pole” Overview]

◼️Installation Date:

August 8, 2026 (Saturday), 7:00 AM (JST / UTC+9) onward

◼️Installation Locations:

They are installed in the following municipalities.

We plan to share photo capture updates every Thursday via our official X account.

We’ll also be sharing more hints going forward. Please follow us and stay tuned!

Official X https://x.com/pictree_dea

Kamisunagawa Town, Numata Town, Hidaka Town, Sōbetsu Town, Urakawa Town

◾️Prizes:
