Collab with the Suiyou Dou Deshou Directors! Fujiyan & Ureshii PicTrée Duel Archipelago Begins!

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, August 8, we’re running a collaboration with Tadahisa Fujimura and Masamichi Ureshino, the director duo behind the hit Hokkaido TV show “Suiyou Dou Deshou”!

Beyond fans of the show, we’re rolling out event and collab items that span both digital and real-world experiences so even more players can enjoy PicTrée. Don’t miss this chance to join in!

① Team Battle Event: “Fujiyan & Ureshii PicTrée Duel Archipelago”

As part of “PicTrée Town Battle in Hokkaido Summer 2026,” starting Saturday, August 8, we’re holding the “Fujiyan & Ureshii PicTrée Duel Archipelago.” Players join either Team Majin (Mr. Fujimura) or Team Ureshii (Mr. Ureshino), and the two squads compete over the season based on the total number of utility poles their members photograph.

How to enter: Sign up via the dedicated entry form (deadline: August 23)

Entry form URL: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1ij4Fb4BR-wOPITL4AiQWYS6olR3UvP5nAYKPlJYEjnY/edit

Prizes:

· All members of the winning team receive a commemorative collab image

· The top 3 photographers on the winning team receive autographed goods from Mr. Fujimura and Mr. Ureshino

② [Sep 9 Only] PicTrée Duel Archipelago in Obihiro

On Wednesday, September 9, Mr. Fujimura and Mr. Ureshino will appear at Obihiro Station in Hokkaido for a real-world event, “[Sep 9 Only] PicTrée Duel Archipelago in Obihiro,” where they’ll play PicTrée together with everyday users.

Date: Wednesday, September 9

Location: Around Obihiro Station

Users who have photographed 100 assets by the day of the event will receive a special gift from Mr. Fujimura and Mr. Ureshino. The top photographers on the day will also receive PicTrée merchandise.

How to join: Details will be announced later on PicTrée’s official X (@pictree_dea).

*Whether the event proceeds will depend on weather conditions.

③ Photograph the “Suiyou Dou Director Duo Fujiyan & Ureshii Utility Pole” for a chance to win an online drinking party ticket!

A special “treasure” utility pole, the “Suiyou Dou Director Duo Fujiyan & Ureshii Utility Pole,” will appear mainly around locations associated with “Suiyou Dou Deshou.”

Installation date: Saturday, August 8

Number of poles: 95

60 in “PicTrée Town Battle in Hokkaido Summer 2026”

25 in “Regular Season 2”

10 in “Vines Operation 2026 in Chiba②”

Photo reward: A ticket to an online drinking party with Mr. Fujimura and Mr. Ureshino, held Saturday, November 21

*The date is subject to change.

*You’ll need to respond to a form accessed from the item.

④ New “Suiyou Dou Deshou” Collab Items

Starting Saturday, August 8, limited-edition collab items will appear at random in treasure chests dropped when you photograph utility poles.

“Chef’s Extra-Spicy Ebi Chili”: Deals 500 damage to an enemy team’s utility pole

“This Will Be Our Campsite” Tent: Restores 30 stamina

Collab Video Released!

To mark this collaboration, we’ve released a special video on the YouTube channel “Fujiyan & Ureshii’s Suiyou Dou Desou TV.” In the video, Mr. Fujimura and Mr. Ureshino give an easy-to-follow rundown of the rules for the team battle event “Fujiyan & Ureshii PicTrée Duel Archipelago,” the collab items, and the details of the real-world event in Obihiro — plus share their enthusiasm for leading their respective teams.
