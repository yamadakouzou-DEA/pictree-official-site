Details of the “NTT ME Reward Boost↑↑ Event in Tohoku, Niigata & Nagano”

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, August 29, 2026, we will launch the “NTT ME Reward Boost↑↑ Event in Tohoku, Niigata & Nagano” in Season 2 of Grid Grab: Capture the Current Regular Season, covering the entire Tohoku region as well as Niigata and Nagano Prefectures. At the same time, Treasure Poles offering reward coins worth 10,000 yen or 1,000 yen will also appear.

◼️”NTT ME Reward Boost↑↑ Event in Tohoku, Niigata & Nagano” Overview

●Event Period

Sat, Aug 29, 5:30 – Sun, Nov 15, 17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

*The event period may be changed or ended early depending on the progress of photo submissions.

●Shooting Hours

August–September: 5:30–18:30 (JST / UTC+9)

October–November: 6:15–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

●Target Areas

Miyagi, Fukushima, Iwate, Aomori, Yamagata, Akita, Niigata, and Nagano Prefectures

*Photo assets already captured in Grid Grab: Capture the Current Regular Season are not eligible for these rewards.

*In some areas, there may be photo assets that are not eligible for the increased reward.

● Heat Map

The heat map is color-coded based on the amount of the reward increase.

The color codes are as follows. Please use this as a reference.

Light blue: 420 reward coins increase (600 reward coins GET)

Blue: 1,020 reward coins increase (1,200 reward coins GET)

Pink: 1,620 reward coins increase (1,800 reward coins GET)

Red: 2,220 reward coins increase (2,400 reward coins GET)

Purple: 3,420 reward coins increase (3,600 reward coins GET)

●Rewards

The standard shooting reward of about 15 yen per pole increases to between 50 yen and up to 300 yen. (The amount of the increase varies by location, such as urban or suburban areas.)

●How to Participate

From Events at the bottom left of the game’s top screen, select “NTT ME Reward Boost↑↑ Event in Tohoku, Niigata & Nagano”.

Alternatively, select “Regular Season 2” from the season selector at the top left of the top screen, then look for and photograph poles marked with the reward boost icon shown below.

<Icon marking a reward-boosted pole>

Poles in urban areas are set to relatively lower rewards, while poles in suburban areas are set higher. You can check each pole’s shooting reward by the number shown on the screen below (the “○○ Coins GET” portion above “Take Photo”).

◼️Treasure Pole Overview

During the “NTT ME Reward Boost↑↑ Event in Tohoku, Niigata & Nagano” period, the following two types of Treasure Poles will appear.

“10,000 Yen Pole”

●Placement Period

Sat, Aug 29, 5:30 – Sun, Nov 15, 17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

●Locations

Fukushima: 4 poles

Akita: 3 poles

Aomori: 3 poles

Miyagi: 2 poles

Yamagata: 2 poles

Iwate: 2 poles

Niigata: 2 poles

Nagano: 2 poles

●Details

“10,000 Yen Poles” will be placed among some of the poles within the event area. When you photograph one, a rainbow treasure chest appears and dispenses reward coins.

●Prize

120,000 reward coins (worth 10,000 yen) × 20 poles

“1,000 Yen Pole”

●Placement Period

Sat, Aug 29, 5:30 – Sun, Nov 15, 17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

●Locations

Miyagi, Fukushima, Iwate, Aomori, Yamagata, Akita, Niigata, and Nagano Prefectures

●Details

“1,000 Yen Poles” will be placed among some of the poles within the event area. When you photograph one, a rainbow treasure chest appears and dispenses reward coins.

●Prize

12,000 reward coins (worth 1,000 yen) will be dispensed at random from two poles each day.

◼️What Are Treasure Poles?

Treasure Poles are special utility poles funded by companies or individuals who wish to support their local communities, with those funds returned to participants as rewards. Treasure Poles are hidden among the poles eligible for photography in Grid Grab: Capture the Current Regular Season. Participants can earn rewards by finding and photographing these Treasure Poles among the many poles out there.
