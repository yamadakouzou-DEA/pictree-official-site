“PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” Eastern Hokkaido Bonus Rewards Campaign

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, September 5, 2026, we will launch the “Eastern Hokkaido Bonus Rewards Campaign” in “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer”, which increases photography rewards in the eastern Hokkaido area centered on Obihiro, Kushiro, and Kitami.

◼️Overview of the “Eastern Hokkaido Bonus Rewards Campaign”

●Campaign Period

Saturday, September 5, 2026, 5:00 – Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (national holiday), 17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

*The campaign may end without prior notice.

●Shooting Hours

September: 5:00–18:00 (JST / UTC+9)

October: 6:00–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

November: 6:00–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

●How to Participate

From Events at the bottom left of the game’s TOP screen, select “Eastern Hokkaido Bonus Rewards Campaign”.

Alternatively, switch the season at the top left of the TOP screen to “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer”, then look for utility poles marked with the reward bonus icon shown below and photograph them.

<Icon indicating a reward bonus>

●Target Area

The eastern Hokkaido area centered on Obihiro, Kushiro, and Kitami

*Assets already photographed in “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” are not eligible for the reward bonus.

*In some areas, there may be assets that are not eligible for the bonus.

●Locations of Eligible Utility Poles

The utility poles shown on the heatmap below are the eligible poles.

The color coding is as follows. Please use it as a reference.

Pink: get 240 reward coins (equivalent to 20 yen)

Blue: get 120 reward coins (equivalent to 10 yen)

●Rewards

In the target area, poles worth 48 reward coins (equivalent to 4 yen) are increased to 120 reward coins (equivalent to 10 yen), and poles worth 96 reward coins (equivalent to 8 yen) are increased to 240 reward coins (equivalent to 20 yen).

Poles in urban areas are set to relatively lower rewards, while poles in suburban areas are set higher. You can identify each pole’s photography reward by the number shown on the screen below (the “XX coins GET” part above “Take a photo”).
