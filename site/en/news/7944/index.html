“PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” Group Formation Campaign Rewards Increased!! & PicTrée Official Community Board Now Open!!

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

For the “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” Group Formation Campaign, which has been running since Saturday, August 8, 2026, we are increasing the reward from the equivalent of 100 yen per person to the equivalent of 2,000 yen!

We have also launched the PicTrée Official Community Board, which you can use to recruit members and share information.

If you have not formed a group yet, be sure to look for teammates on the board!

【”PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” Join Together! Group Formation Campaign Details】

If you form a group of five and clear the participation conditions before the end of the “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” season, every member of the group will receive reward coins worth the equivalent of 2,000 yen.

◼️How to Participate

①Fill in the required information on the form.

②All members of the group photograph 100 or more poles in “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer”.

*Groups that have already entered do not need to submit the form again.

*The form should be submitted by one representative of the group.

*If even one member has not photographed 100 or more poles, the group is not eligible for the reward.

◼️Rewards

If all five members of the group have photographed 100 or more poles as of the end of the season, every member of the group will receive 24,000 reward coins (equivalent to 2,000 yen).

*Scheduled reward distribution date: late November

◼️Important Notes

・Photo counts will be tallied based on the status after the season ends.

・Even if the group had five members when the form was submitted, it will not be eligible for the reward if it has only four members at the end of the season.

・Photo counts are based on the number of photos that passed review.

・If the form is not filled in correctly, we will not be able to grant the reward.

・Each person can receive the reward for this campaign only once.

・If fraud is suspected, the reward will not be granted.

・This campaign may be changed or cancelled without prior notice.

◼️Entry Form

Submission deadline: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (national holiday), 23:59 (JST / UTC+9)

https://forms.gle/1ACsEwJPsnhv1vma8
