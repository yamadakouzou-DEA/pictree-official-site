Announcing “Saitama City Everyone’s Photo Hunt: Capture the Shopping Street Lights!”

Thank you for playing PicTrée.
Starting Friday, October 9, 2026, we will hold a new season, “Saitama City Everyone’s Photo Hunt: Capture the Shopping Street Lights!”, featuring street lights in Saitama City as shooting targets, in collaboration with “Saitama City Minna no App,” a citizen app operated by Tsunagu Co., Ltd., a regional trading company in Saitama.

In this season, in addition to PicTrée Reward Coins, you can earn an equivalent amount in “TamaPon,” the points of “Saitama City Minna no App.” For details, please see “2. How to Get TamaPon” below.

1. “Saitama City Everyone’s Photo Hunt: Capture the Shopping Street Lights!” Season Details

【Season Period and Playable Hours】

◼️Season Period

Friday, October 9, 2026, 9:00 – Wednesday, November 4, 2026, 17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️Playable Hours

・Shooting: 9:00–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)
・Check-in: No time limit

【How to Participate】

Open “Switch Season” from the top left of the PicTrée TOP screen, and select “Saitama City Everyone’s Photo Hunt: Capture the Shopping Street Lights!” from the list to participate.

Choose one of “Nuu Red,” “Nuu Blue,” or “Nuu Yellow,” and compete against the other teams as a member of that team.
・The color shown when you photograph and capture a target changes according to your team’s color.
・Once selected, your team cannot be changed during the season, so choose carefully.

【Shooting Targets, Angles, and Shooting Rewards】

◼️Shooting Targets and Angles

● Shooting Target

・Shopping street lights

● Shooting Angles

・Top

・Bottom

・Full view *1

・Base *1

・Shopping street (association) plate *1

*1) If you can photograph the target angle, select “Yes” and take the photo using the “Take Photo” button. If you cannot, select “No.”

・Abnormalities (*no photo required)

If there is any abnormality with the street light, select and check the applicable items from the abnormality inspection options (multiple selections allowed).

◼️Shooting Rewards

*2) Additional shooting rewards may be added depending on the progress of the season.

*You will earn “TamaPon” points on the “Saitama City Minna no App” equivalent to the value of your reward coins.
For details, please see [2. How to Get “TamaPon”] below.

【Request to Report When Shooting Is Difficult】

If there is a problem with the shooting target, or if shooting is difficult due to the surrounding environment or the condition of the target, please do not force yourself to take photos and instead report it using the reporting function.

Please see below for how to report.

◼️How to Report

① Tap the icon of the target asset

② Tap the megaphone icon at the top right

③ Check the problem occurring with the shooting target and tap “Next”

④ If you have a helpful photo, take it with the “Take Photo” button, then tap “Send”

【Event Area and Heatmap】

◼️Event Area

Saitama City

◼️Heatmap

【Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)】

These rewards are granted based on the results of the team-based ranking competition held each season.

This ranking consists of a “Team Ranking” and an “Individual Ranking within Team,” and the rewards you receive at the end of the season are determined by the team and individual ranking results at that time.

*You will earn “TamaPon” points on the “Saitama City Minna no App” equivalent to the value of your reward coins.
For details, please see [2. How to Get “TamaPon”] below.

(Unit: Reward Coins)

【Exchanging Reward Coins】

Reward Coins can be exchanged for “Amazon Gift Cards,” “Eraberu Pay®,” or “giftee Box®.”

The relationship between the exchange amount and the required Reward Coins is as follows.

*Exchanges can be made from Menu at the top right of the main screen > Profile > Exchange > Select Exchange Destination.

◼️Exchange for Amazon Gift Cards

*Exchanges can be made in units of 1,200 coins.

*Up to 10,000 yen can be exchanged per request.

◼️Exchange for “Eraberu Pay®” and “giftee Box®”

*Exchanges can be made in units of 1,350 coins.

*Up to 10,000 points can be exchanged per request.

Details: https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/news/5054/

2. How to Get TamaPon

“TamaPon” is a digital local currency that can be used in “Saitama City Minna no App.”

By playing PicTrée and applying to participate (answering a survey) in “Saitama City Minna no App,” you can receive “TamaPon” equivalent to the Reward Coins (shooting rewards + ranking rewards) you earned during this season.

*1 TamaPon point can be used as 1 yen.

*For details about “TamaPon,” please check the official website of “Saitama City Minna no App.”

◼️TamaPon to Be Awarded

We will award “TamaPon” equivalent to the Reward Coins (shooting rewards + ranking rewards) you earned during this season.

◼️Application Period

Friday, October 9 – Wednesday, November 11

◼️Scheduled Award Date

Monday, November 30 *Awarded within Saitama City Minna no App

◼️How to Apply

Answer the survey in “Saitama City Minna no App”

① Open “Saitama City Minna no App” and tap “もっと見る (See More)” on the home screen

② Tap “アンケート (Survey)”

③ Tap “商店街の街路灯撮影でたまポン獲得！ ピクトレ参加申込 (Get TamaPon by Photographing Shopping Street Lights! PicTrée Participation Application)”

④ Enter the required items in the survey, including your PicTrée Player ID (see below for how to check it), and tap “回答する (Submit)”

⑤ Tap “OK” to complete!

◼️How to Check Your PicTrée Player ID

① Tap the menu bar at the top right of the map screen in the PicTrée app

② Tap “Settings”

③ Tap the copy button to the right of your Player ID to copy it
