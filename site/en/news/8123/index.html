“PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” Eastern Hokkaido Frontier Challenge 2026 in Kushiro & Nemuro

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, October 17, 2026, we will launch the “Eastern Hokkaido Frontier Challenge 2026 in Kushiro & Nemuro” in “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer”, covering the eastern Hokkaido area centered on Kushiro and Nemuro.

Depending on how many poles you photograph in the target area, you will receive a campaign reward in addition to the usual photography rewards.

There are 11 milestones, from 100 poles up to 2,000 poles — earn up to the equivalent of 30,000 yen!

◼️”Eastern Hokkaido Frontier Challenge 2026 in Kushiro & Nemuro” Overview

●Campaign Period

Saturday, October 17, 2026, 6:00 – Tuesday, November 3, 2026, 17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

*The campaign may end without prior notice.

●Shooting Hours

6:00–17:00 (JST / UTC+9)

●How to Participate

Switch the season at the top left of the TOP screen to “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer”, then look for utility poles and photograph them. (No entry required)

You can earn campaign rewards according to the number of poles you photograph.

●Target Area

Kushiro City, Kushiro Town, Akkeshi Town, Hamanaka Town, Shibecha Town, Teshikaga Town, Tsurui Village, Shiranuka Town, Nemuro City, Betsukai Town, Nakashibetsu Town, Shibetsu Town, Rausu Town

*Assets already photographed in “PicTrée Grid Grab in Hokkaido 2026 Summer” are not eligible.

●Rewards

●Scheduled Reward Distribution Date

Mid to late November

◼️Our shared goal: 15,000 poles!

The goal is a combined total of 15,000 poles from all participants.

The more poles are photographed, the more the unexplored areas of the map open up.

We plan to post the progress on X every week. Be sure to check it out!
