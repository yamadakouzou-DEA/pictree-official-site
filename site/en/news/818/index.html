About Access to the Fujimi Area for Season 5

Event Area for Season 5:
In PicTrée Season 5, which runs from June 15th to June 29th, the target for photography will be utility poles located in the Fujimi Area, spanning from the foothills of Mount Akagi to the surrounding areas of Fujimi.

For those playing in Season 5, it is recommended to use ① Roadside Station Fujimi (Fujimi Onsen) and ② Roadside Station Maebashi Akagi as gathering points or bases, as both locations offer ample parking facilities. These stations can serve as convenient spots to meet up, strategize, or relax before and after your gameplay sessions.

 

▼Overall map for Season 5

 

Access for Event Area:
①Roadside Station Fujimi

Source:https://www.maebashi-cvb.com/spot/3002

 

Roadside Station Fujimi is equipped with a spacious parking area and is recommended as a rest stop. It features the “富士見温泉見晴らしの湯ふれあい館” hot spring facility, the “風ラインふじみ農産物直売所” selling local produce, and the “ふれあい公園,” where children can play freely.

 

Facilities:Parking: 206 spaces, including 5 for large vehicles/Restrooms/Vending machines/Shops/Bicycle rentals
Address:1 Fujimi-machi Akagisan, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture 371-0101
Phone Number:027-235-2211

 

●Directions by Car:
・From Maebashi Station: Approximately 25 minutes via Prefectural Road 4 https://maps.app.goo.gl/kRrx371DUyQA9CiH9
・From Komagata Interchange on the North Kanto Expressway: Approximately 30 minutes towards National Route 353
・From Akagi Interchange on the Kanetsu Expressway: Approximately 20 minutes on National Route 353

 

●For Those Using Public Transportation:
・From JR Maebashi Station: Take the Kanetsu Kotsu bus towards Fujimi Onsen for about 30 minutes, get off at the “Fujimi Onsen” stop
・Fare: 700 yen https://maps.app.goo.gl/HWUJiC1ZdMvoESry7

 

■About Electric Bicycle Rentals:
At Roadside Station Fujimi (Fujimi Onsen), electric bicycles are available for rent.
If you are considering renting an electric bicycle, please download the Maebashi Share Cycle App “cogbe” in advance. https://interstreet.jp/maebashi-cogbe/
 

②Roadside Station Maebashi Akagi

 

Roadside Station Maebashi Akagi is equipped with a spacious parking lot and clean restrooms, and also features a bathing facility. Additionally, it offers a variety of limited-edition souvenirs. In the food court, you can enjoy local delicacies such as roast beef bowls made with Akagi beef and grilled manju soft-serve.
Although slightly outside of the Season 5 area, it is recommended to travel to Roadside Station Maebashi Akagi by car or public transport and then explore the area by bicycle, as the tourist information center there offers electric assist sport bicycle rentals (e-Bikes).
*The distance between Roadside Station Maebashi Akagi and Roadside Station Fujimi is approximately 5.6 km. If traveling by electric bicycle, it should take about 15 to 20 minutes.

 

・Address: 36 Taguchi-machi, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture 371-0048
・Website: https://maebashi-akagi.jp/
・Phone Number: 027-233-0070
・Facilities: Parking lot, restrooms, vending machines, bicycle rental

 

●Directions by Car (Using Expressways):

From the Kanetsu Expressway, it takes about 10 minutes from the Shibukawa-Ikaho Interchange (IC). If you are coming from Tokyo or Nerima IC, the distance is approximately 110 km, which takes about 85 minutes.
It is also about 10 minutes from the Komayose Smart Interchange (IC) on the Kanetsu Expressway.

●Introduction to Tourist Spots:
▼富士見温泉 見晴らしの湯 ふれあい館 (next to Roadside Station Fujimi)

Source:https://www.maebashi-cvb.com/spot/1098

 

“富士見温泉見晴らしの湯ふれあい館” is a day-trip hot spring facility located along National Route 353, which runs east-west mid-slope on Mount Akagi, adjacent to the Roadside Station “Fujimi”. It’s a perfect place to relax during your trip to the sights of Mount Akagi or in between games of PicTrée.

 

・Address: 1569-1 Ishii, Fujimi-machi, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture 371-0105
・Website：https://peraichi.com/landing_pages/view/fujimionsen
・Phone Number: 027-230-5555
・Facilities: Parking, restrooms, vending machines, snack bar

 

▼珊瑚寺

Source:https://www.maebashi-cvb.com/spot/1073

 

石井山珊瑚寺, located in Fujimi-machi, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture, is a 天台宗 temple blessed with abundant nature and seasonal flowers, including cherry blossoms.

 

・Address: 1227 Ishii, Fujimi-machi, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture 371-0105
・Official Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/isii_sangoji/?hl=ja
・Phone Number: 027-288-3503
・Facilities: Parking area

 

▼世界の名犬牧場

 

世界の名犬牧場 is a “Dog Community Park” designed to deepen the bond between dogs and people, facilitate enjoyable interactions among visitors, and help dogs learn socialization. The park includes a cafe, shop, and pet hotel, and even offers a “Petting Area” where people who don’t own pets can interact with their favorite dogs.

 

・Address: 2252-1 Ishii, Fujimi-machi, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture 371-0105
・Website：https://www.meiken-bokujou.com/
・Phone Number: 027-288-1661
・Facilities: Parking, restrooms, vending machines, cafe

 

＊Special Edition (Recommended Spot Outside the Area):

▼ベイシア前橋ふじみモール店

Source: https://www.gunlabo.net/shop/shop.shtml?s=5362

 

This supermarket, located near the southern part of the Season 5 area, primarily focuses on food products. It features a spacious parking lot and includes a drugstore and a dollar store on the premises, making it a convenient location for shopping needs while visiting the area.

 

・Address: 643 Haranogo, Fujimi-machi, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture 371-0116
・Website：https://map.beisia.co.jp/map/185
・Phone Number: 027-288-1511
・Facilities: Supermarket, parking, restrooms

 

▼Gallery Art Soup

 

Located just a 6-minute walk from Maebashi Station, Art Soup is a free-entry art gallery where PicTrée items are available. Look for the large glass doors and the Art Soup flag as landmarks!
Additionally, from June 15 (Saturday) to July 14 (Sunday), a limited-time mini-exhibition event featuring Art Soup and Kamui-Verse will take place. You can view various exhibits, including Kamui-Verse concept art.

 

・Address: 2-20-16 Omotecho, Maebashi, Gunma Prefecture 371-0024
・Website: https://galleryartsoup.wixsite.com/galleryartsoup
・Phone Number: 027-896-9320
・Facilities: PicTrée items, restrooms
Note: There is no dedicated parking available. Please use nearby coin parking facilities.

 

▼赤城山百年古民家 初雪堂（cafe）

 

This Japanese cafe, located along Akagi Prefectural Road, is a renovated traditional house that is over 100 years old. The cafe retains the charm of the historic house with its interior and exterior offering plenty to see. The menu includes meals such as roast beef bowls, as well as sweets like fluffy shaved ice and pancakes.

 

Although slightly outside of the Season 5 area, this cafe is a great spot for lunch or a break. How about giving it a try?
