First Time in Oita! “PicTrée Hot Spring Hopping Season in Oita” Season Details & Friend Referral Campaign

Thank you for always playing PicTrée.
Starting Saturday, October 31, 2026, we will hold a new season, “PicTrée Hot Spring Hopping Season in Oita” — PicTrée’s first-ever season in Oita Prefecture.

In this article, we’ll share the details of “PicTrée Hot Spring Hopping Season in Oita” and the Friend Referral Campaign.

1. “PicTrée Hot Spring Hopping Season in Oita” Details

[Season Period and Playable Hours]

◼️Season Period

Saturday, October 31, 2026 – Sunday, November 29, 2026

◼️Playable Hours

●Photo-taking: 6:30–17:30 (JST / UTC+9)

●Check-in: No restrictions

[How to Participate]

Open “Switch Season” from the top left of the PicTrée TOP screen, then select “Oita Season” from the list to join.

At the start of the season, choose one of three teams (“Ampere,” “Volt,” or “Watt”), and compete against the other teams for scores as a member of that team.

[Event Area]

◼️Event Area

Oita City, Usuki City, Beppu City, Kokonoe Town (Kusu District), Saiki City, Kunisaki City, Bungotakada City, Tsukumi City, Hita City, Hiji Town (Hayami District), Yufu City, Bungo-ono City, Kitsuki City, and Buzen City (Fukuoka Prefecture)

◼️Heatmap

The heatmap is color-coded by reward amount.

The color coding is as follows. Please use it as a reference.

・Blue: 360 reward coins (equivalent to ¥30)

・Green: 720 reward coins (equivalent to ¥60)

・Orange: 1,200 reward coins (equivalent to ¥100)

[Photo Targets and Shooting Angles]

◼️Photo Targets

・Utility pole (concrete pole)

・Utility pole (steel pipe pole)

・Shared pole

・Lucky utility pole (concrete pole)

・Lucky utility pole (steel pipe pole)

・Lucky shared pole

◼️Shooting Angles

<Common to all assets>

・Full view

・Number plate

・Upper half (front, right side, left side)

・Lower half (front, right side, left side)

・Wires (right side, left side)

・Guy wire (metal part) ①②③ ※Optional angles

(10–13 angles in total)

[Please Report When Photo-taking Is Difficult]

If there is a problem with a photo target, or if it is difficult to take photos due to the surrounding environment or the condition of the target, please do not force yourself to take photos. Instead, please submit a report using the report function.

Please see below for how to report.

◼️How to Report

① Tap the icon of the target asset

② Tap the megaphone icon at the top right

③ Check the problem occurring with the photo target and tap “Next”

④ If you have a helpful photo, take it with the “Take Photo” button, then tap “Send”

[Photo Rewards]

※Once a reward has been earned from a photo target, no further photo rewards can be earned from it.

※Lucky utility poles (concrete pole / steel pipe pole) and lucky shared poles each have rewards set in two tiers. You can check the photo reward for each pole by the number displayed on the screen below (the “〇〇 Coins GET” part above “Take Photo”).

2. “Friend Referral Campaign” Details

This season, we are holding a Friend Referral Campaign.

Please refer friends and family who haven’t played PicTrée yet. If the referred person newly installs the app during the campaign period and photographs 50 or more utility poles in “PicTrée Hot Spring Hopping Season in Oita,” both the referrer and the referred person will receive reward coins.

Referrers themselves don’t need to take photos in Oita!!
There is no limit on the number of people you can refer, so please refer as many friends and family members who might be able to visit Oita as possible!

◼️Campaign Period
“PicTrée Hot Spring Hopping Season in Oita”
Saturday, October 31, 2026 – Sunday, November 29, 2026

◼️How to Participate
Please submit the player names and player IDs of both the referrer and the referred person via the form below.
※Either the referrer or the referred person may submit the application (there is no need for both to apply).

<<Application Form>>

◼️Conditions
・Referrer: Someone who installed the PicTrée app and played it at least once before the referred person
・Referred person: Someone who newly installs the PicTrée app and plays for the first time during the campaign period
・The referred person must photograph 50 or more utility poles in Oita during the target season

◼️Rewards
If the referred person photographs 50 or more poles, both the referrer and the referred person will receive 6,000 reward coins (equivalent to ¥500).
※The number of photos is counted based on photos that have passed review.

<Reward Distribution Date>
Scheduled for early December

◼️Notes
・Referrers do not need to take photos in Oita.
・There is no limit on the number of people you can refer.
・A referred person can receive the reward only once (if applications are submitted by multiple referrers, only the first application is valid).
・Those who installed the app before the campaign period are not eligible as referred persons.
・Rewards will not be granted if fraud is suspected.
・This campaign may be changed or canceled without notice.
