A First for a Subway! “Tokyo Metro: Let’s Watch Over Our Stations Together! ~Photograph the Lights and Signs!~ 1” Event Announcement

Thank you for playing PicTrée.

Starting Saturday, November 14, 2026, in collaboration with Tokyo Metro, we will hold the event “Tokyo Metro: Let’s Watch Over Our Stations Together! ~Photograph the Lights and Signs!~ 1” at three stations on the Yurakucho Line (Ginza-itchome, Toyosu, and Shin-kiba). The shooting targets are the hanging signs inside the stations (the shooting angles also include the lighting).

1. What Is “Tokyo Metro: Let’s Watch Over Our Stations Together! ~Photograph the Lights and Signs!~”?

This is an event in which you photograph hanging signs and lighting inside Tokyo Metro stations.

The photos you take will be used to detect minor abnormalities in the facilities at an early stage and to make inspection work more efficient.

[About the Event Rounds]

The “Tokyo Metro: Let’s Watch Over Our Stations Together! ~Photograph the Lights and Signs!~” event is divided into rounds of a set period, and rankings are tallied for each round.

After each round ends, you can earn rewards based on your ranking.

In addition, the capture status is reset after each round ends, and a new round begins.

〈Event Schedule〉

① Saturday, November 14, 2026 – Friday, November 27, 2026

② Saturday, November 28, 2026 – Friday, December 11, 2026

③ Saturday, December 12, 2026 – Friday, December 25, 2026

④ Saturday, December 26, 2026 – Sunday, January 17, 2027

[About Rewards]

The rewards available through this event are Shooting Rewards, Metpo Rewards, Ranking Rewards, and Lottery Rewards.

The amounts of Shooting Rewards, Metpo Rewards, and Lottery Rewards are scheduled to change for each round.

For details on Metpo Rewards, please see “Metpo Rewards” under [Rewards] below.

This article covers the details of Round ①. Details for Rounds ② to ④ will be announced at a later date.

2. “Tokyo Metro: Let’s Watch Over Our Stations Together! ~Photograph the Lights and Signs!~ 1” Details

[Event Period and Play Hours]

◼️Event Period

Saturday, November 14, 2026, 10:00 – Friday, November 27, 2026, 16:00 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️Shooting Hours

10:00–16:00 (JST / UTC+9)

[Event Area]

Yurakucho Line: Ginza-itchome Station, Toyosu Station, and Shin-kiba Station

[How to Participate]

Tap “Event” at the bottom left of the game TOP screen and select “Tokyo Metro: Let’s Watch Over Our Stations Together! ~Photograph the Lights and Signs!~ 1” to join.

In this event, location information is not used inside stations, so your position will not be shown on the in-station map.

Check the positions of the target assets on the map for each station floor and take your photos.

◼️Steps to Take Photos

① Select the target station

② Tap the Tokyo Metro icon on the map

③ Check the target facilities on the in-station map

You can select the floor from the top right of the shooting map.

Go to the floor shown (or display the floor you are on).

Check the location of the target asset and tap its icon.

④ Check the photo example and take your photo

[Shooting Target and Angles]

◼️Shooting Target

・Hanging signs

◼️Shooting Angles

・Hanging sign

・Ceiling (front)

・Ceiling (back)

・Ceiling (left)

・Ceiling (right)

*Please hold your smartphone horizontally (landscape orientation) for all angles.

*The ceiling angles are used to inspect the lighting for abnormalities, so please shoot from directly below the hanging sign so that the lighting in front of, behind, and to the left and right of it is visible.

[Requests for Participants]

・For requests regarding conduct inside stations, please check “Requests from Tokyo Metro.” (https://www.tokyometro.jp/safety/attention/index.html)

・Tokyo Metro does not offer platform/admission tickets, so you cannot enter the ticket gates solely for the purpose of shooting without using the railway.

・Restrooms, stairs, elevators, escalators, and platforms are outside the shooting area.

・Please do not operate your phone while walking, obstruct traffic, or photograph other passengers or staff without permission.

・Please refrain from contacting station staff about the event.

[Handling of Personal Information]

・Photo data and application form responses will be handled in accordance with Tokyo Metro’s Personal Information Protection Policy.

・Photo data collected in this event will not be used for any purpose other than verifying its potential use for maintaining station facilities.

・Photos are taken and sent within the PicTrée app, and no data is stored on participants’ own devices.

・All photo data is encrypted when stored.

・All data is encrypted during communication.

・Photo data will be checked by GRG and DEA, the operators of PicTrée, for images of other passengers or images unrelated to the shooting targets, and then acquired and stored by Tokyo Metro.

・We take necessary security measures such as encryption when storing and transmitting photo data to prevent information leaks.

・This event is conducted with GRG and DEA agreeing to Tokyo Metro’s Personal Information Protection Policy, and photo data obtained in this event, including temporary checking and storage by GRG and DEA, will be handled in accordance with that policy.

・Personal information obtained through PicTrée app user registration, etc., will be handled in accordance with the privacy policy set by the service provider.

<Reference: Tokyo Metro Personal Information Protection Policy>
