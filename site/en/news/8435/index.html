[PicTrée Disaster Preparedness Championship 2026] Rules on Where You Can Take Photos, and the Measures Starting with the November 16 Tally

Thank you for taking part in the PicTrée Disaster Preparedness Championship 2026.

As we work toward finalizing the tournament results, we would like to share the rules on where you can take photos once again, together with the measures we will apply starting with the weekly tally on Monday, November 16.

■ Rules on where you can take photos (the same as those posted on the How to Play page)

・You may photograph facilities that can be photographed from places anyone can pass through, such as public roads and parks.

・Facilities that cannot be photographed without entering the premises of shops, condominiums, companies, schools, and similar places, as well as facilities inside buildings, are not eligible.

・Even if a facility is inside the premises, it is fine as long as it can be photographed from outside, such as from a public road.

■ Measures starting with the weekly tally on Monday, November 16

Records whose positions are registered inside the premises or buildings of the following facilities on the map data will be excluded from the tournament scoring.

・Commercial facilities and shops

・Factories, workplaces, and offices

・Schools and hospitals

■ Which records are affected, and how they are excluded

・The same criteria are applied to all records — the Discovery Event from August 1 onward and the Evacuation Drill from September 1 onward.

・Judgments are made mechanically by matching against map data, and the same criteria are applied to all participants.

・The records are excluded from the tournament scoring (city scores, rankings, and awards such as Disaster Preparedness King). Your in-game records will not be deleted.

■ Why we are taking this measure

We have heard from participants that they want a tournament in which those who take part seriously and follow the rules are rewarded. This measure is intended to make the final results something everyone can accept.

Until the tournament closes on November 30, please continue to enjoy exploring your town safely and in line with the rules.

PicTrée Operation Team
