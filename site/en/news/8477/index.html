PicTrée Update (v3.5.0 / v3.6.0) Announcement

We released a PicTrée feature update (v3.5.0) on Friday, October 30, 2026.

We will also release a PicTrée feature update (v3.6.0) on Friday, November 13, 2026.

The update includes the following.

[v3.5.0 Update Details] (Released on October 30, 2026)

◼️Addition of the “Retake” feature

After you submit a photo, you can now retake it while it is still under review.

A “Retake” button is displayed on the photos you have taken.

If you want to shoot again — for example, you captured the wrong angle or the photo came out blurry — tap “Retake” and take the photo again.

① Tap the menu bar at the top right of the Home screen

② Tap “Review Status”

③ Tap the photo you want to retake (only photos marked “Under Review” can be retaken)

④ Tap the corresponding asset icon

⑤ Tap the “Retake” button

⑥ Check the notes, then tap “Retake”

⑦ Take the photo again using the “Shoot” button

※ Please note that photos whose review has been completed, and which are marked as shot, cannot be retaken.

◼️Other

We made minor improvements and fixed minor bugs.

[v3.6.0 Update Details] (Released on November 13, 2026)

◼️Discontinuation of paid coin sales

Sales of paid coins will end with v3.6.0.

They will no longer appear in the shop’s product list.

Paid coins you already own can still be used as before.

They will not disappear or become unusable, so please rest assured.

◼️Other

We made minor improvements and fixed minor bugs.
