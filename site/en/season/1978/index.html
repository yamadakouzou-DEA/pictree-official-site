Season Information: Numazu Season 3

Here are the details of Numazu Season 3, held in collaboration with Love Live! Sunshine!! in Numazu City, Shizuoka Prefecture.

 

Duration:
March 8, 2025 (Saturday) 9:00 AM to March 21, 2025 (Friday) 4:00 PM

 

Gameplay Hours:
Photography: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
Check-in: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
*Note: The app can be launched outside of these hours, and activities other than “Photography” and “Check-in” are possible.

 

Areas Covered:Ooka, Kanuki, Shizuura, Ohira Area

 

About the Collaboration with Love Live! Sunshine!!:
During the Numazu Season, you can enjoy various collaborative elements with “Love Live! Sunshine!!” within the PicTrée.
For more details about the collaboration, please check the following article:
https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/news/1928/

Ranking Rewards:
In PicTrée, rankings are determined on a seasonal basis with both “Team Rankings” and “Individual Rankings” within each team. The rewards you can earn at the end of the season are based on these rankings at the season’s close.

 

・Coefficient for Team Points Calculation:
Team rankings are determined by the total team points, which are calculated from the cumulative rank points of all players within each team. However, for the calculation of team points, a specific coefficient is applied to the cumulative rank points of the top-ranking players in each team, as detailed in the table below.

Ranking within the Team Coefficient for Individual Rankings within the Team
1st 2
2nd 1.8
3rd 1.6
4th 1.4
5th 1.2

〇Exchange for Exclusive Merchandise
Reward coins can be exchanged for exclusive collaboration merchandise with Love Live! Sunshine!!.
To exchange, you need to visit the Numazu Tourist Information Center located on the 2nd floor of Antre South Exit at JR Numazu Station.


 

 

【”NUMAZU 100 TREASURES” Photography Mission】
Missions will be available at selected spots in Numazu City that have been chosen as part of the “NUMAZU 100 TREASURES.” By participating in these missions and capturing images of the designated “NUMAZU 100 TREASURES” locations, players can earn mission rewards.
※ For more details on “NUMAZU 100 TREASURES,” visit: https://www.city.numazu.shizuoka.jp/takara100/

【Disaster Prevention Mission】
The “Disaster Prevention Mission” is a special mission that occurs irregularly.
By capturing designated disaster prevention-related equipment within the specified period, you can earn rewards.

 

・Disaster prevention-related equipment to be photographed:
View-O (Large Observation Water Gate)
TSUNAMI Evacuation Tower
Fire Brigade Station
Fire Hydrant
Evacuation Site Sign

 

・Disaster Prevention Mission period: March 8, 2025 (Sat) 9:00 – April 27, 2025 (Sun) 16:00
