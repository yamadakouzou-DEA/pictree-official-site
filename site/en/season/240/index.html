●Photography Rewards: In Season 3, in addition to the initial photography reward, you can also earn a reward for the second photography of the same electrical facility.

①First-time Photography Reward (for capturing power equipment that has not been photographed by anyone else and passes review):
・400 coins per utility pole

②Second-time Photography Reward (for neutralizing power equipment that has been photographed once before and capturing it again after it passes review):
・100 coins per utility pole

*Note: The power equipment eligible for the rewards (① and ②) will be indicated by “撮影報酬あり” above the “撮影する” button. Conversely, equipment not marked does not offer a photography reward.
*There are no rewards for the third or subsequent captures.
