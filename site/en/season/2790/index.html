Season Information: Operation Vines in Chiba1

We’re excited to share the details of PicTrée’s new season, Operation Vines in Chiba, held in Chiba prefectures.

 

[Season Overview]
The main goal of this season is the early detection of vines and ivy (collectively referred to as “Vines”) growing around utility poles. A special “Vine Boost Period” will be set during the season. If you photograph a pole that actually has vines on it during this period, you will receive a “Vine Discovery Bonus” in addition to the standard reward.

 

[Season Period]
April 26 (Sat) 9:00 – July 4 (Fri) 17:00
(Vine Boost Period: April 26 (Sat) 9:00 – May 18 (Sun) 17:00)

 

[Game Play Time]
・Photography: 6:00 – 17:00
  *The start time on the first day is 9:00.
・Check-in: 6:00 – 17:00

 

[Event Areas]
Chiba City, Ichihara City, Yotsukaido City (Chiba Pref.)

 

[Photography Targets]
・Poles
・Pole (Vine-Prone): In this season, some poles are designated as “Pole (Vine-Prone)” where vine growth is more likely. These poles have unique icons different from standard poles, offer higher rewards, and come with special effects within the game.

 

<Pole (Vine-Prone) Icon>

 

<Features of Pole (Vine-Prone)>
・Up to 5 connections via Pole Connect
・Utility Pole HP: 5,000

 

[How to Report Vines]
In this season, before sending your photo, you will be asked to select whether vines are present or not.
If you find vines during your photo shoot, please check where the tip of the vine reaches on the utility pole and select the appropriate option.
If there are no vines, select “No.” (You must select one of the options in order to send your image.)

*The “Vine Discovery Reward” is only granted for the first-time capture of a utility pole. Subsequent captures of the same pole are not eligible.
*Please note that not all “Pole (Vine-Prone)” will have vines actually attached.
*If you find vines but select “No” in the vine reporting section before sending the image, the “Vine Discovery Reward” will not be granted.
*Please do not select “Yes” if there are no visible vines. Repeated false reports may result in account suspension.

 

Also, since the “Vine Discovery Reward” is granted after review by the operations team, it will not be issued immediately after the image is submitted like the standard photo reward. The reward will be distributed on the following scheduled dates during the season:
