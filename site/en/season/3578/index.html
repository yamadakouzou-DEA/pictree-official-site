Season Information: Operation Vines in Chiba2

[Season Features]
The main objective of this season is the early detection of instances where plants, such as vines, have wrapped around poles. If you capture a photo of a pole with actual vine entanglement during the season, you will be awarded the “Vine Discovery Bonus” in addition to the usual reward.

 

[Duration]
Operation Vines in Chiba②: Saturday, July 5, 9:00 AM – Friday, August 29, 6:00 PM
This season does not have a “Vine Boost Period”; you can obtain the “Vine Discovery Bonus” throughout the entire season.

 

[Game Play Times]
・Photography: 5:00 AM – 6:00 PM
・Check-in: 5:00 AM – 6:00 PM
 ※On the first day of each season, the start time will be 9:00 AM.

 

[Event Areas]
Operation Vines in Chiba②: Chiba City, Ichihara City, Yotsukaido City (Chiba Prefecture)

 

[Subjects for Photography]
・Poles
・Pole (Vine-Prone): In this season, certain poles are designated as “Pole (Vine-Prone)” where vines are more likely to be wrapped. These poles have unique icons and special effects in the game.
※The photography reward is the same as for regular poles.

<Features of Pole (Vine-Prone)>
・Up to 5 connections via Pole Connect
・Pole HP: 5,000

 

[How to Report Vines]
In this season, you can select whether vines are present when submitting your photo. If you spot vines, confirm the position of the vine’s tip on the pole and select the corresponding option.
If you select “Yes,” you can take one additional photo. If you feel that the vines aren’t clearly captured in the existing image, you can take an additional photo and send it (choose an angle that clearly shows the vines).
If no vines are present, select “No” and send.

There are two types of rankings: a Team Ranking and an Individual Ranking within each team.
At the end of each season, rewards are granted based on the final results of both the team and individual rankings.
