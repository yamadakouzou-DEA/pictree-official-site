[Season Ranking Rewards]
PicTrée features a team-based ranking battle system each season, with teams named Bolt, Ampere, and Watt.
Rankings include both overall team performance and individual rankings within each team. Rewards are granted at the end of the season based on these results.

 

There are two types of season ranking rewards:
・Overall Rewards: Based on both team ranking and individual ranking within your team
・Area-Based Rewards: Based solely on individual ranking within your team by region (team rank does not affect this)
