Season Information: Operation Vines in Saitama②

【Season Features】

In this season, the main targets are utility poles that were not photographed during “Operation Vines in Saitama.”
To promote photographing these unphotographed poles, the “areas” within each season have been subdivided more finely than before. If you place 1st in an area ranking, you will receive 36,000 reward coins (equivalent to JPY 3,000)!
Focus on the areas, capture the remaining unphotographed poles, and aim for 1st place in the area rankings!!

 

【Season Period and Playable Time】

◼ Season Period

・Sep 13 (Sat) 9:00 – Oct 12 (Sun) 18:00
※ There is no “Vines Boost Period” this season. You will have the chance to earn “Vines Discovery Rewards” throughout the entire season.

 

◼ Playable Time

・Photography: 5:00–18:00
・Check-in: 5:00–18:00
※ On the first day of each season (Sat, Sep 13), gameplay starts at 9:00.

【How to Report Vines】

For this season, before sending the image, you will select whether vine growth is present.
If you find vines while photographing, check where the vine tips are located on the pole and select the corresponding option.
If you select “Yes,” you can take one additional photo. If the already-taken image does not clearly show the vines, please take an extra shot before sending (the shooting angle is up to you—choose an angle that clearly shows the vine growth).
If there are no vines, select “No,” then press the “Send” button.
