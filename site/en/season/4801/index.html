Season Information: Tochigi Season

In this season, the target Poles are mainly selected from the previously unphotographed areas of the North Kanto Season.
To encourage the photographing of these unphotographed Poles, the Tochigi area is divided into smaller “Areas,” and players ranking high within each area will receive “Reward Coins.”
Aim for the No.1 position in each Area by completing all the unphotographed Poles!

 

[Season Duration and Playable Hours]

◼️Season Duration

October 11 (Sat), 2025, 6:00 AM – November 30 (Sun), 2025, 4:15 PM
 

◼️Game Playable Hours

● Photo Capture:

November – 6:45 to 16:15
 

● Check-in:
No restriction

[Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)]

Rewards are granted based on the results of the team-based ranking battle conducted throughout the season.
There are two ranking types: “Team Ranking” and “Individual Ranking (within team).”
Rewards will be distributed at the end of the season according to the final standings in both categories.

[Reward Coin Exchange]

Reward Coins can be exchanged for “Amazon Gift Cards” or “Cryptocurrency (DEP).”
The relationship between the exchange amount and required Reward Coins is as follows.
*Exchange is available from Menu → Profile → Exchange → Choose Destination in the main screen.

 

◼️Exchange for Amazon Gift Card

Required Reward Coins Exchange Value
1,200 100 yen

*Exchange is possible in units of 1,200 Coins.
*Up to 10,000 yen per request can be exchanged.

 

◼️Exchange for Cryptocurrency (DEP)

Required Reward Coins Exchange Value
5,000 2,983 DEP

*A handling fee of 500 Coins will be charged.
*Exchange is possible in units of 5,000 Coins.
