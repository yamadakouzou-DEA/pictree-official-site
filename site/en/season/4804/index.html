Season Information: Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama Season

This article provides details for the season “My Pole Battle in Nagano, Yamanashi & Saitama” and announces our collaboration with “Laid-Back Camp Δ SEASON 3.”

 

1. Season Details

[Season Duration and Playable Hours]

◼️ Season Duration

October 11 (Sat), 2025, 09:00 – November 30 (Sun), 2025, 16:30

 

◼️ Game Playable Hours

● Photo Capture:
October: 06:00–17:00
November: 06:45–16:30

● Check-in:
No restriction
* On the first day of the season, play starts at 09:00.

 

[Participation Format]

At the start of the season, select one of the three teams (“Nadeshiko & Ayano,” “Rin & Ena,” “Chiaki & Aoi”). Join your chosen team and compete for scores against the other teams.

2. Collaboration with “Laid-Back Camp Δ SEASON 3”

① Original Teams

Characters from “Laid-Back Camp Δ SEASON 3” are split into three teams—“Nadeshiko & Ayano,” “Rin & Ena,” and “Chiaki & Aoi.” Players choose a team and participate as its member.

 

② Season-Limited Skin

The in-game UI of PicTrée features a special design themed on “Laid-Back Camp Δ SEASON 3.”

 

③ Limited Items

Multiple limited items in collaboration with “Laid-Back Camp Δ SEASON 3” will appear.

 

④ Original Treasure Chests

When checking in a photo asset, special treasure chests themed on “Laid-Back Camp Δ SEASON 3” may appear with a certain probability. Season-limited collaboration items can be obtained from these chests.

 

⑤ Special Telephone Poles

“Special” character-colored Poles are placed at real-world locations across the region and appear together with the characters.
Special Telephone Poles differ from regular ones in the following ways:
・Asset HP: 5,000
・Connection Capacity in Pole Connect: Up to 5 connections
・Treasure Chest Drop Rate and Item Drop Rate

 

⑥ Special Presentation

A special cut-in appears with the characters when “Pole Connect” is performed.

 

⑦ Collaboration-Only Postcards

“Collaboration-Only Postcards” will be sold at select Animate stores in each prefecture (Ōmiya / Aeon Mall Kofu Shōwa / Nagano).
Price: 660 yen (incl. tax)
