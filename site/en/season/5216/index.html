Season Information: Maebashi Season 2025

For this season, in special collaboration with Bandai Namco Filmworks, we will collaborate with the anime “Maebashi Witches,” beloved by residents of Gunma and many others.

This article shares the season details and information about the collaboration with “Maebashi Witches.”

 

1. Season Details

【Season Period and Playable Hours】

◼️ Season Period

November 1 (Sat), 2025 9:00 JST – November 30 (Sun), 2025 16:00 JST

 

◼️ Game Playable Hours

・Take Photos: 9:00 JST ~ 16:00 JST

・Check-In: 9:00 JST ~ 16:00 JST

 

【Participation Format】

At the start of the season, select one of the three teams (“Yuina＆Keroppe”, “Azu＆Mai”, “Choco＆Kyoka”). You will belong to that team and compete against other teams on score.

 

【Photography Targets, Angles, and Shooting Rewards】

◼️ Photography Targets and Angles

● Photography Targets:

● Shooting Angles:

・Full View 1

・Full View 2

・Pole Number

・Potentially Fallen Tree: If there is a tree that seems about to fall toward wires or a pole, please photograph that tree.

※ If there is a tree about to fall, press “Take Photo” to shoot. If not, select “Does Not Take Photo.”

 

< Potentially Fallen Tree — Example >

 

● About Vine Reporting

Before sending images, you will choose whether vines are wrapped around the pole.

If you find vines during shooting, check the height of the vine tips on the pole and select the corresponding option.

If you select “Found,” you can take one additional photo. If you feel your current photos do not clearly show the vines, please take an additional shot before sending. The shooting angle is up to you, so choose any angle that clearly shows vine wrapping.

If there are no vines, select “Not Found” and press the “Send” button as is.

◼️ Shooting Rewards

Shooting rewards are the same for both regular poles and poles with vines.

Target First-Time Shooting Reward Rewards for Subsequent Shots
Pole, Pole (Vine-Prone) 240 Reward Coins (equivalent to 20 JPY) None

◼️ Vine Discovery Reward

This season, if you photograph a pole with vines and it is approved upon review, you will receive a “Vine Discovery Reward.”

Target Vine Discovery Reward
Pole, Pole (Vine-Prone) 1,200 Reward Coins (equivalent to 100 JPY)

※ If you find vine wrapping but select “Not Found” in the vine reporting before sending images, the Vine Discovery Reward will not be granted.

※ Do not select “Found” when there is no vine wrapping. Repeated false reports may lead to account suspension.

Note that the “Vine Discovery Reward” is granted after operational review, so it is not granted immediately after shooting like the shooting reward. It will be granted in bulk after the season ends.

2. “Maebashi Witches” Collaboration

① Original teams

Characters from “Maebashi Witches” are divided into three teams: “Yuina＆Keroppe,” “Azu＆Mai,” and “Choco＆Kyoka.” Players choose a team to join and participate in the game as part of that team.

 

② Season-limited skin

The in-game UI of PicTrée features a special design themed on “Maebashi Witches.”

 

③ Limited items

Multiple collaboration-limited items featuring “Maebashi Witches” will appear.

 

④ Original treasure chest

When you check in a shooting asset, there is a chance that a special treasure chest themed on “Maebashi Witches” will appear.

Collaboration-limited items can be obtained from these chests.

 

⑤ Special Poles

Somewhere in the target area, “Special Poles” colored in each character’s image color will appear together with the characters.

These Special Poles differ from regular poles in the following ways:

・Shooting asset HP: 5,000

・Number of connections for Pole Connect: up to 5

・Increased drop rate for treasure chests and items

 

⑥ Special cut-in animation

When you perform a Pole Connect, a special animation plays where characters appear as a cut-in.

 

⑦ Collaboration-limited postcards

You can exchange Reward Coins earned in the game for collaboration-limited postcards featuring “Maebashi Witches.”

To exchange for collaboration postcards, please visit Gallery Art Soup in Maebashi city.

For details on the exchange location and method, please see the following notice:

 

“Maebashi Witches” Collaboration: Exchanging Collaboration-Limited Postcards for “My Pole Battle in Maebashi City”

Details: https://pictree.greenwaygrid.global/en/news/5137/
