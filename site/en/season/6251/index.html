Season Info: Discover! Crow’s Nest Season in Chiba

【Season Period and Playable Hours】

◼️Season period

March 7, 2026 (Sat) 6:30 – April 10, 2026 (Fri) 17:30 (UTC+9)

◼️Playable hours:

・Photography: 6:30–17:30 (UTC+9)

・Check-in: No restrictions

【How to Participate】
At the start of the season, choose one of three teams (“Ampere,” “Volt,” or “Watt”). Join that team and compete against the other teams for the top score.

【Photo Targets and Rewards】
◼️Photo targets
・Poles

＜Required photo angles＞Two photos are required: “Wide shot” and “Pole number.”
・Wide shot: Take a photo so the entire pole fills the frame.
※Please keep a distance of about 5–10 meters from the pole.

・Pole number: Take a photo so the pole number fills the frame.
※If there are two plates, make sure both plates are visible in the photo.

◼️Photography rewards

There is no set reward per pole photo.

You can earn reward coins through the “Discovery Reward” and the “Treasure Chest Reward” described below.

◼️Discovery Reward

When you discover a pole with a crow’s nest and submit a report, you will receive the “Crow’s Nest Discovery Reward” (this will be checked by the operation team).

To report a discovery, tap the megaphone icon in the upper-right corner of the pole details screen.

●Crow’s Nest Discovery Reward

For each “crow’s nest” photo: 3,600 reward coins (equivalent to ¥300)

＜Example of a crow’s nest＞

●Treasure Chest Reward (Crow’s Nest Treasure Chest)

When you photograph a pole, a special treasure chest (Crow’s Nest Treasure Chest) may appear with a certain probability, allowing you to earn reward coins.

There are four types of Crow’s Nest Treasure Chests, and the amount of reward coins you can obtain varies by type.

◼️If Photography Is Difficult: Please Report

If there is any issue with the photo target, or if the surrounding environment or the condition of the target makes it difficult to photograph, please do not force a photo. Instead, use the reporting feature to submit a report.

Please see below for how to report.

＜How to report＞

① Tap the target asset icon.

② Tap the megaphone icon in the upper-right.

③ Check the issue(s) that apply and tap “Next.”

④ If you have any helpful photos, take a photo using the “Take photo” button, then tap “Submit.”

In addition, the season is divided into areas, and area ranking rewards will be granted after the season ends.

◼️Area Ranking Rewards

These rewards are granted based on the results of the area ranking battles set for each season.

Regardless of the team’s overall placement, rewards are granted according to your “individual rank within your team.”

【Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)】

These rewards are granted based on the results of the team-versus-team ranking battle held over the entire season.

This ranking includes both a “team ranking” and an “individual ranking within the team.”

The rewards you can earn at the end of the season are determined based on the final results of the team ranking and individual ranking.

◼️Coefficient used when calculating team points

The team ranking is determined by each team’s team points.

Team points are calculated as the sum of the total accumulated rank points of all players on the team.
