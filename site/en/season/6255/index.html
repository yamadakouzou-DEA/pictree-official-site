Season Info: Discover! Crow Nest Season in Sano City (Tochigi), Tatebayashi City (Gunma), and Kazo City (Saitama)

【Season Period and Playable Hours】

◼️ Season Period

March 7, 2026 (Sat) 6:30 (UTC+9) – May 31, 2026 (Sun) 17:30 (UTC+9)

◼️ Playable Hours

・Photo-taking: 6:30 (UTC+9) – 17:30 (UTC+9)

・Check-in: No restrictions

【How to Participate】

At the start of the season, choose one of three teams (“Ampere,” “Volt,” or “Watt”). Join that team and compete against the other teams for score.

【Photo Targets and Rewards】

◼️ Photo Targets

・Poles

<Photo Framing> Two photos are required: “Full View” and “Pole Number.”

・Full View: Take the photo so the entire pole fills the camera frame.

※Keep a distance of about 5–10 m from the pole.

・Pole Number: Take the photo so the pole number fills the camera frame.

※If there are two tags, make sure both tags are visible in the photo.

◼️ Photo Rewards

There is no per-pole photo reward.

You can earn reward coins through the “Discovery Reward” and “Treasure Box Reward” described below.

◼️ Discovery Reward

If you discover a pole with a crow nest and submit a report, you will receive a “Crow Nest Discovery Reward” (your report will be checked by the operation team).

To report a crow nest discovery, tap the megaphone icon in the top-right of the pole details screen.

● Crow Nest Discovery Reward

For each “Crow Nest” you photograph: 3,600 reward coins (equivalent to ¥300)

<Examples of crow nests>

● Treasure Box Reward (Crow Nest Treasure Box)

When you take a photo of a pole, a special treasure box (Crow Nest Treasure Box) may appear with a certain probability, allowing you to earn reward coins.

There are four types of Crow Nest Treasure Boxes, and the amount of reward coins you can earn differs by type.

◼️ Request for Reports When Taking Photos Is Difficult

If there is some issue with the photo target, or if the surrounding environment or the condition of the target makes it difficult to take a photo, please do not force yourself to take a photo. Use the report function to submit a report.

Please refer to the steps below.

<How to Report>

① Tap the target asset icon

② Tap the megaphone icon in the top-right

③ Check the issue(s) occurring with the target and tap “Next”

④ If you have a helpful photo, tap “Take Photo,” then tap “Send”

【Event Area and Area Ranking Rewards】

This season will be held in Sano City (Tochigi Prefecture), Tatebayashi City (Gunma Prefecture), and Kazo City (Saitama Prefecture).

Each season is divided into areas, and area ranking rewards will be granted after the season ends.

◼️ Event Area

・Tochigi Prefecture: Sano City

・Gunma Prefecture: Tatebayashi City

・Saitama Prefecture: Kazo City

◼️ Area Ranking Rewards

Rewards granted based on the results of each area’s ranking battle set for the season.

Regardless of team placement, rewards are granted based on “Individual Ranking Within the Team.”

【Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)】

These rewards are granted based on the results of the team-vs-team ranking battle held on a season basis.

This ranking includes “Team Ranking” and “Individual Ranking Within the Team.” The rewards you can earn at the end of the season are determined based on the final team and individual rankings at the season end.

◼️ Coefficients Used When Calculating Team Points

Team ranking is determined by each team’s team points. Team points are calculated as the sum of the cumulative rank points of each player belonging to that team.
