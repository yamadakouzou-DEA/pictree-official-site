Season info: Regular Season 1

1. What is the “Grid Grab: Capture the Current Regular Season”?

The Regular Season is a new permanent season in PicTrée. It features the following characteristics.

[About Regular Season Periods]

In the Regular Season, which runs continuously, rankings are tallied at regular intervals.

At the end of each season, rewards will be distributed based on your final ranking.

After each season ends, the capture status will be reset and a new season will begin.

〈Season Schedule〉

・Regular Season 1: Saturday, June 6, 2026 – Thursday, July 30, 2026

・Regular Season 2: Saturday, August 1, 2026 – Tuesday, September 29, 2026

・Regular Season 3: Thursday, October 1, 2026 – Sunday, November 29, 2026

・Regular Season 4: Tuesday, December 1, 2026 – Sunday, January 31, 2027

・Regular Season 5: Tuesday, February 2, 2027 – Tuesday, March 30, 2027

*Details for April 2027 and beyond will be announced at a later date.

Between seasons, there will be a “Season Switchover Day.”

Maintenance will be performed on the switchover day, so there will be days when the Regular Season cannot be played.

〈Maintenance Schedule〉

・Friday, July 31, 2026: 15:00–24:00 (approx.)

・Wednesday, September 30, 2026: Time TBD

・Monday, November 30, 2026: Time TBD

・Monday, February 1, 2027: Time TBD

・Wednesday, March 31, 2027: Time TBD

[About the Target Areas]

The Regular Season will start with coverage of the Eastern Japan area.

Going forward, we plan to add infrastructure from various operators and gradually expand the target areas and scale.

[About Shooting Rewards]

When you photograph a shooting target that displays “Get XX Coins!” on the asset information screen (pole information, etc.), you will earn a shooting reward.

Once a reward has been claimed for a shooting target, no further shooting rewards can be obtained from that target.

The capture status of shooting targets resets each season, but your history of earned shooting rewards carries over across seasons.

Even when the season changes, previously earned shooting rewards will not be restored.

[About the Shooting Reward Boost Campaign]

We plan to hold limited-time, limited-area campaigns where shooting rewards will be increased (details will be announced at a later date).

▼ Nameplate

You will be presented with a choice of whether to photograph the nameplate or not.

If you can photograph it, tap “Photograph” and take the shot. If you cannot photograph it, select the closest reason from the options provided, then take a photo showing why it cannot be photographed.

[Request to Report Difficult Shooting Situations]

If there is any issue with a shooting target, or if environmental conditions or the state of the target makes it difficult to photograph, please do not force the shot and instead use the report function.

Please refer to the following for how to submit a report.

◼️ How to Report

① Tap the icon for the target asset

② Tap the megaphone icon in the upper right

③ Check the issue that has occurred with the shooting target and tap “Next”

④ If you have a helpful photo, tap the “Photograph” button to take a photo, then tap “Send”
