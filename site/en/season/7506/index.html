[Participation Format]

[Photo Targets & Angles]

◼️ Photo Targets

· Utility Pole

· Utility Pole (Vine & Ivy)

In this season, some utility poles have been designated as “Utility Pole (Vine & Ivy)” — poles that are particularly prone to vine and ivy growth. These poles have a different icon in the asset display and carry special in-game effects.

※ Photo rewards are the same as for standard utility poles.

<Utility Pole (Vine & Ivy) Features>

· Utility Pole Connect connections: up to 5

· Utility Pole HP: 5,000

◼️ Photo Angles

Three photos are required: “Wide Shot 1,” “Wide Shot 2,” and “Pole Number.”

· Wide Shot 1 & 2: Fill the camera frame with the entire utility pole. Wide Shot 2 must be taken from a different angle than Wide Shot 1.

Keep a distance of approximately 5–10 meters from the pole.

※ Stand approximately 5–10 meters away from the pole.

· Pole Number: Fill the camera frame with the pole number plate.

※ If there are two plates, make sure both are visible in the shot.

[How to Report Vine & Ivy]

In this season, you will be asked to select whether vine or ivy growth is present before submitting your photos.

If you spot vine or ivy while on-site, check the height of the vine or ivy tip relative to the pole and select the appropriate option.

Selecting “Present” allows you to take one additional photo. If the existing photos make it hard to clearly show the vine or ivy, take the additional shot from an angle where the growth is most visible before submitting.

If there is no vine or ivy, select “None” and press “Submit.”

<Example: Utility Pole with Vine & Ivy Growth>

◼️ Vine & Ivy Reporting Notes

Please note that the following cases may not be eligible for discovery rewards:

· Fully dried-out vines or ivy (no removal needed; no expected impact on equipment)

· Non-vining plants such as trees, shrubs, or herbaceous plants

· Cases where it is unclear whether the plant is vine or ivy

· Very small amounts that are not entangled with equipment and are unlikely to cause future impact

· Vine or ivy only near the base of the pole, not yet reaching or contacting the equipment itself

· Cases involving trespassing on private property or other safety, courtesy, or legal violations during photography

· Other cases deemed ineligible for rewards by the operations team

Please note that we are unable to respond to individual inquiries regarding eligibility for Vine & Ivy Discovery Rewards.

◼️ Reporting Difficult-to-Photograph Situations

If there is a problem with the photo target, or if environmental or physical conditions make it difficult to photograph, please use the Report function instead of forcing a photo.

Please refer to the steps below for how to submit a report.

<How to Report>

① Tap the asset icon of the target pole

② Tap the megaphone icon in the upper right

③ Check the applicable issue and tap “Next”

④ If you have a helpful photo, tap “Take Photo” to photograph it, then tap “Submit”

[Photo Rewards & Discovery Rewards]

Photo rewards and discovery rewards are the same for both standard utility poles and Utility Poles (Vine & Ivy).

◼️ Photo Rewards

◼️ Discovery Rewards

● Vine & Ivy Discovery Reward

If you photograph a utility pole that actually has vine or ivy growth and your submission is approved by the operations team, you will receive a “Vine & Ivy Discovery Reward” in addition to the standard reward.

※ Not all Utility Poles (Vine & Ivy) necessarily have vine or ivy growth.

※ If you find vine or ivy growth but select “None” in the Vine & Ivy report before submitting your photos, the Vine & Ivy Discovery Reward will not be granted.

※ Please do not select “Present” in the Vine & Ivy report if there is no growth. Repeated false reports may result in account suspension.

Note: The “Vine & Ivy Discovery Reward” is granted after a review by the operations team and is not awarded immediately after photo submission like standard photo rewards. Please allow approximately 1–2 weeks for the reward to be granted.

◼️ Area Ranking Rewards

These rewards are distributed based on the results of the area-specific ranking competition held each season.

Rewards are granted based on your “individual rank within your team,” regardless of your team’s overall ranking.

[Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)]

These rewards are distributed based on the results of the team-vs-team ranking competition held each season.

This ranking includes both a “Team Ranking” and an “Individual Team Ranking.” Rewards are determined at the end of the season based on your team ranking and your personal ranking at the time the season concludes.
