Season2026-10-30

Season info: Disaster Preparedness Championship: Nationwide Evacuation Drill Season 3

[Season Period and Playable Hours]

◼️Season Period

Sunday, November 1, 2026, 6:00 – Monday, November 30, 2026, 19:00 (JST / UTC+9)

◼️Playable Hours

・Check-in: 6:00–19:00 (JST / UTC+9)

*In the “Evacuation Drill Season,” targets are not photographed.

*You can check in at the same spot as many times as you like, once every 30 minutes.

[How to Participate]

Open “Switch Season” from the upper left of the PicTrée top screen, then select “Disaster Preparedness Championship: Nationwide Evacuation Drill Season 3” from the list to join.

Choose one of three teams (“Ampere,” “Volt,” or “Watt”) and compete on score against the other teams as a member of that team.

[Check-in Targets]

[Request for Reports]

If a target has any kind of problem — it does not exist, it is a different object, or its position is significantly off from the map — please let us know through the report function.

See below for how to report.

◼️How to Report

① Tap the icon of the target asset

② Tap the megaphone mark in the upper right

③ Check the problems occurring with the photography target and tap “Next”

④ If you have a helpful photo, take it with the “Take Photo” button and tap “Send”

[Participating Areas]

Among Japan’s 47 prefectures, areas where photos were taken and posted in the “Discovery Event” between Saturday, August 1 and Saturday, October 31, 2026

[Treasure Chest Rewards]

When you check in, the following two types of “treasure chests” appear at random.

◼️Regular Treasure Chests (Bronze / Silver / Gold)

You can obtain “Reward Coins,” which can be exchanged for Amazon Gift Cards and more, as well as in-game items.

Gold treasure chests even offer a chance at a grand prize of 120,000 Reward Coins (worth 10,000 yen)!

◼️”BitTrade Prize” Treasure Chests

“BitTrade Prize” treasure chests may also appear.

If you obtain one, you will receive DEP crypto assets that can be received in an account at BitTrade, the crypto asset exchange and official sponsor of the PicTrée Disaster Preparedness Championship 2026.

<Rewards>

・BitTrade Prize, Large: DEP worth 10,000 yen

・BitTrade Prize, Medium: DEP worth 2,000 yen

・BitTrade Prize, Small: DEP worth 500 yen

<How to Receive>

When you obtain a treasure chest, you will receive a “DEP Exchange Ticket” as an item. Using the item displays a link that explains how to receive your reward, so please follow the instructions to complete the BitTrade account receipt procedure (your PicTrée player ID is required).

[Ranking Rewards]

There are two types of ranking rewards: “Area Ranking Rewards” and “Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards).”

◼️Area Ranking Rewards

Rewards granted based on the results of each area’s ranking battle in each season.

Rewards are granted according to your “individual rank within your team.”

●Areas Eligible for Ranking

Among Japan’s 47 prefectures, areas where photos were taken and posted in the “Discovery Event” between Saturday, August 1 and Saturday, October 31, 2026

◼️Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)

Rewards granted based on the results of the team-versus-team ranking battle held over the season.

Rewards are granted according to your “individual rank within your team.”

[Exchanging Reward Coins]

Reward Coins can be exchanged for “Amazon Gift Cards,” “Erabel Pay®,” and “giftee Box®.”

The relationship between the exchange value and the number of Reward Coins required is as follows.

*Exchanges can be made from the menu in the upper right of the main screen > Profile > Exchange > Select exchange destination.

◼️Exchanging for Amazon Gift Cards

*Exchanges are possible in units of 1,200 coins.

*Up to 10,000 yen can be exchanged per application.

◼️Exchanging for “Erabel Pay®” and “giftee Box®”

*Exchanges are possible in units of 1,350 coins.

*Up to 10,000 points can be exchanged per application.
