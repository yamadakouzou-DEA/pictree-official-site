Season info: Okinawa-wide Utility Pole Photo Hunt 2026-2027

Season Period and Playable Hours]

◼️Season Period

Saturday, December 12, 2026 – Sunday, March 14, 2027

◼️Playable Hours

●Photo shooting: 7:00–18:30 (JST / UTC+9)

●Check-in: No time limit

*Even within the playable hours, photos may be rejected during review if the image cannot be clearly confirmed due to darkness.

[How to Participate]

Open “Switch Season” from the top left of the PicTrée TOP screen, and select “Okinawa 26-27” from the list to participate.

At the start of the season, choose one of the three teams (“Manta Riders,” “Tiida Force,” or “Gusuku Warriors”), and compete against the other teams as a member of that team.

[Area]

◼️Area

All of Okinawa Prefecture, including the outlying islands (target utility poles: approx. 240,000)

[Photo Targets]

・Utility poles

・Telecom poles (concrete)

・Telecom poles (steel pipe)

・Shared poles

[Rewards]

・Shooting Rewards: 360 Reward Coins (¥30 equivalent) to 600 Reward Coins (¥50 equivalent) per pole

・Season Ranking Rewards (earned by placing high in the team ranking)

Further details will be announced at a later date.
