シーズン2026年1月20日

Season Information: Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba Season

【Season Period and Playable Hours】

◼️Season Period

January 31, 2026 (Saturday) 7:00 JST ~ March 8, 2026 (Sunday) 17:00 JST

◼️Gameplay Hours

・Photo capturing:

January 31 (Sat) ~ February 13 (Fri) 7:00~16:45 JST

February 14 (Sat) ~ March 8 (Sun) 6:45~17:00 JST

※Please note that the photo capturing hours will change during the season.

・Check-in: No restrictions

【Participation Format】

At the start of the season, select one of three teams (“Ampere,” “Volt,” or “Watt”) and compete for scores with other teams.

【Photo Targets, Angles, and Rewards】

◼️Photo Targets and Angles

This season, manholes and handholes will be added as photo targets in addition to telephone poles.

Manholes and handholes will be added as photo targets from mid-February and will be available for capture.

● Photo Targets:

・Telephone poles (Concrete poles / Steel poles / Joint-use poles) _Urban / Rural

・Manholes_Urban / Rural　（※Scheduled to be added in mid-February）

・Handholes_Urban / Rural　（※Scheduled to be added in mid-February）

<Urban / Rural>

In this season, each photo target asset has different rewards for urban and rural areas.

Rural photo targets have different asset icons and images from normal ones, and higher rewards (equivalent to 100 yen per pole) are given.

▼Asset Icons

Rural asset icons have an orange mark in the upper right corner.

● Photo Angles:

<Concrete Telephone Pole / Joint-Use Pole>

・Overview ①②

<Concrete Telephone Pole (with support pole) / Joint-Use Pole (with support pole)>

・Overview ①②

・Number tag

・Nameplate (main pole) ※1

・Foothold bolt socket (support pole) ※1

・Nameplate (support pole) ※1

※1）Foothold bolt socket and nameplate photography are optional.

▼Foothold Bolt Socket

You can select whether or not there is a foothold bolt socket.

If there is one, press “Capture” to photograph it. If there is none or it is in a position where it cannot be photographed, select “None or in a position where it cannot be photographed” and take a photo showing that there is none.

▼Nameplate

You can select whether or not the nameplate can be photographed.

If it can be photographed, press “Capture” to photograph it. If it cannot be photographed, select the closest reason from the options and take a photo showing that it cannot be photographed.

<Manhole・Handhole>

・Close-up view

・Distant view

Manholes and handholes have an NTT mark or a Nippon Telegraph and Telephone Public Corporation mark.

・Close-up view: Photograph from about a 45-degree angle, not directly above, so that the photo target and the surrounding 30cm are visible.

・Distant view: Photograph from a position about 4m away so that the photo target and the surrounding situation are visible.

For close-up views, you can select whether or not it can be photographed. If it is on a roadway or in a difficult location to photograph, please refrain from forcing a photo.

If it can be photographed, press “Can be photographed” while paying close attention to surrounding safety. If it cannot be photographed, select “Cannot be photographed because it is in a dangerous location such as a roadway” and take a photo showing that it cannot be photographed.

【Request for Reporting When Photography is Difficult】

If there is a problem with the photo target, or if it is difficult to photograph due to the surrounding environment or the condition of the photo target, please report it using the reporting function instead of forcing a photo.

Please refer to the following for reporting methods.

◼️Reporting Method

①Tap the target asset icon

②Tap the megaphone icon in the upper right

③Check the problems occurring with the photo target and tap “Next”

④If you have a useful photo, tap the “Capture” button to photograph it, then tap “Send”

◼️Photo Rewards

※There are no rewards for photography from the second time onwards.

【Hosting Areas and Area-Specific Ranking Rewards】

This season’s hosting areas are Tokyo, Kanagawa, and Chiba. In addition, the areas are divided into 6 in Tokyo, 7 in Kanagawa, and 6 in Chiba (19 areas in total), and area-specific ranking rewards are awarded.

◼️Hosting Areas (19 Areas)

Tokyo: Western 23 Wards, Eastern 23 Wards, Okutama・Hinode・Hinohara, Western Tama, Southern Tama, Northern Tama

Kanagawa: Yokosuka・Miura, Yokohama, Central, Western, Odawara, Shonan, Kawasaki

Chiba: Tokatsu, Kujukuri, Central Boso, Southern Boso, Hokuso, Bay Area

◼️Area-Specific Ranking Rewards

Rewards are given based on the results of the ranking battle for each area set for each season.

Rewards are given according to “Individual Ranking within Team” regardless of team ranking.

【Season Ranking Rewards (Overall Rewards)】

Rewards are given based on the results of team-based ranking battles held per season.

This ranking includes “Team Ranking” and “Individual Ranking within Team,” and the rewards that can be earned at the end of the season are determined based on the team ranking and individual ranking at the end of the season.

(Unit: Reward Coins)
